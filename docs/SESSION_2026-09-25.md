## 🟡 For the designer (rulings, not bugs)

1. A won **hire** delivers the recruit to the tavern, where hiring costs gold again.
2. **Recurring cast snowballs**: reuse is weighted by edge count (your ruling), so the most-used
   face keeps winning — Vestord was the antagonist in 3 of 4 sagas in one campaign.
3. The unpasteable way to make a scouting report name its lead: the report says what it heard
   and the lead takes that archetype — a category choice handed to the writer.
4. **Injury weight**: a "low" wound (days) is −0.2U per tier; at L3 two tiers = −4.4 coins, which
   zeroes a soldier for ~4 cycles.
6. **Returning faces (§5)**: the memory never reaches the page; the shape that would work is
   dealing the one concrete shared memory at the beat where the face enters (RECURRING_CAST §10b).
5. Early soft-lock risk: after the day-0 packet and 4 drips, leads come only from the Scouting
   lodge (120g); a player who spends on comfort rooms first can end up with no leads and no gold.
