# Playtest session notes — 2026-06-11 (overnight hardening before user playtest)

**What this is:** findings from ~12 real-AI text-UI campaigns + deterministic audits, run to iron
out issues before tonight's hands-on playtest. Everything marked FIXED is committed; everything
marked FLAG is a design question deliberately left for the designer.

**Final validation (round 5, all fixes live, 18 cycles across 2 campaigns):** 17 S / 7 P / 2 F,
0 debts, mock-fallbacks 8→1 (retry/backoff), and all three finale shapes fired and read well —
recruit ("Haldra joins, +132g"), ransom ("Ulric handed off — 250g"), personal ("Marek settles
his past, famous, +134g"). Full gates green (selftest 830 · conformance 1778 · looptest · build).

## 1. What was broken and is now FIXED (highlights)

### Engine / economy
- **Reward generation rewritten per ECONOMY §4** (was: roll-based gen ignored the value budget).
  Units now SPEND their target value: focal worth tracks `maxCharValue` (was flat ~25 at every
  level, min 6); every bundle nets ~V (raid was silently leaking 15% of V); jackpot-with-catch
  revived (was dead code); group caps stop trait-soup (≤2 personality, ≤2 physical).
- **Odds curve retuned** (`thresholdPerMerc 3+0.5L → 2.5+0.4L`): a matched decent-fit merc was
  S19%/F50% (2-slot S17%) → fail-spam, debt piles, dead sagas, lost starter mercs. Now decent ≈
  S50/P31/F19. Observed across campaigns: ~58% S / 28% P / 14% F.
- **Malus risky-gate restored** (ECONOMY §5): only risky quests can land a failure debt (a
  dropped gate had every failure stacking −15g).
- **Promise = grant**: a one-off's offered kind is enforced to the rolled reward kind; middle
  chain beats can no longer offer recruit/captive (they never deliver units); a named quarry on
  a card must fit the pre-rolled unit's identity (no more "Eira" cards delivering a male wolfman).
- **API resilience**: a transient 429/5xx used to throw past the retry loop straight to the mock
  fallback (bland cards, even leaked engine instructions — the mock interpolated the raw beat
  instruction; both fixed: retries with backoff + a fiction-safe mock line).

### Story generation
- Arc steps must each be a complete quest-worthy mission (opener included) — "meet & accept" and
  "get directions" are how a job ARRIVES, not a step. After a failed beat, the next step ADAPTS
  to the fallout (no more "escort the token" when the token was never recovered).
- Name hygiene: focal seed names filtered against the world (two same-cycle "Anika" sagas);
  new characters can't echo roster names ("Aldric Voss" vs our "Aldric the Patient"); flesh
  avoids roster/recent names and gets engine name-seeds.
- Company mercs in bibles: marked COMPANY MERCENARY; never the client/payer/quarry (both the
  poolCast path and the personal-chain path had our own mercs paying the company).
- Byte-stable system prompts (STORY_ENGINE §10.2): outcome + genesis no longer interpolate
  per-call data into the cached prefix.
- Prose↔mechanics alignment: non-risky failures don't write demanded debts into prose; loot is
  THINGS never people; CLI no longer prints the beat's planned reveal ("whisper" spoiler);
  no time-of-day as arrival dressing (a tide-window deadline inside the fiction is fine).
- Dead `closesChain` field dropped from the chainBeat contract (the engine decides finales).

## 2. FLAGS — design questions for the designer (deliberately not changed)
1. **Personal-saga finale failure permanently removes the merc** (the focal IS the merc; finale
   failure → "lost"). Dramatic, but a starter merc can die to one bad roll chain. Soften (scar +
   grief instead of removal)? Keep (stakes are the point)?
2. **Debts never bite**: liabilities sit inert until voluntarily cleared (no interest/collector).
   ECONOMY lists clearing them as a sink, but nothing pressures the player to.
3. **Doc drift** (docs say / impl does): STORY_ENGINE §3 casting tiers (common=no AI;
   rare=known faces) vs impl (every one-off AI-written; recurrence rolled ~45% any rarity);
   §8 organic length vs REWARD_BANK fixed arcs (REWARD_BANK is newer and yours);
   ECONOMY §3/§7 leads-as-rewards not implemented (lead value pays as gold).
4. **coreKind heuristic**: recruit-vs-captive for a saga is an engine roll (twist→captive, else
   40/60); could instead let the story propose it.
5. **Chain expected length is invisible** to the player (beat N shown, not "of ~5") — matters
   for the bank gamble. Add to UI?
6. **flesh "who" occasionally still reads as a tag recital** despite the anti-example (1 in ~3).
   Engine-side retry on recital-detection possible if it grates.

## 3. What to expect tonight (player-facing state)
- Cards: one boss-POV intro + an explicit `Offered:` line (kind enforced by the engine), dialogue
  in nearly every intro, varied arrivals/themes; chains escalate with adapted fallout.
- Resolutions: buildup → roll → consequence with per-merc beats and clear outcomes; finales pay
  the bank (focal + surplus gold; debt/void paths live).
- Economy: starting roster can win matched decent-fit jobs ~50% + partials; debts only from
  risky failures; ransom = 0.6×value; recruits/captives have budget-priced tags worth their V.
- Text UI (`npm run cli` / `-- auto N`) and GUI read the same engine state; CLI shows odds after
  assignment ("coins N vs threshold M").

## 3a. HANDS-PLAYED campaign (9 cycles, interactive CLI, decisions made as a player)

Played seed `playerA` move-by-move chasing attachment. The journal, condensed:
- Took Marek's personal saga first (the ★ lead pulls exactly as intended). Built a Tavern with
  the early purse specifically to recruit captives — the build had a *motive*.
- A hunt delivered captive **Joren Hale** — and the resolution recast him ("I hid him": he'd
  been protecting the child). I recruited him instead of ransoming (60g passed up) purely from
  liking him. Two cycles later **his own ★ saga appeared** — the compounding loop fires, and it
  instantly became my favorite thing on the board.
- Joren's beats kept expressing HIM: he *sang* boarding a flatboat, sang the fevered child calm,
  was favored by his own Iron(tough) tag when I'd misjudged him as frail. Attachment: confirmed.
- **Marek's finale failed at 78%** — and he was simply GONE: prose left him alive and kneeling,
  the roster shrank silently, 192 banked forfeited without a word. Stakes felt real (memorable!)
  but the delivery was broken → FIXED (loud ✝ lines, departure named, forfeit shown). The
  design question (should a personal finale failure remove the merc at all?) stays FLAGGED.
- Joren's finale: PARTIAL — the river took Merrow AND the child; Joren ends *infamous*, 297g
  crystallizes, the child unresolved (sequel seed). A genuinely affecting ending.

What the played session surfaced that auto-runs never did:
1. **Lead indices shift after each pursue** — I grabbed a raid meaning to grab an escort → FIXED
   (board reprints after pursue).
2. **Soft beat**: "hold the plate at the fort and receive the offer" — an appraisal errand as a
   full quest. The genesis mission-principle needs a "no fort-side steps" sharpening (FLAG).
3. **Possession state is invisible to the writers**: the plate was stolen at beat 5, and the
   finale STILL asked us to "escort the plate" (knew the loss in flavor, contradicted it in the
   job). Needs a facts-ledger (who holds what) fed to chainBeat — top prototype gap (FLAG).
4. **No way to back out of a pursued quest** — pursue commits you sight-unseen (FLAG: abandon
   with a small cost?).
5. Sigrun (healer) idles when nobody is hurt — healers lack pull between injuries (FLAG).
6. Prose wounds (Ivo "takes a blade across the ribs" on a partial) don't map to injury cards —
   fiction/mechanics gap, mild (FLAG).
7. Label truncation mid-word ("both partie") → FIXED (word-boundary cut).

**Fun verdict from actually playing:** the loop works. Odds-reading before commit is a real
decision; failures sting narratively without rage-quitting (debt-gating right); the attachment
thesis (recruit → their saga → caring about their rolls) demonstrably fired on me within ~5
cycles. The two things that most threaten the fun: coherence wobbles around lost/held objects
(facts-ledger), and how merc loss is delivered (now louder, but the design question remains).

## 4. Known cosmetic nits (not blocking)
- Coin labels still default to "a heavy purse of gold" in ~1/4 one-offs.
- "Soaked/staggers" arrival texture recurs (fen-appropriate, but samey across many cards).
- chainBeat's situation-field instruction is ~200 words and could compress ~30% (works well;
  not restructured the night before a playtest).
