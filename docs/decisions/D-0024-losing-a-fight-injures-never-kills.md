## Context

The E-0003 party sim showed whole-party wipes under bad tactics, and Claude asked for a party retreat rule.

## Decision

The designer's words: "loss != kill, just injured."

- A merc who drops in a fight is **injured** (out of action for a while), not dead.
- Losing a whole fight injures the party; nobody dies from it.

## Consequences

- A wipe is a setback (lost quest, recovery time), not the end of a merc the player built.
- Open: how long injuries last, whether they leave lasting marks (scars, quirks), and whether anything else can kill.
