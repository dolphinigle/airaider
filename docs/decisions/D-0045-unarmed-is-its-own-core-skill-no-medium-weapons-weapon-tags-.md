## Context

The designer asked whether unarmed fighting should be its own skill, whether weapons should split into heavy, medium and light, and whether bows and crossbows should split. Claude's test: a weapon gets its own core skill only when it fights differently (ranks, statuses, role). When only the flavor differs, the AI's naming covers it. Each new core costs about 5 base abilities (D-0033).

## Decision

The designer answered "agree" to Claude's recommendation:
- **Unarmed is its own core skill:** stuns, grapples, counters, flurries of small hits, buffing yourself. Light Weapons keeps bleeds, precise hits and fast strikes.
- **There is no medium weapons tier.** Heavy covers two-handers; Light covers one-handers and daggers.
- **Ranged stays one skill, with weapon tags.**
  - The AI maps a merc's exact skill to a core **plus a weapon** (e.g. Ranged · crossbow).
  - Each base ability lists the weapons it fits ("Volley": bow; "Heavy Bolt": crossbow; "Aimed Shot": any).
  - A merc only rolls bases that fit their weapon.
- **The same weapon tags apply to Heavy and Light** (e.g. axe, hammer, polearm).

## Consequences

- A weapon skill needs enough bases that each of its weapons still has about 5 that fit, about 7–8 for Ranged.
