## Context

D-0028 had a merc roll a battle skill every level up, which E-0004 showed repeating the same base (Strike four times in one career). Battle skills are now called abilities (D-0031).

## Decision

The designer: "for ABILITIES ... that you acquire when you level up, you cannot learn the same (base ability) twice. The ABILITY you can get to learn depends on what SKILLS the unit has. For exmaple, say we have Brann who has Holy Magic, Alchemy, then he can learn base skills offered by those two."

Restated in full (this replaces D-0028):
- **Every level up rolls one new ability**, with modifiers by star (D-0027).
- It is drawn from the **base abilities of the merc's skills** that the merc doesn't know yet: **never the same base twice**.
- A merc **equips four** abilities.

## Consequences

- A merc's skills must offer enough bases for a full career. With one ability per level, about 13 by Lv 12; at about 5 bases per skill, two skills cover Lv 1–6 and a third skill at Lv 7 (D-0035, proposed) refills the pool just as it runs dry.
- The base-ability list grows to about 5 per battle-capable skill (about 115 for 23 skills).
