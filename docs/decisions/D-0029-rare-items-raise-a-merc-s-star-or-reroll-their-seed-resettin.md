## Context

The designer wants mercs the player loves to grow with them (noted with D-0025). Rarity now exists (D-0027).

## Decision

The designer's words: "VERY RARELY or either LIMITED NUMBER OF TIMES PER GAMES (probably both), an item can INCREASE star but resets their level / rng seeds, so units you really like can get upgraded eventually." And: "There should be item like "Chaos orb" in path of exile that can reroll a unit rng seed -- probably reset their level too or something."

- **Star-up item:** raises a merc's star. Very rare, and probably also limited per game. It resets the merc's level and rerolls their seed.
- **Reroll item** (like Path of Exile's Chaos Orb): rerolls the merc's seed; it probably resets their level too.
- A merc's **seed** decides their random rolls (stat growth, battle skills, modifiers).

## Consequences

- Open: how big a star-up step is (½★ or 1★); what survives a reset (name, race, personality, skills, starting stats, equipped gear); both items' names.
