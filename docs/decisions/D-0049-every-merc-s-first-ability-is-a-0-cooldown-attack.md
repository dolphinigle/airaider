## Context

Civilian mercs may have no fighting skill (D-0046), and with cooldowns a merc can have nothing ready on a turn (D-0041).

## Decision

The designer's words: "every merc gets a 0 cooldown 'attack' skill as their first skill, always".

- Every merc's **first ability is an attack with no cooldown**.
- Every merc has one, civilians included.

## Open (Claude's proposal, not yet ruled)

- **Equipping:** the attack sits in a fixed fifth place, the last fallback after S1–S4, so the four equipped slots stay free.
- **Its coins:** the merc's weapon skill plus its stat. A merc with no weapon skill flips their best of STR or DEX alone ("throw a rock").
- **Its ranks:** these follow the weapon. A melee attack is used from ranks 1–2 and hits ranks 1–2. A ranged or thrown attack can be used from any rank.
- **Naming and modifiers:** the AI renames it like any ability, and it rolls modifiers by star.
