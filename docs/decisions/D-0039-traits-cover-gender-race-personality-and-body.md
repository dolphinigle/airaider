## Context

Passives come from traits (D-0036), race included (D-0038).

## Decision

The designer: "traits cover gender, race, personality, some body stuff too like \"tall\" \"short\" \"muscular\" \"skinny\""

A merc's traits come in four kinds, each able to carry a passive:
- **Gender**
- **Race** (Wolfkin: +10% damage, −10% speed)
- **Personality** (Stubborn, Gruff, Reckless)
- **Body**: tall, short, muscular, skinny…

## Consequences

- All traits are part of the starting position (D-0032): rolled at recruitment, kept through resets.
- Open: whether every trait is a trade-off like the race example, how many traits a merc shows, and how gender traits differ mechanically.
