## Context

D-0022 added PER and folded willpower into CON. Once combat followed Darkest Dungeon (D-0041) with stress (D-0042), the designer found DEX and PER too alike and wanted a stat for the stress check.

## Decision

The designer's words: "DEX and PER are too similar i think, just combine them. PER is DEX or add perception as skill instead. Make WIL instead that is for stress checks."

- **Six stats:**
  - **STR** strength;
  - **DEX** agility, hands, reflexes, aim;
  - **INT** intelligence;
  - **CHA** charisma;
  - **CON** endurance and toughness;
  - **WIL** willpower: nerve, fear, curses, focus.
- **PER is gone.** Aim goes to DEX, and noticing goes to the **Awareness** skill, which already covers spotting, keeping watch, searching and reading people. This is Claude's reading of "add perception as skill".
- **WIL** is the stat the **stress check** flips (D-0042).
- Skills still count in coins, unchanged.

## Consequences

- Skill stat pairings that used PER, or CON's willpower, are remapped in design/mercs.md. That table is the working copy.
- In a fight, DEX gives speed coins, CON gives max HP and WIL makes the stress check.
