## Context

The working model had a fixed kit of battle skills unlocking into slots S1–S4 as the merc levels (E-0003).

## Decision

The designer's words: "Units roll one new skill per level up, and they can EQUIP four skills. ... the skills like survival determine the battle skills offered to them when they level up."

- **Every level up rolls one new battle skill** for the merc, with modifiers by star (D-0027).
- **Which battle skills can come up depends on the merc's skills** (Survival, Swords…, design/mercs.md).
- A merc **equips four** battle skills; the rest wait at base.

## Consequences

- A merc collects about 13 battle skills by Lv 12 and the player picks the best four: the equip screen is where the choice lives.
- Each skill offers several base battle skills, so a merc can roll the same base twice with different modifiers (E-0004 career sample: Strike four times).
- D-0020's skill unlocks (every 3 levels) still stand; a new skill widens which battle skills can come up (Claude's reading, to confirm).
- Touches design/mercs.md and the working model's slot ramp.
