## Context

D-0016 moved the player's level-up choice to skills and left open how often and how many choices ("every (2? 3?) levels … one of two or three choices (these choices are rolled at character start or smth?)").

## Decision

Proposed by Claude:

- **Every 3 levels** (Lv 3, 6, 9, 12): four unlocks over a career, on top of the starting skills. Each unlock is an event, and skills, which have their own XP (D-0012), have time to grow.
- **Pick 1 of 2.** Two options make a sharp trade-off and keep mercs distinct.
- **Rolled at recruitment and shown on the merc's screen** ("Lv 3: Bows or Tracking"). The whole gacha lands at hiring, where the player judges a recruit's future, and the career can be planned.
- Race and background weight which skills appear.

## Options considered

- **Every 2 levels** (6 unlocks): more choices, but skills dilute and each one matters less.
- **Pick 1 of 3:** more agency, weaker identity.
- **Rolled at the moment of level up:** a surprise each time, but no planning when hiring.
