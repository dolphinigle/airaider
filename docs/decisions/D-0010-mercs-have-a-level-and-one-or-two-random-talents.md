## Context

The E-0001 stat screen marked strong stats with descriptive traits ("Strong", "Smart"). The designer replaced them with talents and added levels.

## Decision

The designer's words: "add LEVEL too. like Lv 1. and when they level up, add a 'level up screen' that player can choose which attr they can increase in. remove the 'strong/smart' thing, instead, add a 'talented' to one or two stats, e.g., STR+ or STR++. The two stats are chosen randomly, can they can overlap (eg STR+ DEX+ or STR++ one)."

- Every merc has a **level**, starting at Lv 1.
- **Talents:** two random draws over the five stats (D-0009). They may land on the same stat, giving one stat **++** or two stats **+** each. Talents replace descriptive stat traits like "Strong" and "Smart".
- On level up, a **level-up screen** lets the player choose which stat increases.

## Consequences

- How XP is earned and what a talent does at level up: proposed in D-0011.
