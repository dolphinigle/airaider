## Context

D-0045 had Heavy (two-handers) and Light (one-handers), with weapon tags (axe, hammer, polearm) filtering bases. D-0061 put swords in Light.

## Decision

Accepted by the designer, 2026-10-22: "melee split good lets go."


The designer, 2026-10-22: "how about Fast weapons (swords, daggers), Cleaving weapons (greatsword, axes), Heavy weapons (hammers, clubs), and Long-reach melee (polearms)? because their abilities likely differ this way."

- **Fast Weapons:** swords, daggers, rapiers. Claude: multi-hits, bleeds, crits, speed.
- **Cleaving Weapons:** greatswords, axes. Claude: hits two adjacent foes, bleeds.
- **Heavy Weapons:** hammers, clubs, maces. Claude: stuns, armor break, knockback.
- **Long-reach Weapons:** polearms, spears. Claude: strikes from rank 2–3, reaches deep ranks, pierces through a rank.

What D-0045 and D-0061 rulings stand: Unarmed is its own skill and covers claws and bites (werewolves); Ranged stays one skill with weapon tags (bow, crossbow, gun, thrown); shapeshifting and stances are dropped. Melee weapon tags are no longer needed.

## Consequences

- A Bounty Hunter is Villainy plus any melee skill.
- Melee identity rides on ranks (Long-reach) and target count (Cleaving), which uses the D-0041 line.
