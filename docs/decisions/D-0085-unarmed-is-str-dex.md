## Context

D-0084 paired Unarmed (fists, kicks, grapples, martial arts, claws and bites) with DEX alone.

## Decision

The designer, 2026-10-22: "Unarmed should be STR+DEX no?" Claude agreed: punches and grapples are muscle, martial arts are speed.

**Unarmed is STR + DEX**, the same pair as Fast Weapons. This one row amends D-0084; the rest of it stands.

## Consequences

- Load per stat: STR 6, DEX 6.5, INT 7, CHA 6, CON 4.5 (flatter than before).
- Two battle skills now share STR + DEX; they differ by what they do, not by stats.
