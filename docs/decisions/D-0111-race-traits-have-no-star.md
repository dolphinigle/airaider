## Context

D-0068 gave every trait a star; D-0110 carried Claude's reading that a race trait's star scales its passive. E-0011's recruits showed race traits with stars.

## Decision

The designer, 2026-10-23: "race trait doesnt have stars."

- **Race traits have no star:** every merc of a race gets the same race trait. Amends D-0068 (stars stay on personality and body traits) and drops Claude's reading in D-0110.

Claude's reading: gender (flavor only, D-0110) has no star either.

## Consequences

- A race's passive and stat shift are fixed; rarity chase lives in skill, personality and body stars.
