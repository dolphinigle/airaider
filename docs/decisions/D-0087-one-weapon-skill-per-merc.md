## Context

D-0063 split melee into Fast, Cleaving, Heavy and Long-reach; Unarmed and Ranged are weapon skills too. Nothing stopped a merc rolling two of them.

## Decision

The designer, 2026-10-22: "for the weapons ones make sure each merc can only get one. otherwise having both unarmed and cleaving weapons make little sense. they can combine with magic."

- **A merc has at most one weapon skill:** Fast, Cleaving, Heavy, Long-reach Weapons, Unarmed.
- Every other battle skill combines freely with it: the magics, Defender, Leadership, Performance, Animal Companion, Dirty Fighting, Stealth, Doctor, Alchemy.
- **Open:** whether Ranged counts as a weapon skill (Claude: let Ranged pair with one melee weapon, as the Darkest Dungeon Highwayman does with a blade and a pistol).

## Consequences

- Recruit rolls and the Lv 7 pick of 1 of 3 skip a second weapon skill.
- A merc's weapon is readable at a glance; the rest of the kit is how they fight with it.
