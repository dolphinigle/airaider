## Context

D-0094 added Fortune (LUK) as the coin-manipulation skill. Reviewing the whole roster, the designer reshaped it.

## Decision

The designer, 2026-10-22: "the fortune skill is really weird yeah. lets remove it. Change Fury to Berserk. Add Precision (increase chance coin flip heads) skill under DEX. Add warfare back under STR+INT. need one more that manipulates enemy resistances".

- **Fortune is gone.** LUK has no skills again (D-0079, D-0086).
- **Fury is renamed Berserk** (same scope, D-0089).
- **Precision** (DEX) joins the non-weapon battle skills: raises the heads chance of coin flips (self or allies), and so crits (D-0092).
- **Warfare** (STR + INT) comes back as a battle skill (deleted in D-0073). Its scope is open (Claude's draft: the soldier's craft: combined attacks with an ally, breaking foe shields, formation tricks like swap-and-strike; moving teammates and focus fire stay with Leadership).
- **A skill that lowers foe resistances** (e.g. weaker to Fire) is wanted; which one is open.

## Consequences

- 25 battle skills now (8 weapon + 17 others), 26 with the resistance skill.
- Precision puts DEX back to about 9 in load; Warfare adds to STR and INT.
- Single Weapon STR + DEX and Dirty Fighting DEX + CON (designer) stand, pending one table decision.
