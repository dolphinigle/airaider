## Context

D-0087 limited each merc to one weapon skill and left open whether Ranged counts.

## Decision

The designer, 2026-10-22: "so: at MOST one of these: cleaving weapons, long reach weapons, ranged, heavy weapons, fast weapons, unarmed."

- **A merc has at most one weapon skill:** Fast, Cleaving, Heavy, Long-reach Weapons, Unarmed, **Ranged**.
- Every other battle skill combines freely with it (D-0087: "they can combine with magic").

## Consequences

- Recruit rolls and the Lv 7 pick skip a second weapon skill.
- No blade-and-pistol hybrid (Darkest Dungeon Highwayman): a Ranged merc's melee comes from generic bases (D-0050) or other skills.
- A merc with no weapon skill (a pure mage) gets their first 0-cooldown attack from another skill or the generic fallback (D-0050).
