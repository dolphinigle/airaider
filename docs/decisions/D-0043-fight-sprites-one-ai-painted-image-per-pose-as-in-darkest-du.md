## Context

The battle system follows Darkest Dungeon (D-0041). The designer saw that its sprite approach fits AI image generation. v3 already painted roster portraits with `gpt-image-2.5-sunburst` at low quality, about half a US cent each (archive/v3/UI.md §5).

## Decision

The designer's words: "the sprite system can also follow from darkest dungeon then, which is nice. Aka we ask the image generator generate idle, attacking pose, etc then just use like in darkest dungeon one image per animation".

- **Each fighter has a small set of poses, one image per pose,** generated by the image model.
- **The fight swaps poses** in the Darkest Dungeon way:
  - the attacker steps forward;
  - the camera zooms in;
  - the hit target shakes;
  - damage numbers show.

  There are no frame-by-frame animations.

## Open (Claude's proposal, not yet ruled)

- **The pose set**, about 6 per merc:
  - idle;
  - one action pose per ability kind the merc has (strike, shoot, cast, guard, heal);
  - hurt;
  - downed.
- **Foes** get the same set, made once per foe type, not per fight.
- **When images are made:**
  - Only for mercs who join the roster (the v3 rule), at about 3¢ for 6 poses.
  - Tavern candidates get the idle pose only, or none.
- **Engine risk (Claude's):** the same merc must look the same in every pose. The plan is to paint the idle pose first and use it as the reference image for the others. A small test is needed before relying on this.
- The text UI shows pose names as text, so it stays at parity.

## Status note
