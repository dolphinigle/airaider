## Context

D-0077 merged the check skills to nine; the merged skills' names were Claude's working picks.

## Decision

The designer, 2026-10-22: "rename Deception to Intimidation. rename Engineering to Craft."

- **Intimidation**: threats, interrogation, lies, bluffs, disguises (was Deception + Intimidation).
- **Craft**: building, machines, explosives, traps, weapons, armor, repairs (was Engineering + Smithing).
- Persuasion and Lore keep their names.

## Consequences

The nine: Athletics, Survival, Awareness, Roguery, Streetwise, Persuasion, Intimidation, Lore, Craft. Touches design/mercs.md and NOW.
