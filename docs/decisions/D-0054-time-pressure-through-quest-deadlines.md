## Context

D-0052 parked how to keep players from grinding and how to add time pressure.

## Decision

The designer's words: "quest deadline is the way to go probably".

- **Quests on the board have deadlines.** With one pick a week (D-0052), choosing a quest means letting others lapse.

## Open

- Parked with anti-grind; fun comes first.
- How long deadlines run.
- What a lapsed quest costs: only the reward, or something that gets worse later.
- Whether some quests have no deadline.
