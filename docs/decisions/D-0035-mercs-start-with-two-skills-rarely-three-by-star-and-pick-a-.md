## Context

D-0020 unlocks a skill every 3 levels (pick 1 of 2). Skills now also decide which abilities a merc can learn (D-0033).

## Decision

Accepted by the designer (2026-10-22): "yeah sounds good."

The designer, proposing: "insetad of learning new skill every 6 levels, just have each unit start with two skills (rarely three, the rarity scales up with stars), then at level 7 learn a third one choice btween 3 or something? what do you think, is that more interestigng?"

- A merc **starts with two skills**; a **third is rare**, likelier at higher stars.
- At **Lv 7** they learn one more skill: **pick 1 of 3**, hidden until then (as in D-0020).

## Consequences

- Claude: more interesting. One fork at Lv 7 is a build decision ("which family of abilities opens for the back half") where D-0020's four small picks were incremental; it lands just as the first two skills' abilities run out (D-0033).
- Skill checks: a merc with 2–3 skills fails more checks outside them, so who you send matters more.
- Stars would then affect more than modifiers (D-0027 said "for now rarity only affect mods").
