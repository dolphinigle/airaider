## Context

In E-0001 run 2 the narration packed mechanics into prose ("Three of them, one of him. He takes a cut across the ribs."). The designer judged the writing not great.

## Decision

The designer's words: "Writing not great, should just be simpler. for eample Bandits on the road! Atlas took a cut! -- then engine tells its wounded. The second is decent."

- Each test gets one simple line announcing it ("Bandits on the road!") and one simple line for the result ("Atlas took a cut!").
- The **engine** states the mechanical effect ("Atlas is WOUNDED"), never the prose.
- Reference voice: "Bleeding and freezing, Atlas gives up before dawn." was judged decent.

## Consequences

- Within D-0006's light tone, lines get shorter and plainer: who did what, in a few words.
- The writer needs only the event and the outcome, so prompts stay tiny.
