## Context

The working model had tactic books as whole rules ("vs undead → S3") (E-0003, partysim2).

## Decision

The designer's words: "for TACTICS, i think you want to separate the CONDITION and EFFECT. e.g. a tactic book "use S4" and condition can be "when an ally hp < 50%". a third slot for tactic is like "target ally with lowest HP" or "target enemy with higest damage"."

A tactic has three slots:
- **Condition:** when it fires ("an ally is below 50% HP").
- **Action:** what the merc does ("use S4").
- **Target:** who it hits ("the ally with the lowest HP", "the enemy dealing the most damage").

## Consequences

- Close to Final Fantasy XII's gambits, which split the target out of the condition as well.
- The target picks within the battle skill's own reach (a frontline-only skill still hits the front).
- Open: which parts are loot and how many tactic lines a merc gets.
