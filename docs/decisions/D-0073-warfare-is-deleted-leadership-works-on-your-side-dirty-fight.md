## Context

Warfare (debuffs, moving teammates and foes, D-0046/D-0058) overlapped with Leadership in name and had a thin identity next to the melee split (D-0063) and Dirty Fighting (D-0071).

## Decision

The designer, 2026-10-22, on Claude's proposal: "yup sonds good."

- **Warfare is deleted.** Its jobs move:
  - moving foes → Heavy Weapons (knockback), Long-reach Weapons (push and pull), Dirty Fighting (yank a foe forward);
  - debuffing foes → Dirty Fighting (blind, weaken, mark);
  - moving teammates and "focus fire" orders → Leadership.
- **Leadership works on your side, Dirty Fighting on theirs.**

## Consequences

- 19 battle skills. Man-at-Arms maps to Defense + Leadership.
