## Context

D-0029 left open what survives a star-up or a reroll.

## Decision

The designer: "what survives a reset is EVERYTHING except the rng seeds -- the rng seeds are only those that affect things on LEVEL UP. it doesnt affect STARTING POSITION."

- A merc's **starting position** (race, star, starting stats, talents, starting skills and abilities, traits, name) never changes.
- The **level-up seed** decides only what level ups roll: stat growth, new abilities and their modifiers, skill offers.
- A star-up or reroll (D-0029) resets the level and rerolls that seed; nothing else changes.

## Consequences

- A reset is a fresh climb for the same merc: a second chance at better level-up luck, and with a star-up, better modifier odds on the way.
