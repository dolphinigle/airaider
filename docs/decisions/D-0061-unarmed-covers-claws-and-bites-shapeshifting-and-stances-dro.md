## Context

E-0005 round 4 left two Darkest Dungeon mechanics unplaced (shapeshifting, stances) and asked how the Bounty Hunter's weapon fits.

## Decision

The designer, 2026-10-22: "the two mechs are too specific, lets ignore them. bounty hunter probably either HEAVY or LIGHT weapon right? swords fall into light weapon i think. ... i think i want to make unarmed also encompass things like werewolf fighters"

- **Shapeshifting and stances are dropped.**
- **Unarmed covers natural weapons:** claws, bites and beast fighters (werewolves) as well as fists, kicks, grapples and martial arts.
- **Swords are Light Weapons** (greatswords stay Heavy). A Bounty Hunter is Villainy plus Heavy or Light.

## Consequences

- Beastfolk races have a natural home in Unarmed.
