## Context

v3 bet on AI-written engaging story and stalled near 6/10 (D-0002). During the first v4 sim (E-0001), while choosing a substitute for combat, the designer set the balance between mechanics and AI text.

## Decision

The designer's words: "so previously, going for engaging story doesnt work. so instead, we want a mechanic heavy game with ai only providing small story."

- The game is **mechanics-heavy**: the fun comes from the systems, the rolls, and the player's choices.
- AI text is **small**: short flavor that colors a mechanical result. It never carries the fun alone.

## Consequences

- Candidate mechanics are judged on whether they are fun with the text removed.
- AI writing tasks stay short and narrow (a line or two per moment), which also suits cheap models.

## Options considered

- Story-led game with AI as the main content (v3): rejected, see D-0002.
