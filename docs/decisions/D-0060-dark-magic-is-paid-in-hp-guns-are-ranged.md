## Context

E-0005 round 4 checked every Darkest Dungeon class against our battle skills. All fit; four mechanics had no home: shapeshifting, abilities paid in HP, stances, guns.

## Decision

The designer, 2026-10-22: "dark magic is paid in HP, good enough. Isnt guns just ranged?"

- **Dark Magic abilities cost HP** (the Flagellant's mechanic lives here). Costs elsewhere stay cooldown-only (D-0041).
- **Guns are Ranged**: one more weapon tag (gun) beside bow, crossbow and thrown (D-0045).

## Consequences

- Dark gets a distinct cost: strong effects paid in the caster's own HP.
- Shapeshifting and stances stay unplaced (not ruled).
