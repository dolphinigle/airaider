## Context

D-0013 said most quests test two stats, and left open whether that means one test pooling two stats or a quest whose tests span two stats.

## Decision

The designer's words: "quests can test one, two, or even rarely three stats."

- A test names **one, two, or rarely three stats**. The merc flips the coins of all of them together, plus the matching skill, against the threshold (D-0013). *Claude's reading: stats pool within a test, like the "STR + Swords" tests in E-0001.*

## Consequences

- Thresholds scale with how many stats a test pools.
- A two-stat test rewards a merc strong in both; a lopsided merc lands in between.
