## Context

Response to the level-up proposal (D-0011) in E-0001.

## Decision

The designer's words: "so for level up definitely has a 'button' to allow them to level up when they reach the exp threshold. ofc xp can 'overflow' to next one. xp should be like in path of exile where your xp gain is severaly penalized if oging on lower level quests. SKILLS have their own exp i agree, sounds fun."

- When a merc reaches the XP threshold, a **Level up** button appears; the player presses it to open the level-up screen (D-0010).
- XP **overflows** into the next level.
- **Quests have levels.** XP from a quest below the merc's level is **severely penalized**, as in Path of Exile.
- **Skills have their own XP** and level up separately from the merc.

## Consequences

- Mercs must keep taking quests at their level to grow; farming easy quests stalls them.
- Exact numbers (the penalty curve, skill XP thresholds) are open; Path of Exile's curve is: full XP within a safe zone of about 3 levels, then a steep falloff.
