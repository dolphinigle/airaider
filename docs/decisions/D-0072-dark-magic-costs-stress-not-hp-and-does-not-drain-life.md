## Context

D-0060 made Dark Magic abilities cost HP, and listed draining life in its scope.

## Decision

The designer, 2026-10-22: "dark magic shouldnt drain life. dark magic thing is that it costs stress (not HP)"

- **Dark Magic abilities cost stress** (D-0042), not HP.
- **Dark Magic does not drain life.** It keeps curses, necromancy and pacts, and deals Chaos (D-0070).
- From D-0060, still standing: **guns are Ranged** (a weapon tag beside bow, crossbow and thrown).

## Consequences

- Dark casters push themselves toward Breakdown or Resolve at 100 stress: power now, a WIL check later. Stress relief at the base matters more for them.
