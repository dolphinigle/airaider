## Context

The designer: "And also Passives -- so a unit can have like 4 abilities and 4 passives? or is that too much? or do you think its better that passives come from other place like traits?" Personality traits (Gruff) have no mechanics yet (design/mercs.md, Open).

## Decision

Proposed by Claude: passives are **personality traits**, one or two per merc, rolled at recruitment (part of the starting position, D-0032). "Stubborn: can't be stunned while above half HP."

## Consequences

- Four abilities stay the one battle loadout to choose; traits say who the merc is and never need managing.
- Gives traits a job, and their text doubles as character (readable story).
- A trait can also touch skill checks or base life, not only fights.

## Options considered

- Four abilities and four passives: doubles the loadout to read per merc (8 × 4 mercs = 32 lines before a fight) and doubles the equip decision.
- Passives learned at level up alongside abilities: competes with abilities for the level-up moment.
