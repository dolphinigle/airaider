## Context

Abilities come from a merc's skills (D-0033), so the skill list decides who can fight and how. The designer narrowed which core skills give abilities.

## Decision

The designer's words: "the core fighting skills would be: heavy weapon, light weapon, ranged, defense, warfare (buffs/debuffs), animal companion (new, replaces beasts), stealth, leadership (buffs/debuffs), performance (buffs/debuffs), doctor (new, healing), alchemy (status effects), science (status effects), all magic ones?"

- **Fighting skills, 18** (with Engineering, added below):
  - weapons: Heavy Weapons, Light Weapons, **Unarmed** (D-0045; added by Claude, it was missing from the list), Ranged;
  - Defense;
  - buffs and debuffs: Warfare, Leadership, Performance;
  - **Animal Companion** (new, replaces Beasts);
  - **Villainy**, which is bounty-hunter skills (designer, 2026-10-22: "Villainy is a fighting skill. its basically bounty hunter skills");
  - Stealth;
  - **Doctor** (new, healing);
  - status effects: Alchemy, Nature Lore (was Science);
  - magic: Elemental, Holy, Dark.
- **The other core skills are minor fighting skills, not purely for quests** (designer: "some of the others are not PURE quest only. for example, athletics probably have something that allows them to move between ranks. theyre just less powerful skills"). They have fewer and weaker bases. Claude's niches, not yet ruled:

  | skill | niche |
  |---|---|
  | Athletics | moving between ranks (dash, swap with an ally), dodge |
  | Survival | second wind (small self heal), shrugging off damage over time |
  | Awareness | marking a foe, revealing hidden foes, going faster in round 1 |
  | Roguery | dirty tricks (Blind, Weak), caltrops |
  | Intimidation | fear (Weak, a skipped turn), stressing foes |
  | Deception | feints, a decoy that pulls one hit, a disguise (can't be targeted for a round) |
  | Persuasion | calming (stress relief), talking a foe out of attacking for a turn |
  | Arcane Lore | exposing the weakness of undead and monsters, warding off curses |
  | Streetwise, Bargaining, Civilization Lore | maybe none (a bribe that makes a hired foe flee?) |

- **Craft changes** (designer: "I think we want to change Craft. Alchemy is already separate. Maybe Smithing and something else?"). Claude's proposal, not yet ruled: split it into two skills.
  - **Smithing** covers weapons, armor and repairs. In fights: sharpen an ally's weapon (more damage), mend armor (a shield). At base: gear, once gear comes.
  - **Engineering** covers building, machines, explosives and setting traps. In fights: bombs, caltrops, barricades. At base: construction.

## Rulings, 2026-10-22 (designer)

- **Civilian mercs exist.** Not every merc has a fighting skill: "some are civilian mercs meant for more civilization quests."
- **Doctor heals. Alchemy** keeps potions, poisons and flasks ("yes"). Explosives go to Engineering if Craft splits.
- **Science** analyzes monsters and the like: it exposes weaknesses ("probably things like analyze monsters and stuffs"). It was renamed Nature Lore below.
- **The support skills:**
  - **Leadership** is mostly stress healing and team buffs.
  - **Warfare** is mostly debuffing and **moving teammates and enemies around**.
  - **Performance** is stress healing and buffs.
- **Craft splits into Smithing and Engineering** (designer: "Smithing and Engineering sounds good").
- **Science becomes Nature Lore** (designer: "Change science to nature lore then"). It covers plants, beasts, weather, stars and natural monsters, and in fights it analyzes them and exposes their weaknesses. "How things work" and numbers go to Engineering. Claude's split: Arcane Lore covers undead, demons and curses.
- **Animal Companion** works like Darkest Dungeon's Houndmaster: the pet acts through the merc's abilities and takes no rank of its own.

- **"keep those"** (designer, 2026-10-22), on Claude's last three proposals:
  - **Leadership** gives party-wide auras and Resolve; **Performance** gives songs that last a few turns, Haste and distraction.
  - **Engineering** is a main fighting skill, and **Smithing** is a minor one.
  - **Villainy** keeps its name.
- A civilian in a fight uses a generic attack (D-0050, D-0051).
- Rank movement comes from Athletics (moving yourself) and Warfare (moving others).
- The minor-skill niches above are a working list, to be refined in the ability review.
