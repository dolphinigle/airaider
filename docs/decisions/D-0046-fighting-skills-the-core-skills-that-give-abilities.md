## Context

Abilities come from a merc's skills (D-0033), so the skill list decides who can fight and how. The designer narrowed which core skills give abilities.

## Decision

The designer's words: "the core fighting skills would be: heavy weapon, light weapon, ranged, defense, warfare (buffs/debuffs), animal companion (new, replaces beasts), stealth, leadership (buffs/debuffs), performance (buffs/debuffs), doctor (new, healing), alchemy (status effects), science (status effects), all magic ones?"

- **Fighting skills, 16:**
  - weapons: Heavy Weapons, Light Weapons, **Unarmed** (D-0045; added by Claude, it was missing from the list), Ranged;
  - Defense;
  - buffs and debuffs: Warfare, Leadership, Performance;
  - **Animal Companion** (new, replaces Beasts);
  - Stealth;
  - **Doctor** (new, healing);
  - status effects: Alchemy, Science;
  - magic: Elemental, Holy, Dark.
- **The other core skills give no abilities.** They work in quests and skill checks only:
  - Athletics, Survival, Awareness;
  - Roguery, Villainy, Streetwise;
  - Persuasion, Deception, Bargaining, Intimidation;
  - Civilization Lore, Arcane Lore, Craft.

## Open (Claude's flags)

- **A merc whose skills don't fight has no abilities.** A merc starting with Bargaining + Streetwise has an empty ability pool, and one fighting skill runs dry by about Lv 4 (5 bases, one per level). Options:
  - every merc starts with at least one fighting skill;
  - a shared basic pool;
  - quest-only specialists who sit out fights.
- **Doctor vs Alchemy:** bandaging and remedies move to Doctor. Alchemy keeps potions, poisons and bombs.
- **Science:** what its status effects are (exposing weaknesses, smoke, flash?), and whether it is Science Lore renamed.
- **Telling the buffers apart:**
  - Warfare: marks, expose, formation;
  - Leadership: rallying, lowering stress, party buffs;
  - Performance: songs, Haste, distracting foes.
- **Animal Companion:** the pet acts through the merc's abilities and takes no rank, as Darkest Dungeon's Houndmaster does. In quests it keeps Beasts' riding, taming and handling.
- **Athletics** stays in the speed coins (DEX + Athletics), though it has no abilities.
