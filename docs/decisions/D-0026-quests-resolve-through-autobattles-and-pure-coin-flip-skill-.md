## Context

E-0001 showed a quest of plain coin tests felt flat, and that a test naming one combat skill makes little sense. E-0003 researched about 60 games, mocked five quest models and simmed a party coin autobattle: the player's setup swung fights from 0% to 95% wins, the best setup changed by foe, and close finishes happened. The designer steered it to battles plus checks.

## Decision

The designer's words: "so likely we'll have battles AND skill checks. skill checks are just pure coin flips." · "the 2x3 grid etc stuff is not locked. the LOCKED part i think is: Autobattler and skill checks."

- **Fights are autobattles.** The player sets the party up before the fight; the fight then plays itself (displayed, D-0023; losing injures, D-0024).
- **Everything else is a skill check:** a pure coin flip of the merc's pool against a threshold (D-0013, D-0019).
- Not locked: the grid, party size, skill slots and tactic books, and the battle-skill model (D-0025, proposed). These are the working model for the next playtest.

## Consequences

- Combat skills feed battles; non-combat skills feed checks (and may also give battle skills, D-0025).
- Whether battles make quests more fun is still open (findings); the next simulated playtest tests it.
