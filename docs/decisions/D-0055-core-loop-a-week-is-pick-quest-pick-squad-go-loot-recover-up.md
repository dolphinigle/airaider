## Context

With the campaign following Darkest Dungeon (D-0052) and fights played by hand (D-0053), the designer set out the core loop and asked whether it is good.

## Decision

The designer's words: "pick quest to do this week, pick squad to go, go, get loot, spend loot to recover squad, extra loot if quest succeed for upgrading, pick another quest and another squad, go, repeat? is this good?"

- **A week:** pick a quest → pick a squad → go → get loot → spend loot to recover the squad.
- **A successful quest gives extra loot for upgrading.**
- Then the next week begins.

## Open (Claude's review, 2026-10-22)

- **Recruiting belongs in the loop.** New recruits show up each week, as on Darkest Dungeon's stagecoach (the star gacha, D-0027). The squad pillar needs it.
- **The level-up reveal is a moment of its own:** a new ability, named by the AI, with modifiers rolled by star.
- **The choices inside "go":**
  - fights played by hand;
  - who makes each skill check;
  - pushing on or retreating between battles.
- **Spending is the key economic choice:** recovering (stress, injuries) against upgrading (buildings, level caps). Gold must stay tight.
- **A failed quest still pays a little.** Failure should stay content, not dead air (findings).
- **A long-term goal** pulls the loop forward (Darkest Dungeon has its final dungeon). Not designed yet.
