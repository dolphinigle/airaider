## Context

D-0009 took v3's five stats, where DEX merged agility and perception. Checking D&D's skills (reference/rpg-skill-lists.md) showed perception-type skills had no natural stat. Claude offered a sixth stat, WIL or WIS.

## Decision

The designer's words: "WIL is 'CON' i think. how about PCP isntead?"

Restated in full (this replaces D-0009):
- **Six stats:** **STR** strength · **DEX** agility, hands, reflexes · **PER** perception: noticing, aim, reading people · **INT** intelligence · **CHA** charisma · **CON** constitution: endurance, toughness **and willpower** (nerve, fear, curses).
- Skills are counted in coins like stats (Swords ●●● = 3 coins), unchanged from D-0009.
- Abbreviation **PER** (as in Fallout's SPECIAL), proposed by Claude in place of "PCP".

## Consequences

- Usual stat pairings in D-0021 shift: Awareness → PER, INT · Tracking → PER, INT · Ranged → DEX, PER · Survival → CON, PER.
- With three coins per level (D-0018), an untalented stat grows about 0.38 per level instead of 0.42–0.5.
