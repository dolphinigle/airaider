## Context

D-0014 caps growth at one coin per stat per level, which rules out D-0011's "talented stats grow more" idea. Talents (D-0010) still need a reason to matter after the start.

## Decision

Proposed by Claude:

- **At start:** each talent mark adds one coin to its stat (STR++ starts at ●●●●).
- **At level up:** when the player places a coin on a talented stat, the game flips one coin per mark (+ = 1, ++ = 2). On any head, that coin is **free**: the player gets a fourth coin to place on another stat. The one-per-stat cap holds.
- So talented stats are cheaper to grow, and leaning into a talent is a small gamble that can pay off with a wider level.

## Consequences

- Level up stays a coin moment and keeps the D-0014 cap.

## Options considered

- **Start bonus only:** talents add coins at creation and nothing after. Simplest, but talents fade as levels pile up.
- **Talent raises the cap:** a ++ stat may take two coins per level. Breaks the D-0014 cap.
