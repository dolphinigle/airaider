## Context

Under the Darkest Dungeon campaign (D-0052), Claude asked whether skill checks should be a big part of the game or whether it is mostly combat. Mostly combat means merging the check skills.

## Decision

The designer's words: "i dont think we'll have civilization quests mostly anymore with this structure tho?"

- **The game is mostly combat** (designer: "yea i mean mostly combat sounds good."). Quests are expeditions of fights. Civilization (all-check) quests are mostly gone.

## Open

- **Civilian mercs** (D-0046) lose their quests. Claude's options:
  - they become the base crew (RimWorld-style jobs: Bargaining lowers shop prices, Smithing repairs gear, Doctor speeds healing);
  - or civilians are dropped, and every merc has a fighting skill.
- **Merging the 12 minor skills.** Claude's proposal is 6: Athletics, Scouting (Survival + Awareness), Roguery (+ Streetwise), Talk (Persuasion + Deception + Bargaining), Lore (Civilization + Arcane), Smithing; Intimidation folds into Villainy.
- **Jobs for the non-combat skills outside fights:**
  - base jobs;
  - knowing what lies ahead (Scouting and Lore reveal a quest's foes and twists before you pick the squad: "knowledge feeds the next roll", findings);
  - the few checks left inside quests (still to be decided, D-0055).
