## Context

E-0009 used a placeholder roster of eight races (with bearkin, dwarf, giantkin). D-0106 set a race's stat effect to +1 / −1.

## Decision

The designer, 2026-10-23: "races: wolfkin, catfolk, elf, dragonkin, human (should have races too like northerner, eastern, western, southern which maps roughly to scandinavian, european, japanese, and african)"

- **Five races:** wolfkin, catfolk, elf, dragonkin, human.
- **Humans come in four peoples:** northerner, western, eastern, southern, roughly Scandinavian, European, Japanese and African in look and naming (Claude's reading of the order: north = Scandinavian, west = European, east = Japanese, south = African).

Claude's working calls:
- Stat shifts (D-0106, + / −): wolfkin DEX / INT, catfolk LUK / CON, elf INT / CON, dragonkin CON / DEX.
- **Human peoples are flavor only** (looks, names, the AI's writing) and share the human's random +1 / −1. Fixed stat shifts on real-world-flavored peoples would read as stereotypes.
- Exclusive skills: Beast Blood for wolfkin and catfolk, Ancient Blood for dragonkin, both also for shapeshifters (E-0007).

## Consequences

- Race identity: trait passive (D-0038), exclusive skill, stat shift; for humans, their people's flavor.
