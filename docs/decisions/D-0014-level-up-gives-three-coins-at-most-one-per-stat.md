## Context

D-0011 proposed one coin per level and asked whether one, two or three is better.

## Decision

The designer's words: "How about three coins, BUT, you can gain AT MOST one coin per stat per level."

- Each level up gives **three coins**. The player places them on the level-up screen (D-0010).
- **At most one coin per stat per level**, so a level raises three different stats by one.

## Consequences

- A merc can't dump a whole level into one stat; specialists still grow their key stats every level, and the third coin goes somewhere else.
- With five stats, each level leaves two stats behind: the choice is which two.
- What talents do at level up: proposed in D-0015.
