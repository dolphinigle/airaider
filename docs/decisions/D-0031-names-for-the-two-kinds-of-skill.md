## Context

"Skill" means two things now: what a merc knows (Survival, Swords → Light Weapons; tested in skill checks, D-0026) and what a merc does in a fight (Lunge +Swift, equipped four at a time, D-0028). The designer: "pls distinguish between battle skill and skkills like survival -- pls invent PROPER name so we can distinguish the two."

## Decision

The designer: "i dislike Arts. How about Ability?"

- **Skills**: Survival, Swords and the rest; what a skill check tests (design/mercs.md).
- **Abilities**: what a merc does in a fight ("Brann learned a new ability: Twin Hew"). An ability comes from a skill.

## Options considered

- Arts (Claude's first proposal): the designer disliked it.
- Techniques, Moves, or renaming skills to Trades.
