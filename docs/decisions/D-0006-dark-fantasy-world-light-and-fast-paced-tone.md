## Context

During E-0001 the game narrated Atlas's walk in short lines ("Suddenly a bear appears!", "One growl, three cuts. The chief flees and the road is clear."). The designer set the tone from those samples.

## Decision

The designer's words: "pls rmb the tone: world is dark fantasy but tone should be light / fast paced like the above."

- **World:** dark fantasy (bandits, beasts, danger, death are real).
- **Tone:** light and fast. Short punchy lines, one beat each, a little wry. The reference voice is the E-0001 run 1 transcript ([log.md](../../experiments/E-0001-first-game-sim/log.md)).

## Consequences

- AI lines stay short and brisk, which fits D-0004 (small story) and cheap models.
- Grim events are told briskly and without wallowing; the danger is in the stakes, not the prose.
