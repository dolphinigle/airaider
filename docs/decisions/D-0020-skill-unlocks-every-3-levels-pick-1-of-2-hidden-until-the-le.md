## Context

D-0017 showed a merc's future skill choices on their screen from recruitment. Seeing it on Atlas's screen, the designer changed that.

## Decision

The designer's words: "skil unlock should be hidden."

Restated in full (this replaces D-0017):
- **Every 3 levels** (Lv 3, 6, 9, 12) the merc unlocks a new skill.
- **Pick 1 of 2.**
- The two options are rolled at recruitment (weighted by race and background) but stay **hidden until that level up**.

## Consequences

- Each skill unlock is a surprise reveal on the level-up screen.
