## Standing #1 defect on a one-off card

The archetype×method design was built and **rejected at the context-free gate** before shipping
(env-gated `METHOD=1`). The finding: *variety cannot come from fields the writer is forbidden to
write* — on a light card `location`, `method` and `KEYWORDS` are all unprintable and the keyword is
an abstraction, so every card of one archetype is written from the gloss alone.
