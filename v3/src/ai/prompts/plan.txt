- seed: the idea under the story, never pasted.
[[shape]]- shape: the kind of story.[[episodes]] episodes: the jobs before the showdown, in order: what the soldiers do, the kind of win.[[/episodes]]
[[types]]- jobs: how many before the showdown. types: what soldiers do, the kind of win.
- cast: the people; traits: what they are like.[[notrade]] One with no trade gets one that fits the seed.[[/notrade]] Use them all as the seed's people; add nobody who matters unless the seed has them. [[stake]]- stake: what is at risk; lose makes it concrete.
[[!personal]]- ending: whose fate the showdown decides. likely: the end it builds toward. ways: how it can end, and what each means.
[[personal]]- ending: whose old matter the showdown settles. likely: the way it builds toward. ways: how it can be settled.
- land, places: the setting; places may repeat.
- tone: how it should feel.
[[direction]]- direction: the player's wish; it wins over tone.
[[avoid]]- avoid: recent stories; differ in pattern, not only names.

Write:
- title: a few plain words.
- cast: [[personal]]except the soldier, [[/personal]]a label: at most one of their traits, then their trade; no name.
- [[!personal]]asker: want, what the one who asked wants[[/!personal]][[personal]]soldier: want, what they want[[/personal]] to happen, naming who or what[[!personal]]; never what becomes of the person in ending[[/!personal]].[[personal]] past: the old wrong, naming who was wronged.[[/personal]]
- question: one sentence starting "Nobody knows": the hidden thing the player most wants to know, not the ending.
- episodes, in order. job: a verb, whom or what, and one place; it follows the last win. people: ids of those there in person[[!personal]]; episode 1 includes the person in ending[[/!personal]]; ids nowhere else. trouble: who stands in the way (by label if in cast; armed people or a beast), with what, what they will do. win: what success changes, naming who or what. gain: a thing, captive or ally the company then holds. learn: a plain fact the win brings out, clear on its own, that narrows the answer, never naming what the question asks for; the showdown keeps the last piece. why: what [[!personal]]the one who asked[[/!personal]][[personal]]the soldier[[/personal]] can then do toward their want with only what the job names: an action, never seeing, knowing, learning, showing or proving.
- showdown: job, people, trouble, why as above[[!personal]]; its job names the person in ending[[/!personal]]. edge: how each job's gain, in order, helps here. settles: a done fact, no "if": how [[!personal]]the one who asked[[/!personal]][[personal]]the soldier[[/personal]] gets their want. All these fit every way in ending, even after failed jobs. lose: what [[!personal]]the one who asked[[/!personal]][[personal]]the soldier[[/personal]] loses for good if the company fails.
- options: per way in ending, what the soldiers do, verb first.
- answer: who or what, and why, in a sentence or two: surprising, not in the seed, no new person, fitting the learns, every way in ending and settles.

Titles, jobs, whys and troubles, the showdown's too, are shown before play: never a learn or the answer.

A story that works: one person wants one thing badly, someone stands in the way, each for a plain human reason.

Plain words, third person; no numbers, nothing modern, no pay. The company never met anyone in cast[[personal]] but its own soldier[[/personal]][[memory]][[personal]] and[[/personal]][[!personal]] but[[/!personal]] the one with a memory[[/memory]]; only the company lives at its fort.
