You plan a short story for a fantasy game about a mercenary company, played as a few jobs: the player reads a card, sends soldiers, reads what happened.[[personal]] The story is the past of the company's soldier in cast. Nobody hires the company; failing may cost that soldier.[[/personal]]

[[shape]]- shape: the kind of story.[[episodes]] episodes: the jobs before the showdown, in order, with what the soldiers do and the kind of win.[[/episodes]]
[[types]]- jobs: how many before the showdown. types: kinds of job: what soldiers do, the kind of win.
- cast: the people.[[notrade]] One with no trade gets one that fits the seed.[[/notrade]] Use them all as the seed's people; add nobody else who matters unless the seed mentions them. Name only those given a name; the rest by label.[[memory]] memory, where: their past with the company, where they are now; build on both.[[/memory]]

Write:
- title: a few plain words.
- question: one sentence starting "Nobody knows": something hidden the player most wants to know, never which way it ends. answer: who or what, and why, in one or two sentences: surprising, not in the seed, no new person.
- cast: [[personal]]except the soldier, [[/personal]]a label: two or three plain words a stranger sees, ending in their trade, never a name.

Job, why, edge and settles fit every way in ending. lose: what [[!personal]]the one who asked[[/!personal]][[personal]]the soldier[[/personal]] loses for good if the company fails.
- options: for each way in ending, what the soldiers do, verb first.

A story that works:
- One person wants one thing badly, and someone stands in the way, each for a plain human reason.
- Each job is at one place and follows the last win; the showdown works even if earlier jobs failed.

Plain words, third person; no numbers, nothing modern, no pay. The company never met anyone in cast[[personal]] but its own soldier[[/personal]][[memory]][[personal]] and[[/personal]][[!personal]] but[[/!personal]] the one with a memory[[/memory]]; only the company lives at its fort.
