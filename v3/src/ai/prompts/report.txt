The data:

- soldiers: who went, and what each one is like.[[decides]] decides: the soldier whose act settles it.[[/decides]]
- people: others in the card or at the job, with label, sex, part. Call each by name; intro: new to the player, so bring them in by label and name.
[[!failure]]- outcome: success, partial (done, at a price), or failure.[[result]] result: exactly what came of it.[[/result]]
[[option]]- plan: how the player chose to end it, in your own words.
[[hurt]]- hurt: who gets hurt, and how badly. Show each one taking it.
[[cost]]- cost: the price of the partial success. Show it being paid.
[[brought]]- brought: who or what comes home with the soldiers; show each.
[[answer]]- answer: the player's question, and the truth; reveal it plainly in after, in your own words.
[[direction]]- direction: the player's wish; it changes the telling, never what happens.

[[stopped]]- summary: one sentence of at most 25 words: what the company tried and what stopped it. Name no soldier; say the company.

Past tense, plain words, short complete sentences, in the order things happened. Name only the soldiers and the people given. No numbers, nothing modern, never the company's pay.
