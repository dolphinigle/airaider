# BLIND CARD BATCH 3

Quest cards from a dark-fantasy mercenary game, produced by different generators and shuffled.
You do not know which is which and must not guess.

## Card 1
**Deserter husband**

A woman knocked at your gate this morning. She asks you to find her deserter husband; his tools are gone from the shed.

`JOB:` Find a missing husband who left his home.

## Card 2
**Livestock on the old elf road**

A miller drove stock onto the old elf road. A churchwoman begs, "Keep them — they draw the trades," and the livestock are loose by the churchyard.

`JOB:` Investigate why livestock keep appearing on the old elf road.

## Card 3
**Thornhollow salvage claim**

A wanderer arrives at your gate with a splinted man. They ask escort to Thornhollow for salvage—smoke rises from the winter huts.

`JOB:` Escort a claimant to Thornhollow to press a salvage claim.

## Card 4
**chapel parcel**

A carter carelessly left a parcel at the chapel. They want escort to Peatbourne through the ridgeline; from your walls an owl watches the chapel.

`JOB:` Provide an escort for a parcel from the chapel to Peatbourne through the ridgeline.

## Card 5
**Old-growth Hive**

A charcoal-burner pried open a wild beehive in the old-growth and hauled out its comb. They want you to take whatever remains before it becomes a millstone for their camps; a collapsed beehive lies at the trailhead.

`JOB:` Recover the remaining comb and brood from the smashed hive at the charcoal-burners' camps.

## Card 6
**Moved stone at Brammere**

A sergeant moved a boundary stone at Brammere. Neighbours demand the line be settled and fear betrayal; the stone has been moved recently and the grass under it is still green.

`JOB:` Settle the disputed boundary around the moved stone at Brammere.

## Card 7
**Beekeeper's Homage**

A woman carried a small coffin into the beekeepers' glade west of the fort. She asks safe escort home to Sedgefen—of course she calls it homage; a hut hung with scorched wreaths stands at the gate.

`JOB:` Provide safe passage for a woman and a small coffin through the Western Forests to Sedgefen.

## Card 8
**sergeant's chart**

A sergeant limped to your gate and dropped a torn chart into your hands. His company lay scattered from a betrayal and he wants someone to chase the source of the leak; a thin dog sits at his feet.

`JOB:` Pursue the informant who betrayed his company and recover whatever they stole or took from the chart.

## Card 9
**Debt at the Ford**

A shipmaster left a bound man at the ford after handing a promise and a name to your visitor. He asks you to track the companion who fled and recover the promised coin — you can see a soaked horse standing in the shallows.

`JOB:` Track the companion who fled from the ford and recover the promised coin.

## Card 10
**sealed letter**

A woman who tends the beekeepers' clearings left a sealed letter at your gate — of course she hesitated before she gave it. She asks you to carry it upriver to a hut in the old glade; the seal on it is fresh but the paper is old.

`JOB:` Escort a sealed letter upriver to a hut in the old glade.

## Card 11
**missing beekeeper procession**

A beekeeper led a procession into the western wood and did not return. They ask you to find them; an overturned ladle lies, still wet.

`JOB:` Track the procession into the western wood and report who is found and whether they need aid.

## Card 12
**new thatch on one side**

A beekeeper patched a cottage eave. You are asked to watch it at night; there is new thatch on one side only.

`JOB:` Keep watch over the cottage roof through the night.

## Card 13
**wet porridge bowl**

A beekeeper came to your gate with a ladle. She wants trackers to follow a night's footprints; a porridge bowl is still wet.

`JOB:` Follow the recent footprints leading out from the beekeeper's clearing.

## Card 14
**Lamp at the Barrow**

A tanner shoulders a lamp and asks that someone carry it into the old barrow. He stops at the field gate and waits.

`JOB:` Take the lamp from the tanner at the gate and carry it into the barrow.

## Card 15
**Sealed message for the forester**

A town herald came to your gate with a sealed message. He wants it delivered to the forester and does not ask what happens after.

`JOB:` Carry a sealed message to the forester at Oakstead.

## Card 16
**Ford Shadow**

A woman brought her niece through the ford and a strange man followed them. She wants the stranger gone before nightfall; he already knows which lane.

`JOB:` Deal with the stranger who followed a woman and her niece at the ford.

## Card 17
**Iron chest from the charcoal-burners**

A carter stands at your gate with an iron chest from the charcoal-burners' camps. He wants its lock opened; the key is with a man he will not name.

`JOB:` Open the iron chest brought from the charcoal-burners' camps without using a key.

## Card 18
**Banished Duty**

A woodsman dragged the unearthed skull of an old hound from Woldgill and set it beneath your gate. They mean to force the exile's task back on someone, and you can see their demand marked by a rusted iron pommel placed on the skull.

`JOB:` Decide who will answer the banished duty bound to the hound's skull and bring them to the fort.

## Card 19
**Torn Receipt**

A chapel acolyte fled clutching a receipt. She wants escort because villagers call her thief and a torn receipt lies on the chapel steps.

`JOB:` Provide a safe escort for an acolyte to the warden-stones.

## Card 20
**False vassal at the gate**

A rider in lordly livery dismounted at the gate and pledged fealty while his horse stamped. If he is not what he seems, what do the village doors lying barred in daylight mean for the folk within?

`JOB:` Confirm the rider's identity and the truth of his oath.

## Card 21
**guided poaching confession**

A tenant farmer confessed guiding poachers in the western woods. He asks you to stop them; you see a fawn in his cloak.

`JOB:` Drive off the band hunting fawns and take their traps.

## Card 22
**abandoned mill**

A miller slipped a folded proposal through the gate and waited. "Find why the mill emptied and beasts stray to the churchyard," he says; loose livestock block the old elf road near the churchyard.

`JOB:` Investigate why the mill was abandoned and why livestock are straying from the old elf road toward the churchyard.

## Card 23
**Loose well cap**

A banished woodsman heaved a rough slab across the old well in the Western Forests. They want it sealed for good; the rope has been cut off short.

`JOB:` Seal and secure the old well in the Western Forests.

## Card 24
**Bound woman from the Western Forests**

A warden limped to the gate carrying a bound woman. The warden says the forest claims her for a grievance; her lips are violet-streaked.

`JOB:` Keep the bound woman in the gatehouse against the forest claimants.

## Card 25
**silver trinket at the ford**

A foster-mother overturned a cart at the river ford and a locked trunk sank into the shallows. She wants her niece's small silver trinket recovered from the wreck— a silver pendant is caught on a reed in the ford.

`JOB:` Recover the child's silver pendant from the river shallows at the ford.

## Card 26
**Deserter's note**

A deserter stashed a folded note in a warden-stone's hollow. They want to know who sheltered him or betrayed him; the paper's ink is frozen.

`JOB:` Find who sheltered or betrayed the deserter and report what they learn.

## Card 27
**Ringed squire at the gate**

A man in a squire's livery rode to the gate and had the village doors barred in daylight. He swears fealty to Thornhollow and asks sanctuary — can his oath be trusted with a ring on the horse's rein

`JOB:` Decide whether to admit and shelter the man claiming fealty to Thornhollow

## Card 28
**Ford Promise**

A shipmaster rode to the river ford and nailed a sworn note to the crossing post. You are asked to find the man named in that promise; a frayed sling hangs from the post.

`JOB:` Locate the man named in the shipmaster's sworn note at the river ford.

## Card 29
**Cellar Antlers**

A tenant farmer at your gate confesses to guiding poachers into the old-growth. He says an advisor took the kill; the cellar door bears antlers.

`JOB:` Recover the kill the farmer says an advisor took.

## Card 30
**Thornhollow**

A steward barred the doors. From your walls they want the ruin cleared and to be seen paying; who will take his coin when he counts the fee out twice and still gets it wrong?

`JOB:` Clear the ruin and accept the patron's payment in view.

## Card 31
**stolen pack from the charcoal-burners' camp**

A poacher fled the charcoal-burners' camp after stealing a pack. The burners ask you to trace him and recover the pack, and an owl sits on a low branch.

`JOB:` Track the poacher who stole a pack from the charcoal-burners and bring the pack back.

## Card 32
**Mark the calves at your gate**

A woman who keeps the ford led a young carter and three calves to your gate. They ask you to mark the calves and the marking iron is new.

`JOB:` Mark three calves with the client's iron.

## Card 33
**Stolen dowry chest**

A wedding courier ran off with a bride's dowry chest. She wants it recovered; the chest it came in is still in her room.

`JOB:` Retrieve the bride's dowry chest taken by a courier.

## Card 34
**Churchyard grave sale**

A buyer presses up from beyond the lane, saying, "Move the grave before the sale." From your walls you can see livestock loose on the old elf road and the buyer waiting in his yard.

`JOB:` Arrange for the grave at the churchyard to be moved before the sale proceeds.

## Card 35
**Niece's trinket at the ford**

A niece keeps dropping her father's carved trinket into the ford each midsummer. Her kin want it recovered and the accidents ended, and a frayed rope lies in the shallows at the river ford.

`JOB:` Retrieve the carved trinket from the river ford and put an end to the repeated accidents there.

## Card 36
**Spying at the charcoal camps**

A woodsman came back from the charcoal camps with a spade. He wants you to find who spies on the burners — an owl perches.

`JOB:` Investigate who has been watching the charcoal-burners' camps.

## Card 37
**banishment cord**

A warden took the banishment cord from a dead ranger and tied it to his belt.  The forest folk think he has claimed the dead man's duty or his exile, and a single black braid hangs at the fort gate.

`JOB:` Deal with the warden who wearing the ranger's banishment cord—determine what he means for the dead man's duty.

## Card 38
**sealed mine shaft**

A tenant farmer came to the gate and confessed he had sealed the old shaft. He asks you to reopen the mine so salvage can be taken; the last crew's tools are still down there.

`JOB:` Restore access to the abandoned shaft for salvage.

## Card 39
**Deserter at the warden-stones**

A scribe sheltered a deserter beneath the warden-stones this frozen morning. They ask sanctuary for coin, and a frosted quill lies on the moss.

`JOB:` Harbor a deserter at the warden-stones and keep him hidden.

## Card 40
**Wanderer with a fresh splint**

A wanderer limped into the logging huts from the Western Forests asking for a guide. They want an escort to Thornhollow to claim salvage rights—everyone suspects a hoax; the wanderer wears a splint newer than it should be.

`JOB:` Escort a lone claimant from the logging huts to Thornhollow.

## Card 41
**boat at the ford**

A shipmaster hired a crew to beach a boat at the ford landing. You are asked to watch the boat at the landing to see what the men ashore move, and it has been unloaded once tonight.

`JOB:` Watch the boat at the river landing.

## Card 42
**Folded Chart**

A sergeant came back to the gate and left a folded chart. His men were not with him; the sergeant avoids meeting eyes while a dog noses the chart.

`JOB:` Find out why the sergeant returned alone and what became of his patrol.

## Card 43
**funeral hut**

A young mourner carries a carved box toward a glade. They ask escort to the hut so a proper homage can be paid — of course from regret; a beehive hangs at the path's edge.

`JOB:` Provide safe passage for a mourner bearing a funerary box to the hut.

## Card 44
**Net at the gate**

A miller dragged a wet net to the fort gate. The crowd accuses a passing hunter and demands bounty paid—there is a net tied to the gatepost.

`JOB:` Produce the hunter the villagers accuse.

## Card 45
**Rider at the ford**

An oarsman chased a rider. You must stop him before the ford — he cannot say what the rider carries, only that it must not arrive.

`JOB:` Intercept the rider and prevent him crossing the ford.

## Card 46
**Beehive taken from hollow at the burners' camp**

A charcoal-burner tore a beehive from a hollow oak after your men worked that stand. They want the brood gone before whatever jinx follows, and you can see it lashed in a tarred cart, still wet.

`JOB:` Remove the beehive from the charcoal-burners' cart and take it elsewhere.

## Card 47
**open net at the gate**

A hunter pulled a net from the forest path and left the bait exposed. You have a crowd at your gate demanding the bounty be paid— they jab a finger at an open net.

`JOB:` Decide if the hunter took the pledged bounty and bring proof to the villagers.

## Card 48
**hidden woman at the forest edge**

A town official dragged a sobbing woman into the Western Forests. They ask you to fetch her; a hound sits at your gate.

`JOB:` Bring back the woman the official took into the Western Forests.
