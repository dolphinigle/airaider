# BLIND CARD BATCH 2 — score each card independently

Quest cards from a dark-fantasy mercenary game, from several generators, shuffled. You do not
know which is which and must not guess.

## Card 1
**One-Sided Thatch**

A young beekeeper came to your gate. They want whoever has been watching the old lodge's roof at night found; there is new thatch on one side only.

`JOB:` Find who has been watching the lodge's roof at night.

## Card 2
**stale smoke**

Stale smoke curls from an unburned charcoal heap at the northern camp, two nights old. The charcoal-burner bears it and cannot tend his kilns nor send out wood crews. He wants the camps cleared, and will pay coin and grant salvage rights when done. The forester who left last week branded a stick with the heap's mark.

`JOB:` Clear the unburned charcoal heap at the northern camp.

## Card 3
**Overturned kiln-lids at Harrowhollow burns**

A row of kiln-lids lies overturned beside the burns, knocked askew three nights past. A charcoal-burner is left bearing the loss and cannot cart his kiln-goods to market while they wait. He wants the lids shown pushed by hands, and he will pay coin and salvage rights when that is proved. The charcoal-burner set his apprentice's small leather boot upon one overturned lid before he vanished.

`JOB:` Go to Harrowhollow burns, inspect the overturned kiln-lids, and report who pushed them.

## Card 4
**Boat at the Ford**

A shipmaster ties a small boat at the river ford. He asks that it be watched through the night because it has been unloaded once tonight.

`JOB:` Keep watch over a small boat at the river ford through the night.

## Card 5
**Elder-path spoor**

Heavy, wide pawprints cross the elder path, and they are two nights old. The grove-warden bears that trouble and it costs him his nightly rounds of root-tending. He wants the quarry taken alive and will pay coin and salvage rights. A woodsman has tied his tame ferret to the trail-post.

`JOB:` Capture alive the beast that left the pawprints on the elder path.

## Card 6
**Spindle-tied tuft at Hawfen copse**

A tuft of dark fur, knotted with spindle thread, is fixed to the Hawfen copse gate and has been there since yesterday. The copse warden stands with it, and keeping watch at that gate keeps him from bringing out the foraged herb-baskets he sells. He wants the creature that left the tuft found or driven off, and he will pay coin. An oarsman has tied his spare spindle to the tuft.

`JOB:` Send a small party to track the thing that left the tuft at Hawfen copse and drive it off.

## Card 7
**split warden-stone**

A warden-stone lies split on the lower ridgeline, unmended for three nights. The forest warden bears the duty, and while it lasts he cannot mark trails. He wants the stone set and the ridge pass usable again, and will pay coin. The warden slid his guide's badge beneath the crack.

`JOB:` Send a small guard to the lower ridgeline to set and secure the broken warden-stone so the ridge pass can reopen.

## Card 8
**A lock opened without the key**

A charcoal-burner opened a lord's lock without the key. The caller wants that done for a sealed chest and says the key is with a man who keeps to himself.

`JOB:` Open a sealed chest's lock without using its key.

## Card 9
**booted corpse in the ford**

A corpse face-down in the river ford has lain two nights. A ford-keeper bears it and cannot tend the crossing, so trade stalls. He wants the body gone and proof of cause, and will pay coin and first claim to anything pulled from the river. A ferryman lashed the corpse's boots to his skiff at dawn.

`JOB:` Retrieve the booted corpse from the river ford for the ford-keeper's inspection.

## Card 10
**Intercepted grievance**

A town official stopped a rider bearing a grievance. They demand the message go on unanswered; he does not ask what happens after.

`JOB:` Carry the grievance onward to its intended recipients.

## Card 11
**Rider at the ford**

A rider spurred toward the ford. You are told he must be stopped before it; he carries a small, humming bundle.

`JOB:` Keep a rider from crossing the ford.

## Card 12
**stacked combs**

A cart of honey lies overturned in the beekeepers' clearing, two nights old. A beekeeper cannot tend his hives while he watches the wreck, and stacked combs sour. He wants the cart righted and the honey saved, and will hand over salvage rights and coin. The beekeeper has driven a carved homage token into the broken wheel.

`JOB:` Escort the beekeeper and his overturned cart from the beekeepers' clearing to the fort.

## Card 13
**Rider at the Ford**

A woodsman stopped a rider before the ford. He wants the rider kept from crossing, because he cannot say what the rider carries, only that it must not arrive.

`JOB:` Detain a rider at the ford before they cross.

## Card 14
**stranger followed home**

A woman followed a stranger home from the ford into the Western Forests. She asks that he be kept away from her niece's foster cottage; he already knows which lane.

`JOB:` Keep a stranger from reaching a foster cottage.

## Card 15
**husband missing from the ridgeline**

A woman reports her husband missing from the warden-stones ridgeline. She wants his whereabouts known and she has already moved his tools out of the shed.

`JOB:` Locate the man who did not return from the warden-stones ridgeline.

## Card 16
**Capped Well**

A hunter capped the village well to keep something down, and the people nearby fear the banishment may have failed, because the rope has been cut off short.

`JOB:` Examine the capped well and report what was inside.

## Card 17
**Boundary stone shifted**

A sergeant moved a boundary stone, because the grass under it is still green. Neighbors now press for the line to be settled before new tempers turn old claims to blows.

`JOB:` Settle the disputed boundary

## Card 18
**Moved Tools**

A woman asks that her husband be found. He is suspected to have deserted into the warden-stones on the ridgeline, and she has already moved his tools out of the shed.

`JOB:` Locate a missing man last seen near the warden-stones and report his fate.

## Card 19
**Anvil in the Hearth**

An iron anvil sits on a logger's hearth and has lain there since last dusk. The hut-foreman stands by it and cannot send his crew to haul winter timber. He wants the anvil gone and the hearth returned, and offers salvage rights with coin. The hut-foreman has hung a child's woolen mitten upon the anvil.

`JOB:` Escort the hut-foreman and his anvil out of the winter huts and deliver them to the fort's smith.

## Card 20
**barred doors at Thornhollow**

A bailiff barred Thornhollow's doors in daylight. A steward insists the ruin be cleared and to be seen paying for it, and he counts the fee out twice and still gets it wrong.

`JOB:` Clear the ruined halls of Thornhollow and render proof of payment to the steward.

## Card 21
**Sealed Letter**

A courier left a sealed letter at your gate. It must go upriver to the beekeepers' clearing; the seal is fresh but the paper is old.

`JOB:` Carry a sealed letter upriver to the beekeepers' clearing.

## Card 22
**A white hunter's ribbon**

A white hunter's ribbon on an elm at Elmholt has hung for several nights. The sergeant tends that elm and so leaves the road unguided and the carts sitting. He wants the ribbon's meaning found and quieted, and offers coin. The sergeant braided a fresh sprig into the ribbon.

`JOB:` Follow the ribbon at Elmholt; discover who tied it and why.

## Card 23
**disturbed grave**

A gravedigger lifts a coffin from the family plot. You can see from the walls that they want the grave moved before the sale; the buyer is waiting in his yard.

`JOB:` Arrange for the old grave to be removed before the property's sale.

## Card 24
**Ward-stone ring**

A footpath at the warden-stones lies trampled and untended for three nights. A ridge warden carries it, and it keeps him from his patrols and pay. He wants the path clear and will reward the company in coin. The ridge warden unhooked a ward-stone's iron ring and walked off.

`JOB:` Investigate why the warden-stones' path is trampled and bring back who is responsible.

## Card 25
**Thornhollow salvage**

A wet-haired broker lays a pouch of coin on the gate bench below your walls. He wants Thornhollow cleared and to be seen paying for it; he counts the fee twice and still gets it wrong.

`JOB:` Clear the ruin at Thornhollow and make the clearing visible to the man at the gate.

## Card 26
**Calves to Mark**

A woman from the winter huts brings calves to your gate. She wants them marked and led home; the marking iron is new.

`JOB:` Escort the party needed to mark the calves and return them to the winter huts.

## Card 27
**Hastily capped well**

A woodsman uncovered a hastily capped village well while you were searching the western forest. The locals want to know who sealed it and why; the rope has been cut off short.

`JOB:` Investigate the sealed well at the search site.

## Card 28
**Stolen dowry chest**

A passing trader took a bride's dowry chest on the road. She wants her dowry recovered, and you can see the chest it came in still in her room.

`JOB:` Retrieve a missing bride's dowry chest and return it to her.

## Card 29
**The ferryman's bell**

Overturned punts and a child's shawl lie in the ford's shallows where they have lain since the last market. The ferryman cannot ply the line and loses his fares while boats cannot pass. He wants the ford clear and the shawl returned, and he will grant salvage rights and coin when it is done. Before he left, the ferryman hooked his service bell to the largest punt.

`JOB:` Clear the ford's shallows so boats may pass again.

## Card 30
**Tenant's confession**

A tenant farmer confessed at your gate this morning that he tunneled into an old mine to keep his children from starving. He asks you to reopen the shaft so the last crew's tools can be brought up, and the tools are still down there.

`JOB:` Reopen the old mine so it can be worked again.

## Card 31
**Stolen Dowry**

A courier was robbed through carelessness while carrying a young bride's dowry. She wants it recovered because the chest it came in is still in her room.

`JOB:` Recover the missing dowry from the road.

## Card 32
**Branded calves**

A drover branded calves at the edge of the loggers' winter huts. He demands they be taken under his claim, and his hand shows a marking iron that is new.

`JOB:` Escort the drover's calves to his claim at the winter huts.

## Card 33
**Ridgewarden's pulley**

Thornhollow's outer doors hang barred in daylight since the last moon. A ridgewarden bears the cost: he has left the ridgeline, so cannot tend the warden-stones. He wants the doors opened and will pay with salvage and coin. The ridgewarden has unhooked the ruin's iron pulley and carried it away.

`JOB:` Set Thornhollow's outer doors open at daylight so they stand unbarred.

## Card 34
**Violet Warning**

A town messenger thrusts a folded warning at the fort gate and rides away before anyone can answer. The chapel's keepers fear blasphemy and demand explanation, and the messenger does not ask what happens after.

`JOB:` Deliver the folded warning to the chapel keepers.

## Card 35
**moved boundary stone**

A sergeant from Elmholt moved the boundary stone at dawn. From your walls it puts the border in question and demands a settled line; the grass under the stone is still green.

`JOB:` Confirm and settle the boundary at the moved stone.

## Card 36
**Crude snares in Yarbourne's game-thicket**

A ring of crude snares crouches along Yarbourne's game-thicket, set last night. The tenant farmer at Coldworth spends daylight tending ruined traps and losing pelts. He will have the snares removed and offers first pick of salvage and coin. The forest-warden cut a snare's rope and left the short length knotted on a stump.

`JOB:` Send men to sweep Yarbourne's game-thicket and destroy the snares.

## Card 37
**Barrow Lamp**

A carter brought a lamp toward the old barrow. Those who paid want it carried inside; he stops at the field gate and waits.

`JOB:` Escort a hired lamp to the old barrow.

## Card 38
**burnt votive cloths at Oakstead shrine**

A pile of burnt votive cloths lies before Oakstead's stone shrine and has lain since dawn. The grove-keeper tends it and so cannot prune the sapling beds or guide morning mourners. He will pay coin when the shrine is cleared and the burned offerings taken away. A woodwright has lashed the shrine's carved post to his cart.

`JOB:` Clear the shrine at Oakstead, remove the burned offerings, and bring the place back to order for the grove-keeper.

## Card 39
**moved grave**

A gravedigger cut an old burial from the churchyard and set the coffin upon the old elf road. Villagers fear the seller rushed the sale and hint at abandonment of the plot, because the buyer is waiting in his yard.

`JOB:` Confirm that the churchyard grave was moved before the plot's sale and report who ordered it.

## Card 40
**Roof Vigil**

A woman kept vigil on the longhouse roof each night. The neighbours want someone to learn what she fears and why, because there is new thatch on one side only.

`JOB:` Learn why a woman keeps vigil on the longhouse roof.

## Card 41
**boat at the ford**

A shipmaster landed a small boat at the river ford tonight. They want you to watch that boat because nobody can vouch for its cargo, and it has been unloaded once tonight.

`JOB:` Keep eyes on the boat at the river ford through the night.

## Card 42
**Homage Letter**

A courier brings a letter for delivery upriver through the Western Forests. It must reach an old hut where homage is expected, because the seal on it is fresh but the paper is old.

`JOB:` Carry a sealed letter upriver to the hut in the old elven wood.

## Card 43
**bright ribbon on the churchyard gate**

Livestock roam the old elf road by the churchyard since the last moon. The shepherd stands at that gate and cannot drive his flocks to market, leaving pens and pastures untended. He wants the road cleared and every beast returned to its pen, and he will pay in coin. The forester has tied a bright ribbon to the churchyard gate and left.

`JOB:` Recover the loose livestock from the old elf road and return them to the shepherd.

## Card 44
**lamp to the barrow**

A man carries a lamp to the barrow. A barrow-keeper asks it be set inside, and his godchild watches the lane because he stops at the field gate and waits.

`JOB:` Convey a light to the burial mound.

## Card 45
**Smashed hives at Ravenbourne**

A row of smashed hives sits in the southern beekeeper clearing of Ravenbourne, two nights old. The beekeeper cannot tend his other hives and watches his honey spoil for lack of hands. He wants the clearing quiet and the hives tended again, and he will pay a pouch of coin. The beekeeper has been ladling a bitter draught over the broken combs.

`JOB:` Find what felled the hives in the Ravenbourne clearing and whether it will return.

## Card 46
**Niece Followed from the Ford**

A stranger followed a niece from the ford, jingling a brass trinket that made her laugh and run. Her keeper fears he came for the trinket and dreads what he will do, because he already knows which lane.

`JOB:` Stop the stranger from reaching the household.

## Card 47
**Withheld key**

A foreman pried open the charcoal-burners' storeroom lock after a delivery went missing. Now the burners want to know who else can open it, because the key is with a man he will not name.

`JOB:` Open the storeroom lock despite the key being withheld.

## Card 48
**Old Mine Reopened**

A tenant farmer confessed that he helped a handful of men pry open the old mine and reopen its mouth. Neighbors fear whoever reopened it will strip the workings for salvage, and the last crew's tools are still down there.

`JOB:` Find who reopened the old mine.
