# 30 quest cards

## 1
Jalila enthusiastically advertises while Nabhani keeps accounts beside her, counting money - can't shortchange my good friend's hard-earned coin!

## 2
A charcoal-burner from Bircombe dumps damp logs at the gate and demands you search. He says which end to start.

## 3
In a hidden corner of the city, a silent death duel took place. All you know of it is what the survivor later told you.

## 4
The Destroyer rages, their warriors appearing overhead - angels skilled in torture prepare to preemptively slaughter all who still defy.

## 5
The moment the Sultan appears, you regret your decision - he's accompanied by a well-equipped Royal Guard, clearly not just for his protection...

## 6
You quietly push open the door to Zaki's house in the drizzle. The dim light reveals ropes and whips, with the scent of essential oils in the air. A pale, emaciated woman lies twisted on the shiny silk carpet, lifeless. You must dispose of her before dawn.

## 7
A small gravestone lies half-buried by the old elf road. He stands where he cannot see the road. Your company will need to count the tolls for a day.

## 8
A fresh gravestone sits half sunk beside your old elf road. This is rivalry, the tollkeeper gnaws his nails and will not stop counting. He stands where he cannot see the road.

## 9
Send your rider and steed to compete, place bets. If victorious, you may receive a reward. Failure means losing everything.

## 10
An oarsman lays a damp packet on your table and presses papers that say the grave must be moved before the sale. The buyer waits in his yard.

## 11
You found a lamp burning on the low stone at the Old Beekeeper's Crossing outside Dewcroft. The lamp waits to be kept burning. He pays for a month at once and gives no name.

## 12
Yesterday, you presented a Bloodshed Card in court. Today is the day you and your opponent must settle this fight to the death.

## 13
The parish undertaker finishes clearing the vault and leaves a candlelit lantern at the lychgate for your watch. His own man has the other key.

## 14
A company search turns up a toppled barrow lid in Woldgill. The hunt-master stands at the field edge, ashamed and stubborn. he stops at the field gate and waits. He needs a lamp carried to the barrow and your company bears that.

## 15
A gravedigger in your charge lights a candle and sells a looking away. His own man has the other key.

## 16
The Sultan's blade cleaves through the air straight for your face. Yet in that moment before death descends, you catch something in the cruel monarch's eyes – an undertow of regret, perhaps even... compassion...

## 17
The unusual evil atmosphere in the Haunted Mansion is disturbing. Perhaps you can seek help from the Purist Order.

## 18
You've gathered the empire's finest minds - or at least the cleverest you could find - to create a convincing counterfeit of the Sultan's personal sheath.

## 19
The maid has given you a lead. You begin your investigation. First, you must trace the path the tax collector’s wife and child took on their journey home. Where did they go? Where did they vanish? You decide to send your people to walk the same road, to conduct a field investigation.

## 20
Abdul covets the wealth Fatuna inherited. He sends you to take what was never yours to claim – by whatever filthy means necessary.

## 21
You decide to spare Murtaz. He is nothing more than a man driven mad by the Sultan's cruelties. You will try to dissuade him from speaking so openly of his schemes – it is not wise, and you will have something to satisfy the Vizier.

## 22
A charcoal-burner comes in with black palms and sets down the chest he hauled from Elmgill. Recovery of her dowry remains unmade and waits on you. The chest it came in is still in her room.

## 23
A beekeeper from Sedgefen brings a petition already addressed to your company at the fort. He cannot say what the rider carries, only that it must not arrive.

## 24
A ferryman arrives at the gate to leave the guarding of the Coldmoss bridge with you. He has already had a toll-board painted.

## 25
A lamp stands trimmed at the old crossing on your road. A beekeeper drums a carved staff and paces with sharp impatience. he pays for a month at once and gives no name

## 26
While searching the ridgeline you find the wardens' boundary stone turned and the forest warden sits beside it with a closed ledger in their lap and stares out over the trees. The stone has been moved recently and settling the boundary waits on you.

## 27
To cover Jalila, you claimed the noble's murder at the House of Delights. Naturally, his family comes for revenge.

## 28
A ferryman from the loggers' winter huts brings a word to the gate that the boat at Hawholt landing waits for your watch. It has been unloaded once tonight.

## 29
Various clues lead you to a magnificent mansion in the noble district. Security is clearly more stringent than at the House of Delights - you even spot fully armed assassins in many hidden corners. Riel crouches beside you, reluctantly admitting her people probably can't defeat so many guards, and you'll need a more detailed plan.

## 30
A fresh pit gapes at the river ford where the shallows used to lie. The spoil heap beside it is smaller than the hole. The ford-keeper brings the matter to the fort and names you responsible for filling the pit before the rains. The bank-side folk have set token candles and stand watch as if the hole were a newly opened chapel.
