# 48 quest cards

## 1
Merek the shipmaster slips a name from the ledger into his pocket. You can see the ledger clearly.

## 2
Rell the sergeant pries Elmholt's mouth back open to send miners. Rell pins a chart accusing you to cover a theft. The last crew's picks condemn Rell.

## 3
Merrin sexton winds a spindle round a coffin and drags it to the lane. A buyer waits in his yard.

## 4
Doran the tenant farmer confessed, in front of the morning callers, to opening the Council's cedar chest. He blames an advisor and the key is with a man he will not name.

## 5
You have awakened this ancient being from its long slumber, shaped it in form. Now it demands something deeper... something of the soul.

## 6
Mara the weaver gave her dowry to her brother before the marriage. She rails at kin and names him a thief. Her brother leaves the chest it came in in her room.

## 7
Galen the gravedigger has exhumed Edda and tied her with a spindle. The buyer waits in his yard.

## 8
Tobin sexton has bolted the churchyard gate and set a candlelit lantern on the stile. He hired you to watch and his own man holds the other key.

## 9
Bram the ferryman hauls a small crate ashore and begs you to watch it. Someone has unloaded it tonight.

## 10
Maris the beekeeper carries a message and hides the messenger beneath her apron and does not ask what happens after. Her ladle rests in her palm.

## 11
Beren the ferryman straps an old sheet into a new wax seal and pushes upriver. He bars his Millwell door in daylight and the seal on the letter is fresh but the paper is old. Someone upriver will pry it.

## 12
Maren the charcoal-burner chased a hound from the path, fastened a crude collar to it and claimed it before you, though the hound belongs to someone else. The collar hangs from a post.

## 13
Merrin the sacristan has bolted the churchyard gate and promised to look away. His own man has the other key.

## 14
Marin the beekeeper has rallied the clearing folk and driven stakes across the glade road. He cannot name the rider's cargo.

## 15
Lysa, a brewer, has accused the charcoal-burners of stealing her dowry. The chest it came in is still in her room and she has ridden to their camps. Her brother has set a millstone at the fort's gate for you.

## 16
Send your rider and steed to compete, place bets. If victorious, you may receive a reward. Failure means losing everything.

## 17
Edrin the sexton hired a candlelit watch and finds his own man holds the other key. The church gate is left ajar.

## 18
Talen the shipmaster tore a name from a ledger to keep it off a list. He carries the ledger on his person.

## 19
Marin the beekeeper carries a sealed message into the old-growth and hides a child. Marin leaves the child under overturned frames and does not ask what happens after.

## 20
Jonas, charcoal-burner, has taken a hound and held it from the hunt. You find the hound wears another man's brand.

## 21
Gideon the boatman lashed an anvil to the skiff at Hawbarrow and vowed to watch it. The cargo has been unloaded tonight.

## 22
Merrin the gravedigger sank a pit and called it a baptism. Merrin's spoil heap is smaller than the hole. Who will carry the missing earth?

## 23
Though the venue is filthy and vulgar, many nobles come to watch the show. If you are madness or foolish enough, you can even participate.

## 24
Mara the bride seized a merchant's collar and accused him of stealing her dowry. She points at you now. Mara keeps the dowry chest in her room.

## 25
Marek the beekeeper chases a rider out of the beekeepers' clearing. He cannot say what the rider carries and the rider rides for the ford.

## 26
Maris the ferryman ties a sealed letter to his pole and pushes upriver. He bars his riverside door in daylight and tells anyone the packet is a favor of fealty while his thumb presses paper that smells of old ink. Someone will break the wax.

## 27
Bram the tenant farmer has broken the larder lock and confessed the key lost. You saw key in advisor's pouch.

## 28
This Freeman Hunting Band operate from a riverside outpost. As you approach, they're unloading weapon crates from moored vessels - steel will decide this day.

## 29
Doran, a minewright, has come and demanded the shaft be reopened. He has pressed a stained chart that marks where his crew stopped. You will have to haul the tools up.

## 30
Corin the shipmaster tore a namesake free and bragged to prove you wrong. He carries the ledger still.

## 31
"This young pup came back to me, still set on being a thief... but that's wrong. So, my Lord, help me set him straight until he goes back to the mill."

## 32
You need to occupy the slums to block the harbor; you need to occupy the lower city, for half of the capital's grain and tools are stored here... You also need to occupy the upper city, or you won't be able to control access to the palace.

## 33
Travelling merchants tell tales of a rare beast known as the crocodile living in remote forest rivers. Now, due to a rich man's mistake, a crocodile hatchling has slipped into the sewer. No one knows if it’s still alive.

## 34
Gide the gravedigger has cut a wide pit for a hurried burial. Gide has gone to the neighbor bearing a handful of earth as pledge. Smaller than the hole?

## 35
Of course, the ring doesn't kill you – how could it? If this ends everything, the ruler's expectations and desires remain unfulfilled. The Sultan nods at you silently, as if in approval, then waves his hand. Those still loyal draw their swords, charging at your group like a torrent... And so, the next act begins.

## 36
Rohen beekeeper warns you that the rider must not pass the beekeepers' glade. He cannot say what it bears.

## 37
Edran the beekeeper presses a folded note into a ladle and hands it to a rider. He does not ask what happens after.

## 38
Rook the gravedigger dug a pit to bury what the neighbors dragged from the chapel. Rook lashed planks across the mouth and swore no one would find it. Will you fill the pit before the rains lift those planks, when the spoil heap beside it is smaller than the hole?

## 39
Badriyyah's summoned Cultic God has emerged... but power... They crave more power... Evil creatures unleashed upon the city desperately hunger for blood, for flesh...

## 40
Gareth the sergeant cleared the mouth and announced the mine open. He filed a chart and ordered descents, while the last crew left their tools below. You must haul the tools.

## 41
Abdul covets the wealth Fatuna inherited. He sends you to take what was never yours to claim – by whatever filthy means necessary.

## 42
Mira the woodwarden has stopped hunters by laying claim to the hound for you. The hound belongs to a smuggler.

## 43
Ivar, boatman, fastens a letter to the pulley and sends it upriver to keep his oath. He bars the landing in daylight and points at the scorched sigils on the walls as proof. The seal on the letter is fresh though the paper is old and the parcel drifts toward Thornhollow.

## 44
Rian the gravedigger drags a family coffin into the buyer's yard to stop the sale. She leaves the coffin across his gate and the buyer waits in his yard.

## 45
To escape the Sultan's rule, you must first break out of his walls, but to end his rule, you must fight your way back in... alive.

## 46
Lysa the tenant farmer broke the larder lock to hide a poached stag. The key is with a man she will not name.

## 47
You behold countless volumes, each one containing the destiny of a mortal life... Yet you can reach only the nearest few, for they have just been rewritten by the compiler and placed where your fate touches them.

## 48
Haw the ferryman nails an anvil to the boat and sits watching the landing. The boat has been unloaded tonight.
