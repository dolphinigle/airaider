# 48 quest cards

## 1
Marin the beekeeper carries a sealed message into the old-growth and hides a child. Marin leaves the child under overturned frames and does not ask what happens after.

## 2
Marten the exile has pried a sword pommel from a stranger's hand. He ties the pommel to his belt and stops at the field gate to wait. Will you carry his lamp through the gate?

## 3
Harlan the oarsman carries a sealed message and leaves a spindle as its proof. He does not ask what happens after.

## 4
Doran, a minewright, has come and demanded the shaft be reopened. He has pressed a stained chart that marks where his crew stopped. You will have to haul the tools up.

## 5
Talen the shipmaster tore a name from a ledger to keep it off a list. He carries the ledger on his person.

## 6
Daran the carpenter pries the kiln woodpile apart to find a gilt ribbon his godchild lost. Old Thom tells him which end to start and the ribbon hangs from Daran's tool belt.

## 7
Rook the gravedigger dug a pit to bury what the neighbors dragged from the chapel. Rook lashed planks across the mouth and swore no one would find it. Will you fill the pit before the rains lift those planks, when the spoil heap beside it is smaller than the hole?

## 8
As the cups are raised and laughter flows, you find yourself speaking with Hassan about his little lamb... When did it begin following him? How old could it be? Yet the moment the questions form, a strange presence seems to nudge you away, pushing those thoughts back into the mist where they belong.

## 9
The smell of the wound, the sounds within your body, the color of the vomit... all tell you that the footsteps of death are near.

## 10
Alim wants to slice up that beggar leader with his knife – considering what the man did, he deserves it; but you might handle it better, like finding out where Hemir is... What's keeping a ruthless trafficker from talking anyway?

## 11
The guesthouse overflows with people! Many who never contributed labor joined the food queue, desperately hoping to taste Habib's renowned cooking. Quickly identified by legitimately hungry workers, arguments erupted and escalated toward violence!

## 12
Anselm the bailiff caught a stranger trailing him home and shoved a violet sash into the man's hands. He left the man tied to the gate you came through and he already knows which lane.

## 13
Merrin the warden has hauled a hunting hound from the line and lashed it to her saddle pommel. The dog wears a collar stamped with Talon's banishment brand and shows the hound belongs to another. Will you put a hand to that leather and cut it loose?

## 14
Marek the beekeeper chases a rider out of the beekeepers' clearing. He cannot say what the rider carries and the rider rides for the ford.

## 15
Purist followers hunt cultists throughout the city again - their sacred privilege. Whenever they identify violators of the Purist's faith, they may arrest and punish them according to their laws. Badriyyah and her cultists have scurried like rats through countless close escapes. This time, they've tired of hiding and prepare a ritual to teach the priests a lesson...

## 16
Jawad comes to you in a rage, demanding a duel. He claims you insulted his daughter. Generally, one will be furious at times like this and say 'I will kill you myself,' but Jawad hires the most expensive gladiator from the Dark Alley – perhaps there's still room for negotiation.

## 17
Maren the scribe slid the ridge warden-stone to shrink her lord's claim. You found the stone moved and the grass beneath it still green.

## 18
Of course, the ring doesn't kill you – how could it? If this ends everything, the ruler's expectations and desires remain unfulfilled. The Sultan nods at you silently, as if in approval, then waves his hand. Those still loyal draw their swords, charging at your group like a torrent... And so, the next act begins.

## 19
High Constable Adil is searching for your evidence... You need to do something, or he might really put you on trial – when obviously when it’s all clearly the Sultan’s fault!

## 20
Lysa, a brewer, has accused the charcoal-burners of stealing her dowry. The chest it came in is still in her room and she has ridden to their camps. Her brother has set a millstone at the fort's gate for you.

## 21
Maren the beekeeper has nailed her hut door shut and called the gladefolk to meet. An elder cannot say what the rider carries and the gladefolk wait armed on the track.

## 22
Tilda, a ferryman, lashes a boat to the landing and hammers its hatch shut. She drives an iron anvil into the keel to prove the craft holds. They unload the boat tonight. You find the hatch unlatched.

## 23
Yesterday, you presented a Bloodshed Card in court. Today is the day you and your opponent must settle this fight to the death.

## 24
Bram the toller has wedged a carved staff and begun to mark the passing carts. He stands where he cannot see the road. The ledger lies open beneath the staff for you to finish.

## 25
Menar’s talents have caught the Sultan’s attention. She petitions him for permission to explore beyond the great Sea of Sand – to seek a legendary stronghold hidden in distant peaks. A journey of great peril. This time, she does not come to you for aid.

## 26
Bram the tollkeeper pried an old elven gravestone free. He wedged it across the old road and carved every passer's name. He cannot see the road and a bruised bear comes for the stone.

## 27
Aldric sexton locked the churchyard gate and posted watchers for the candlelit vigils. His man wears the other key on a leather thong at his belt.

## 28
Here is the prison for nobles and serious criminals. Unlike what the common people might imagine, there are no gold toilets or velvet quilts here. What you have is darkness, dampness, and ignored rats. The only unusual thing might be that, due to family bribes, the prisoners' meals aren't very bad.

## 29
Marlow, sexton, pried a coffin from its nails and carried the boards. He has hollowed the lot for the estate sale and the hollow gapes while the buyer waits in his yard.

## 30
You decide to spare Murtaz. He is nothing more than a man driven mad by the Sultan's cruelties. You will try to dissuade him from speaking so openly of his schemes – it is not wise, and you will have something to satisfy the Vizier.

## 31
Yori, the sexton, chased livestock off the road by the churchyard. Yori nailed boards along the eaves and asked you to watch the roof at night. One side of the roof bears new thatch only and someone will test the eaves tonight.

## 32
Alden, a warden, takes a well the dead left and drives a wooden cap into its throat. He has the rope cut off short and you find his folded receipt pinned beneath the cap's rim.

## 33
You hope to convince Sadani to join your regicide plot. It is better to conspire of such things in person. You decide to take the risk and sneak into the palace to meet Sadani.

## 34
Ragn the woodcutter hides his godchild's charm in the woodpile and splits it while telling you which end. A fresh notch marks the log at the end he points to.

## 35
Merrin charcoal-burner has seized a hound and shut it in the smoke-shed. The hound bears a brass rune on its ear that proves it belongs to someone else and you find it muzzled beneath the charcoal kiln.

## 36
Garron the herdsman followed a stranger home along the old elf road. He drove his loose goats into the churchyard and wedged the iron gate against flight. He already knows which lane the stranger chose and sets a lantern at its mouth.

## 37
Mara innkeeper and old friend brings a list to you and marks rats beside names. A young lad shows the traps unset after she swears the traps hold. She opens the cellar hatch.

## 38
Only a fool would do charity in a slum; scholars have found that those with property less than twenty coins possess no spirit, neither knowing gratitude nor compassion... mm... anyway, this should count as a true act of Extravagance!

## 39
Taren, mason, shifted the boundary stone and set his anvil upon its face. He left the grass under it still green for you to examine.

## 40
Alden the ferryman has rigged iron chains across the crossing and sits to watch them. He has already had a toll-board painted.

## 41
Lysa the innkeeper says her husband crossed the ford and did not come back. She has dragged his tools out of the shed and stacked them by the gate. Her niece wears his brass trinket and is already walking toward the shallows.

## 42
Merin the cooper has brought a folded list after they counted the cellar for vermin. Merin locked the traps in a chest and left the key with the warden. An inspector opened the chest and found the traps unset and the sacks already chewed.

## 43
The ritual's elegance lies in its simplicity - lure the greedy god into the vessel, block escape with darkness, counter resistance with mirrors, then slice open the vessel to release divine essence - no different from butchering livestock.

## 44
Marta herdswoman has branded the calves with a private ringmark. She has pressed a pawned trinket into a buyer's hand to seal the bargain. The marking iron is new and a steward will come to seize it.

## 45
Rian the gravedigger drags a family coffin into the buyer's yard to stop the sale. She leaves the coffin across his gate and the buyer waits in his yard.

## 46
Marek the ferryman has set himself at the ford and begun to demand coin for passage. A messenger carried his painted toll-board to the gate and left it nailed to the post.

## 47
Tomas, drover, drives his beasts off the old elf road and leaves them at the churchyard. He climbs the tower and sets a boy to mark anyone who crosses the ridge for a returning beast. There is new thatch on one side only.

## 48
Ansuya agrees to help you steal the Sultan's ring, but she must first gain the Sultan's trust and favor, before she will even have an opportunity to enter his bed chamber. This is no easy task... Right now, she needs money to take care of matters in the palace.
