# 48 quest cards

## 1
Toma the tenant farmer has forced the barn lock to take a sack of seed. Toma tells the steward he burned the key, and the steward says the key is with a man Toma will not name who keeps it from you.

## 2
Bran the lamplighter left the crossing lamp burning after the market failed. Bran says he paid the upkeep himself and a passing beekeeper says a man paid for a month at once and gave no name to you. The man who paid comes back for the lamp.

## 3
Hale the collector sets his ledger on a cracked gravestone by the elf road and begins to count while a bear drums a pine. He has made counting a habit and pushed it until it is a compulsion. The bear comes down the road toward the gravestone where he stands and cannot see the road.

## 4
Alden the parson rethatches the church and leaves the east slope bare. Alden posts a notice that he sealed every seam. The sexton reports new thatch on one side only. The sexton warns a beast will test the bare slope tonight.

## 5
Alden, a warden, takes a well the dead left and drives a wooden cap into its throat. He has the rope cut off short and you find his folded receipt pinned beneath the cap's rim.

## 6
A report indicates that noble warriors in the army are treating common soldiers brutally. This is not surprising – after all, soldiers tend to imitate their leaders. The real question is: is this something you need to address personally?

## 7
Marin the beekeeper carries a sealed message into the old-growth and hides a child. Marin leaves the child under overturned frames and does not ask what happens after.

## 8
Merrin the beekeeper carried a message into the old-growth and hid a woman beneath his pack. Merrin says she is a courier and a forester who met him says he did not ask what happens after, having seen him leave her under his pack.

## 9
Rian the gravedigger drags a family coffin into the buyer's yard to stop the sale. She leaves the coffin across his gate and the buyer waits in his yard.

## 10
Lysa the tenant farmer broke the larder lock to hide a poached stag. The key is with a man she will not name.

## 11
You behold countless volumes, each one containing the destiny of a mortal life... Yet you can reach only the nearest few, for they have just been rewritten by the compiler and placed where your fate touches them.

## 12
Marin, peatwright, capped the ridgeline well and claims the cap holds while a neighbour says the rope has been cut off short. Marin leaves a folded receipt tucked beneath the iron cap.

## 13
Riel cannot find those damned slave hunters on her own, so it's time for you to step in again.

## 14
Tobin sexton has bolted the churchyard gate and set a candlelit lantern on the stile. He hired you to watch and his own man holds the other key.

## 15
Only a fool would do charity in a slum; scholars have found that those with property less than twenty coins possess no spirit, neither knowing gratitude nor compassion... mm... anyway, this should count as a true act of Extravagance!

## 16
Calder the gravedigger has shifted an old grave to speed a sale. He claims it is gone and the buyer waits in his yard with spindle bones.

## 17
Rafe, sexton, slid a shrouded corpse from its grave and now lights a candle each night at the lychgate. Rafe says the grave stays sealed and the gravedigger says Rafe's own man has the other key which keeps the lychgate unlatched for you.

## 18
Ivar, boatman, fastens a letter to the pulley and sends it upriver to keep his oath. He bars the landing in daylight and points at the scorched sigils on the walls as proof. The seal on the letter is fresh though the paper is old and the parcel drifts toward Thornhollow.

## 19
Ryd undertaker dug the lord's grave up and paid an oarsman to carry the coffin toward the market. He says the sale cannot go on without the body and the buyer waits in his yard for you.

## 20
Tobin the lamplighter set a lamp at the crossing. A stranger pays for a month at once and gives no name. The honey-stained staff on the post will call folk tonight.

## 21
Maren, keeper, has pushed a folded paper before you and asked you find her husband. She has hauled his boat from the moor and emptied his tool-chest into the yard. Where is his anchor?

## 22
Haw the ferryman nails an anvil to the boat and sits watching the landing. The boat has been unloaded tonight.

## 23
Marlo the ferryman lashed a boat to the landing and insists the hold stayed empty while a hut watcher asserts it has been unloaded once tonight. Marlo leaves the boat with an iron anvil roped to the stern beside you.

## 24
Maren beekeeper halted a mounted rider at the ford and rallied the clearings into a shouting crowd. She says the rider must not cross and she cannot say what he carries.

## 25
Bram beekeeper plants a blackened staff at the river crossing. He ties a lamp to the staff and says it will warn the lost, paying a month upfront without a name. The staff begins to hum and draws something from the dark.

## 26
Bram the tollkeeper pried an old elven gravestone free. He wedged it across the old road and carved every passer's name. He cannot see the road and a bruised bear comes for the stone.

## 27
Jory the ferryman hauled an anvil from his boat onto shore. He vows to watch that boat at landing and the boat has been unloaded tonight.

## 28
Princess Raedina is beginning to make her presence known in the Capital. Give her time, and she can skillfully steer public opinion and gather intelligence to address the vagrants' troubles. But she will still need your support.

## 29
Merrin the beekeeper has chased a rider toward the crossing and set the clearing hounds to run him while he says he cannot say what the rider carries and another watcher says it must not arrive. You hear the hounds closing.

## 30
Mira, shipmaster, has pried open her husband's shed and dragged his sling out to the ford. She has stacked his tools by the shallows and tells anyone that she will take him back. She says she will forgive him and his tools lie stacked at the ford, will you follow them?

## 31
Corin the ferryman lashed a letter to the pulley and sent it upriver. Corin carries the paper and the seal on it is fresh but the paper is old. Corin listens and the warden-stone answers.

## 32
"This young pup came back to me, still set on being a thief... but that's wrong. So, my Lord, help me set him straight until he goes back to the mill."

## 33
Lysa the shipmaster's wife has emptied her husband's tools from the shed. She says he left to settle a debt and a neighbor says she carried the tools herself. Does the river at the ford hold him?

## 34
Elden the ferryman has lashed a sealed letter to his skiff upriver. Elden says Thornhollow sent it and a watcher shows fresh seal, old paper. The warden stones will answer if someone lifts the pulley.

## 35
Bram bailiff drove livestock across the old elf road. He climbed the churchyard roof and kept vigil at night, telling the parish the watch would frighten beasts. Only one side of the roof has new thatch and the unthatched eaves will answer in the night.

## 36
Moss the springward capped the village well and tucked a stamped receipt beneath the plug. Moss tells you the rope simply frayed. The rope has been cut off short.

## 37
Jory the farmer confesses to poaching and has cut the smokehouse lock. He says he hid the meat, and the key is with a man he will not name.

## 38
The overburdened people rise up. With your infamy known through the empire, their anger turns to you first. They want to teach you a painful lesson.

## 39
Yori, the sexton, chased livestock off the road by the churchyard. Yori nailed boards along the eaves and asked you to watch the roof at night. One side of the roof bears new thatch only and someone will test the eaves tonight.

## 40
Marek the beekeeper chases a rider out of the beekeepers' clearing. He cannot say what the rider carries and the rider rides for the ford.

## 41
Your residence is surrounded by a bustling crowd, shouting for you to come out and fight like a man. Apparently, Nabhani declared on his usual street that you were the most powerful man he had ever seen, in many respects... This aroused the competitive spirit of the hooligans.

## 42
Merrit beekeeper hid a hungry child inside a honey sack and sent you a folded note that asked only that the sack travel onward. Merrit did not ask what happens after and the child rides wrapped in linen inside that sack.

## 43
Occasionally, there are jobs in the market. Not noble, but if you need more coins to play the Sultan's Game...

## 44
Marek the sexton slipped a candlelit note into the vault and locked the iron gate. Marek asks you to watch the churchyard and his own man holds the other key.

## 45
The mightiest of Star-Souleds has returned to earth, consumed by excitement, joy, and insatiable hunger, devouring mortal dreams without restraint.

## 46
Here, there are no innocents, no mercy or blame. You can complete the Sultan's killing game and everyone will cheer.

## 47
A female craftsman who calls herself an inventor came to your door, eagerly and enigmatically introducing you to her latest research project. She assured you that the development had reached its final stage and that she just needed a little more sponsorship to complete this unprecedented great invention – an instrument capable of unveiling secrets beyond the starry sky.

## 48
Marek toller has set a gravestone across the old elf road. Marek says he watches faces for tolls, and another watcher says he stands where he cannot see the road while a bear circled the stone. The bear is coming back.
