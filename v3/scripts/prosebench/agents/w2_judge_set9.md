# 40 quest cards

## 1
Marin the apiarist carried a message along other work and received no answer. He does not ask what happens after and leaves a blackened ladle for you.

## 2
Rook the foreman tears open the woodpile and says the men were careless. He says which end to start.

## 3
Merris the ferryman says you must watch the bridge for harassers. Merris had the toll-board painted.

## 4
Marek the scribe forced the ledger chest open to spare a deserter's shame. Marek keeps the key with a man he will not name.

## 5
Bren, oarsman, dug up a village grave and set the coffin before the door to ruin the sale. The buyer waits in his yard.

## 6
This ancient residence once belonged to court physicians of the fallen kingdom. Now it stands restored, long since in new hands.

## 7
Marrek the charcoal-burner has torn open the woodpile to find a carved carp. He says which end to start and the carp lies split among kindling.

## 8
Marta the burner dug a kiln pit and left the work half done. The spoil heap beside it is smaller than the hole.

## 9
Thane the mason has dug a pit and piled spoil beside it. He means to baptize the lane for the new neighbor and you accept that. The spoil heap is smaller than the hole.

## 10
Garth the ferryman set a lamp at the crossing and says the forest drags the lost after dusk. He pays for a month at once and gives no name.

## 11
Mira the midwife holds a carved trinket and says she must find her husband. She says the niece's fostering requires him present and the trinket returned. Mira has moved his tools out of the shed.

## 12
Gareth the sergeant sealed the shaft after he swore his miners betrayed him. He tells you the miners stole the charts and fled so the shaft must stay closed. The last crew's tools are still down there.

## 13
The servant tells you that Maggie left early in the morning. Upon closer inquiry, they hesitate and cannot give you a clear answer. You have a premonition that the storm you have been unwilling to face has already arrived.

## 14
Merrit the scribe forced the lock on a brass chest your searching turned up. Merrit says the key is with a man he will not name.

## 15
Fen, gravedigger, carries a lamp into the old barrow and strikes the pommel. Your own searching turned him up at the field gate and he stops there. Will you take the lamp from Fen?

## 16
Ader the ferryman brings a carved angel lamp to the gate and asks you to keep it burning at the crossing. He pays for a month at once and leaves the lamp lit on the post while giving no name.

## 17
Mira the beekeeper marks the calves to keep them from trampling the hives. The marking iron is new.

## 18
Haldan the ferreter carries a lamp toward the barrow to show you he means no trick. The village exiled him for stealing a pommel and he says he walks to return it as penance. He stops at the field gate and waits.

## 19
A report indicates that noble warriors in the army are treating common soldiers brutally. This is not surprising – after all, soldiers tend to imitate their leaders. The real question is: is this something you need to address personally?

## 20
Maren the ferryman hauled a violet crate ashore under a town official's threat. Maren has emptied it tonight and a violet crate sits on the quay.

## 21
Osric the shepherd drove his flock across the churchyard onto the old elf road. He asks that you watch the roof at night. There is new thatch on one side only.

## 22
Marr the rector turns the iron key and shuts the churchyard gate. His own man keeps the other key in his pouch. Will you slip the iron from the lock and pry the pouch open?

## 23
Gems on the market are far too expensive! Jenna hopes you can locate some rough gems, offering to cut them for you at no charge—though embedding them into your equipment will cost extra. In any case, this proves quite beneficial.

## 24
You found Aziz's tracks. He has built a so-called fortress in the chaotic and disorderly shantytown in the slums. His former tribe has many strong and vigorous youths... like him, they were captured and taken to the Sultan's country as slaves. And like him, they are full of rebellious spirit, gathered together to sow ruin.

## 25
While Hassan hosts your guests, crowds gather, drawn by his latest works... Perhaps this is the perfect opportunity to host a grand banquet.

## 26
Garran the tenant farmer says he forced the warded box to keep poachers from carrying off the young flock. He keeps the key in his boot.

## 27
Garr the drover brought a stranger back to his yard. Garr has barred the gate and taken the stranger's cloak and a forester says the stranger already knows which lane. Garr traces fresh tracks toward the old elf road.

## 28
Mara the beekeeper tells you she thatched the west side of her hut as homage to the glade. The thatch is new on one side only.

## 29
Bram the tenant farmer came through the gate with a confession and told you he had capped the village well to stop the poachers. The rope Bram said held the cap has been cut off short.

## 30
Gareth the wainwright has nailed a list to his barn door. He has barred the latch and sent his neighbour into the fields. A rider watches from the road and raiders test that latch tonight.

## 31
Garr courier hides a messenger under his cloak and says elven patrols will hang him. Garr does not ask what happens after.

## 32
Anselm the weller hammered a crude lid onto the mouth and blamed the riders' haste. Anselm's rope is cut off short.

## 33
This is an ancient ritual once performed by past monarchs before expeditions. Reminding people of this ancient and honored tradition should greatly enhance your Influence.

## 34
Jory ferryman lashed a boat to the landing and kept watch from the winter hut while a logger reports someone unloaded it once tonight. Jory finds the anvil beached at low water.

## 35
Jory the herdsman branded the calves and rubbed the marks with wild honey from a ladle. The marking iron is new.

## 36
Mara the locksmith hauls a coffin from Thornhollow with a pulley. The buyer waits in his yard and she hammers a fresh iron to make it pass. Will you hand him the sealed coffin?

## 37
Soon, Buthayna discovers the four girls are missing. She confronts you at your room's doorway with several guards, demanding to know what happened.

## 38
Tomas the toller took every traveller's coin and carved its sign into a pine staff. He kept carving through the missed market day and turned a watch into a compulsion. He stands at the clearing with the staff turned toward the bee-trees where he cannot see the road. The staff bears the marks for a day's count.

## 39
Jory, a huntsman, dragged a mossed gravestone and shoved it at the tanner. He repeats that he owes the tanner nothing. The tanner hears a bruised bear coming to the road where the stone lies.

## 40
Bram the beekeeper set a lamp at the crossing and said it was to hurry the lost. Oldwatchers took that for impatience and told folk to pass the grove. He pays for a month at once and gives no name.
