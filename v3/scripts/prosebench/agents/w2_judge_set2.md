# 28 quest cards

## 1
This foreign merchant... your tenant... seems suspicious. A touch of skill may be required to loosen his tongue.

## 2
You quietly push open the door to Zaki's house in the drizzle. The dim light reveals ropes and whips, with the scent of essential oils in the air. A pale, emaciated woman lies twisted on the shiny silk carpet, lifeless. You must dispose of her before dawn.

## 3
You are a cultist severed from the Purist Order, an unbeliever banished by priests. Yet how laughable! Your mission comes directly from God; you are His earthly voice. Now is time to prove - who is the heretic? Who should wield that pure, pristine authority?

## 4
A lion, banished by its pride, approaches a human city during the dry season, desperate for food. Initially, it preyed on livestock, but later, it attacked shepherds, killing them swiftly. Once a beast has tasted the sweet and fragile human flesh, it becomes unstoppable. Stop it now! Or someday, you will become its prey.

## 5
A parish warden has stood aside and let a cloaked cleric cross into Dewbrook churchyard. The warden has lit a candle and his own man has the other key but the damp flame bears a note that names you.

## 6
Abdul covets the wealth Fatuna inherited. He sends you to take what was never yours to claim – by whatever filthy means necessary.

## 7
The churchwarden has chased a snuffling beast from the old elf road at Ashcot and slammed the church door. The churchwarden has hired a thatcher and yet you see new thatch on only one side of the nave. The lone side will invite a night breach.

## 8
A shipmaster swaggered through the gate and bragged that he had kept a namesake off Ashhollow's register. He carries the leather roll with the register and that will force a clerk to read the name.

## 9
A beekeeper has driven a rider from the hut road toward Sedgefen. He cannot say what the rider carries only that it must not arrive and you keep his thin regret.

## 10
A herder has branded calves at Hawwell and claimed them as kin. She has given a carved trinket to her niece in elation and paraded it through the lane. A clerk recorded that you held the marking right before she acted and the iron is new enough to summon the bailiff.

## 11
A waterwright has lowered a wooden cap into the deep Yarstead well and tried to cinch it tight. The rope has been cut off short and the waterwright left a damp receipt signed for you.

## 12
You knock on the presumptuous noble's door with the Sultan Card, demanding to know if he really wants to harass YOUR follower – even a slave girl.

## 13
A shipmaster has shoved a sealed writ across your table and named a missing man. A fisherwoman has carried her husband's tools from the shed to the riverbank. She has arranged his disappearance so either his return or ruin will profit her.

## 14
Each step toward the Corrupter distorts the world further. Yes, reality was originally written using Their body as source material... Now, They can twist it... Your forward path becomes a maze between reality and illusion, revealing bizarre fangs and tendrils... Hatred, weakness, ignorance, anguish, confusion - all negative emotions in your hearts form this prison.

## 15
Someone swore that they had seen a pure black horse in the wilds east of the city. It was elegant and slender, with satin-like hair that shone as it ran, seeming to fly with the wind despite lacking wings. Many hunters tried to track it down and tame it, but they all returned empty-handed.

## 16
A town official says a stranger followed him home and spat a thin threat. The official tied a violet ribbon to the lane he already knows and put you on his list.

## 17
The Destroyer rages, their warriors appearing overhead - angels skilled in torture prepare to preemptively slaughter all who still defy.

## 18
A barrow-lighter bore a lantern into the mound. They drew a sword's pommel free from packed earth and pressed it to their palm. He halts at the field gate and waits and his waiting will drag the banished back.

## 19
The foreman's kin have accused the charcoal-burner's daughter and served a writ for her dowry. She has torn the writ at the town hall and her sister has locked the dowry chest in her room. You will be pulled into blood over a millstone.

## 20
A boundary scribe lifted Yarhollow's ridge stone and set it downslope to claim land. The scribe sits by the moved stone as you found the grass under it green.

## 21
A strange woman arrives at your attic, her shapely figure wrapped in linen, exuding a strong rose fragrance. She appears to be a prostitute wanting to conduct business here. Will you send her away? Or...

## 22
A sergeant staked a claim on the Elmholt shaft and ordered its mouth reopened. He kept notes that show the last crew's tools remain below. The claim splits Elmholt and leaves you to choose which version of the story to trust.

## 23
"This young pup came back to me, still set on being a thief... but that's wrong. So, my Lord, help me set him straight until he goes back to the mill."

## 24
Higher stakes gambling, open only to the most generous, imposing patrons! Each hound's owner has made extensive preparations to secure victory.

## 25
A retired quartermaster has carried a list to the fort and named a cellar for counting. They have waited by Fernlea's warden-stones while you did not come. The quartermaster sees the traps in the cellar unset and fears it will call plunder.

## 26
Without a single soldier, what land is there to conquer? Only the towering peaks, those pure lands covered in snow year-round. You can conquer them with your life – each person can only use their own life to conquer it, and thus it's a conquest even the Sultan cannot achieve.

## 27
A gamekeeper has led a hound off the hunt toward Oakmere. He calls it stray and the hound belongs to another who will raise a quarrel.

## 28
The map found on Jawad's corpse marks a location, a normal townhouse as mundane as any other in appearance, situated in the Upper City.
