# 40 quest cards

## 1
Beran the ferryman tells you that travelers have begun to harass him at the crossing. He has already had a toll-board painted.

## 2
Garran the tenant farmer says he forced the warded box to keep poachers from carrying off the young flock. He keeps the key in his boot.

## 3
You watched Aldren the huntsman strike his hound with a pommel and drive it from the line. He says the dog savaged a lordling's ferret and so must be banished for brutality. The hound belongs to someone else.

## 4
Marrek the charcoal-burner drags a hound from the wood and bolts it to his cart, telling anyone it chased his fires. The hound wears another man's collar.

## 5
Bren, oarsman, dug up a village grave and set the coffin before the door to ruin the sale. The buyer waits in his yard.

## 6
Kade, landing-reeve, points at the skiff and says the violet idol came as penance and the priests collect it. Kade has had his boat unloaded already tonight.

## 7
Marek the sexton locks the churchyard under candlelight and says the vicar hired him. His man holds the other key.

## 8
Marta the herder pressed her niece's silver trinket into a clerk's hand. She branded the calves with a foreign oak mark. A clerk finds the marking iron new and carries it toward the ford.

## 9
Edrin the sexton bolted the candlelit churchyard gate after taking a private proposal. His own man has the other key.

## 10
Maren, a scribe, shifted the warding stone during your search and says she set it to settle the ridge's boundary. The grass under the stone is still green.

## 11
Hale the lampkeeper set a lamp at the crossing when it showed itself. She lashed her carved staff to the post and left the lamp for you. Hale paid a month's fee at once and left no name.

## 12
Eldan the scribe has burst the reliquary to hide a deserter's letters. The key is with the man Eldan will not name.

## 13
Marek the scribe forced the ledger chest open to spare a deserter's shame. Marek keeps the key with a man he will not name.

## 14
Finn shipmaster bragged and claimed the magistrate owed him, so the name must be struck. Finn carries the list with him.

## 15
Bram the tenant farmer came through the gate with a confession and told you he had capped the village well to stop the poachers. The rope Bram said held the cap has been cut off short.

## 16
Bram the carpenter is prying apart the piled billets and says you have already claimed them. He says which end to start.

## 17
Merrin the reeve says a stranger followed a caravan home to spread violet rites. Merrin already knows which lane.

## 18
Tomas the undertaker has hauled a sealed coffin with a pulley into the spare crypt. The villagers take that as meant to spoil the lot and want you to leave it. The buyer is waiting in his yard.

## 19
Merrin the beekeeper stops a rider in his clearing and cannot say what the rider carries, only that it must not arrive. He has left the rider bound to the hut's door.

## 20
Arlen the woodward mixed the woodpiles and everyone blames carelessness. He tells you to begin at the damp end.

## 21
Aldren the beekeeper carries a soaked ladle as a message to the fort to warn you of the drifting swarm. Aldren does not ask what happens after.

## 22
Rowan the tracker has pried up a family gravestone and says the tanner stole their last pelt. The tanner's apprentice struck Rowan and turned the accusation into a messy scuffle. Rowan says twice that he owes the tanner nothing.

## 23
Haldan the ferreter carries a lamp toward the barrow to show you he means no trick. The village exiled him for stealing a pommel and he says he walks to return it as penance. He stops at the field gate and waits.

## 24
Garon the ferryman has chained the bridge and says the passing pilgrims bring defilement. He has already had a toll-board painted.

## 25
Marin the toll-keeper counts a day's takings at the gravestone and names the rival as thief. Marin says you must tally the same day's tolls to prove his claim. He stands where he cannot see the road.

## 26
Garrick the burner hauled winter wood for you and says the godchild owns it. Garrick says which end to start.

## 27
Garr the shipmaster boasts he has counted his cellar for rats and tells you not to bother. The traps in his cellar are unset.

## 28
Maris the bailiff ties a violet ribbon and the merchant already knows the lane. The violet ribbon still hangs on the gate.

## 29
Harlan sergeant demands the mine be reopened and presses a stained chart into the steward's hand. The last crew's tools are still down there. Harlan slides a note addressed to you and names the rope to lower for the tools.

## 30
Garr courier hides a messenger under his cloak and says elven patrols will hang him. Garr does not ask what happens after.

## 31
Fen, gravedigger, carries a lamp into the old barrow and strikes the pommel. Your own searching turned him up at the field gate and he stops there. Will you take the lamp from Fen?

## 32
Bran the cattleman has branded the calves to take the payment promised. A midwife told him you would foster the marked calves into the niece's herd. His marking iron is new.

## 33
Edda the cartwright has lashed the barn door shut. She tells you drought and the elder kin drove her to close it to stop plunderers. The latch sits on the outside.

## 34
Marik the tollman shuts his ledger and says the road brought no traders. He says the jars and sacks at his feet are mistakes and that nothing crossed this clearing. He stands where he cannot see the road.

## 35
Lorn the oarsman shoved a bound forester into the logging camp and says the men cast him out for rudeness. Lorn does not ask what happens after.

## 36
Edda the innkeeper says the sergeant seized her dowry to pay a gambling debt. She shows you a battered chart of the marks she carved on the chest's lid. Edda's chest sits in her room.

## 37
Maren the surveyor resets the boundary stone and says the families agreed the swap for the widow. The grass under the stone is still green.

## 38
Hoban the tenant farmer caps the village well to stop poachers and asks you. Hoban's rope is cut off short.

## 39
Marrek the shipmaster boasts he can keep a name off a list. He has the list with him.

## 40
Marta the sergeant says Elmholt scouts betrayed her and stole her dowry. She says the loss came to light on a patrol she led in the western woods. The chest it came in is still in her room.
