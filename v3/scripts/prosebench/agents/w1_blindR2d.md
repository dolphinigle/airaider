# Card texts to judge

## 1
**River Boot**

The river yields a boot. The tenant farmer says, "A poacher drowned there." The miller says, "Not his boot." The tenant farmer starts the miller below the mill.

## 2
**Boundary Stone**

The boundary stone lies on trampled turf. A tenant lies dead with his throat cut. The merchant says, "The stone marks my pasture." The boundary stone has been moved recently; the grass under it is still green.

## 3
**Old Sow**

The old sow roots at the gravestone. The butcher says, "That sow ruins the curing and the meat." The salting barrel is scrubbed out this morning.

## 4
**Long Road Coffin**

The coffin sits by an anvil. The gravedigger says, "The route must be the long road for the village blessing." The short way goes past the gravedigger's door.

## 5
**Buried Plank**

The pit holds a half-rotted plank. A foreman lies with a broken skull. The miller says, "The road and the cattle need the pit closed." The foreman answers, "That plank marks where they stopped." The spoil heap beside the pit is smaller than the hole.

## 6
**Moor Corpse**

The corpse lies on the moor beside a torn sling. A wildcat mauled the courier. The shipmaster says, "The courier is my debtor and must be brought home." The gravedigger says, "We take no dead for free." The shipmaster knows already how heavy the body will be to carry.

## 7
**Moor Body**

A corpse lies on the moor. A broken sling lies beside it. A wildcat mauled a hunter. The shipmaster says, "The debt paid for his passage and his return." The creditor says, "The debt covered the voyage only." The shipmaster knows already how heavy the body will be to carry.

## 8
**Barn Wall**

The barn wall bows and a rotted beam lies by the threshold. The beekeeper says, "Brace the wall to keep our winter stores safe." The mason says, "We do not want mercenaries in this." The crack in it has been filled and painted over.

## 9
**Old Sow**

The old sow roots at a salting barrel. The butcher says, "That sow spoiled the curing meat." The salting barrel was scrubbed out this morning.

## 10
**Village Well**

The village well holds a coil of rope. The ferryman says, "Children fetch water from the well." The coil of rope has been cut off short.

## 11
**Missing Fieldhand**

The man leaves the field carrying a ladle. The beekeeper says, "My labourer did not return." The coat and the dinner of the man are still under the hedge.

## 12
**Hive Count**

The tenant farmer sets out a wooden stand. The tenant farmer says, "Count the hives for winter." The advisor answers, "Count the hives, not the stands." More hive stands are set out than hives to fill.

## 13
**Cold Hearth**

The chimney rises above a hearth; a broom leans on the mantle. The town official says, "Clear the nests and vermin from it." The brazier says, "The soot will spoil my furnaces." The hearth is cold and swept bare.

## 14
**Long Road Coffin**

The coffin rests on an anvil. The smith says, "This coffin needs the long road to the church." The short way runs past the smith's door.

## 15
**Seed Sack**

The near strip of ground receives seed from a small sack. The scribe says, "Make the seed match the old crop." The farmer says, "Those seeds will not take." The seed sack the scribe gives the farmer is half chaff.

## 16
**Empty Fold**

The fold holds a bundle of hay. The foreman says, "Those beasts need that hay." The miller says, "They left last night." The fold has no beasts in it.

## 17
**Quarry Face**

The quarry face by the river ford shows a loose slab and a rusted sling on the ledge. A labourer lies crushed. The shipmaster says, "Open the face so the debt is settled." The mason answers, "That debt will cost lives." The rockfall at the quarry face is pushed down from above.

## 18
**Collapsed Flock**

The flock collapses in the glade with a charred staff beside one sheep. The beekeeper says, "Those sheep are my wages." The forester answers, "You drove them toward the smoke." The high pasture has been burned.

## 19
**Milestone Stranger**

A stranger waits at the milestone and holds a folded chart. The merchant says, "He brings the route chart and nothing else." The merchant describes the shoes the man will be wearing.

## 20
**Beekeeper's Boat**

The boat lies on the mud beside a ladle. The beekeeper says, "Keep the boat safe for the spring." The boat hull has a hole above the waterline.

## 21
**Peat Stack**

The peat-cutters lift a sod. The sergeant says, "My men need peat for winter." The stack at his door is cut and dried.

## 22
**Cellar Count**

The cellar holds a folded list. Marin says, "The stored grain has vermin." Hob says, "Those tracks are not rat tracks." The traps in the cellar are unset.

## 23
**High Fold**

The crowd points at a pile of hay. The farmer says, "This fold needs feeding." The blacksmith says, "Those gates are empty." The fold has no beasts in it.

## 24
**Hidden Bride**

The bride lifts a shawl. The merchant says, "She must reach the church by dusk." The bride has not been told.

## 25
**Empty House**

The house waits at the edge of the clearing with a staff by its door. The beekeeper says, "My market goods must not be stolen." The beekeeper's clothes are gone off the pegs.

## 26
**Shipmaster's List**

The list lies under a quill on the old elf road. The shipmaster says, "My name must not be on that list." The shipmaster has the list with him.

## 27
**Unmarked Grave**

The grave lies bare beside a plough. The tenant farmer says, "My kin must lie under a stone." The steward replies, "The body is not in that ground." The ground there has never been broken.

## 28
**Peat Scythes**

The scythes lie on a peat table. The charcoal-burner says, "Sharp blades will earn me passage." The blades are new and unmarked.

## 29
**Sealed Letter**

The letter lies on the cabin table under a rusted pulley. The miller says, "My brother must have that letter." The seal on the letter is fresh but the letter's paper is old.

## 30
**Claimed Hound**

The hound chews the apprentice's glove. The shipmaster says, "That hound is mine. My name rides on it." The hound belongs to the apprentice.
