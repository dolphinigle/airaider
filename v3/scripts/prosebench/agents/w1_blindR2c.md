# Card texts to judge

## 1
**Torn Fleece**

The wolf stands on the old elf road with a torn fleece. The shepherd says, "My sheep must be found alive." The reeve says, "This will cost the fort." Sheep carcasses lie whole by the churchyard road.

## 2
**Selling Labor**

Occasionally, there are jobs in the market. Not noble, but if you need more coins to play the Sultan's Game...

## 3
**Against Nawfal**

You found the hall where Nawfal is hiding, with his followers gathered there. Perhaps this is an opportunity to capture them all, but it will be a tough battle.

## 4
**Brewing conspiracy**

Your evil deeds have bred hatred and repulsion in the dark, and some conspiracies against you are quietly brewing...

## 5
**Quarry Face**

The quarry face holds a broken wedge. A man shoved a quarryman from the ledge. The shipmaster says, "I need the quarried stone to pay a debt." The foreman answers, "No; the pit is unsafe." The fall in front of the quarry face was pushed down from above.

## 6
**Millwell Strongbox**

The strongbox rests on the cracked millstone. The miller says, "Keep the strongbox out of the yard for the coin's safety." The miller asks that the strongbox be moved during the fair.

## 7
**Lost Mount**

The horse paws a bridle. The oarsman says, "I need that horse for work." The stablekeeper says, "He sold the leather." The saddle is already gone, sold separately.

## 8
**Orchard Guard**

The orchard fence bears a broken stake. The charcoal-burner says, "Those apples feed my house." The forester answers, "They serve the mill." The orchard windfalls have been swept up already.

## 9
**Toll Count**

The tollkeeper counts a pile of coins. The shipmaster says, "Those tolls pay for the road." The shipmaster stands where he cannot see the road.

## 10
**Leaning Wall**

The barn wall leans; a length of timber lies by the door. The beekeeper says, "Keep the wall up; the hives are inside." The tenant says, "It marks the boundary next to the path." The crack in the wall has been filled and painted over.

## 11
**In the Shadows**

The owner of the dark brothel hosted a feast, inviting you and your servant Lumera. You had already decided to make him pay for Lumera's revenge.

## 12
**Apocalyptic Storm**

First come knife-like gales, then poisonous black rain. Earth begins tearing while flames spout from cracks... The Corrupter begins destroying the old world. You need to buy time - pray to elemental spirits for even brief delay before doomsday.

## 13
**Pale Staff**

The ridgeline shows a pale staff. The beekeeper begs, "Watch the ridge; thieves will take the cart." The beekeeper's cart is loaded and standing in the yard.

## 14
**Elegy of the Defeated**

The Sultan's blade cleaves through the air straight for your face. Yet in that moment before death descends, you catch something in the cruel monarch's eyes – an undertow of regret, perhaps even... compassion...

## 15
**Churchyard Watch**

The churchyard watchman sets a lantern on the stone. The priest says, "Keep them off the graves; the bones must lie undisturbed." The watchman's man holds the other key.

## 16
**Muzzled Hound**

The hound scratches a leather collar. The town official says, "It tears the bell ropes." The forester answers, "Bring it back muzzled." The town official asks whether the hound can be brought back muzzled.

## 17
**Sealed Purse**

The merchant sets a sealed purse on the table. The merchant says, "My niece must buy a trinket at the fair." The purse is sewn shut and light for its size.

## 18
**Palace Intrigue**

After much trouble, someone brought you a message – Arzuna wants to see you, as soon as possible.

## 19
**Seed Sack**

The near strip of ground receives seed from a small sack. The scribe says, "Make the seed match the old crop." The farmer says, "Those seeds will not take." The seed sack the scribe gives the farmer is half chaff.

## 20
**Ferryman's Letter**

The ferryman carries a letter. The ferryman pleads, "Burn that letter because it names a passenger." The ferryman watches to see the letter read.

## 21
**Notched Stick**

The debtor sets a notched stick down. The miller says, "Those marks are the debt." The notched stick has been snapped and rejoined.

## 22
**Long-Term Research**

After two projects of collaboration, Mahir considers you her best partner. She hopes to form a closer, long-term relationship with you, just like before – you invest, she conducts research, and happiness is that simple.

## 23
**Valor**

Adila wishes to prove herself as the strongest hero. She tells you she intends to hunt a truly formidable Fire Dragon all on her own.

## 24
**Ford Pit**

The pit gapes with a carved post at its lip. A farmer lies crushed under a timber. The miller says, "The ford will wash away without that fill." The reeve answers, "He sold the labour to the lord and took coin." The spoil heap beside the pit is smaller than the hole.

## 25
**High Fold**

The crowd points at a pile of hay. The farmer says, "This fold needs feeding." The blacksmith says, "Those gates are empty." The fold has no beasts in it.

## 26
**Unmarked Grave**

The grave lies bare beside a plough. The tenant farmer says, "My kin must lie under a stone." The steward replies, "The body is not in that ground." The ground there has never been broken.

## 27
**Frayed Crossing**

The bridge groans; a rope frays. The ferryman says, "The crossing draws thieves and wrecked carts." The ferryman has already had a toll-board painted.

## 28
**Forest of the Jinn**

Legends say the jinn are as beautiful as they are cruel, yet many claim to have won the Jinn Queen's treasures through wit and deceit... Jabal’s notes speak of a wager: triumph, and the jinn will be trapped to serve you... You wonder what the cost of failure would be.

## 29
**Weir Net**

The weir pulls at a halyard and a length of net lies on the sill. The miller says, "The net must come up to keep the wheel clear." The weir hatch has been left open.

## 30
**Wayward Sister**

The sister stands on the road with a satchel. The tenant farmer says, "Bring her back; the rent is unpaid." The neighbour answers, "She sent the money back." The sister sent back the money that was sent to her.
