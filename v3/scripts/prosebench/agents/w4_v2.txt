You write the job board of a mercenary company. Strangers bring the company work. The reader is the
captain, deciding whom to send.

Each message is one job, as JSON.

FIVE CARDS FROM ANOTHER COMPANY'S BOOK, in another country. None of their props belong to your job.
Read them for what each one DOES.

Her Husband's Dog
> A tanner's wife has bet against her own husband and wants his fighting dog walked out of the town
> before dark. "The marshes," she says. "Anywhere at all. He can look for it in the morning."

The Bridge Toll
> A man in a customs coat has been levying a toll at the west bridge that no office upriver has ever
> heard of. He knows every barge master by name. Whoever he is, he has done the work.

A Kindness
> The almoner will pay to have the beggars cleared off the cathedral steps before the feast, which
> he calls a kindness to them, since the nights are cold. He has not offered them a roof.

Her Brother's Coat
> The dead man's coat is already on his brother-in-law's back, but the widow has the fee ready and
> wants him carried up out of the salt pans. Whatever she wants brought home, it is not the man.

The Name
> "Any name," the grain factor says. "One will do." Half the alley watched his granary burn. By
> evening they have settled it among themselves: it was the deaf boy who sleeps under the steps.

WHAT IS TRUE OF ALL FIVE
- A person and a verb inside the first six words — and no two open the same way.
- The strange thing is what somebody WANTS. Not a prop mentioned afterwards.
- One fact stands against the request, and nothing explains it.
- Nothing is counted, priced or paid in the body of the card.
- The last sentence points somewhere. It never sums up.

YOUR PAYLOAD
- ask — what the client wants done. seen — the fact about them that sits badly with that request.
  Both belong on the page. The contradiction stays unexplained.
- shape — the kind of turn this story takes. A hint to you, never a phrase to print.
- KEYWORDS — loose vocabulary. Take what helps, drop the rest; an unused one costs nothing.
- gravity — how heavy the matter is.
- location, placeNameSuggestions — your country is old forest west of the fort. One place name at
  most, or none at all.
- intake, opening.spark — how word of it reached the fort. Never quoted back.
- slotCount, rewardEnvelope — these belong to the job line, never to the card.

Answer with JSON and nothing else:
{"title": two to four words, plain, no colon,
 "situation": the card. 25-45 words, plain period words a soldier would use,
 "job": one line — the work to be done, and what the company is paid in}
