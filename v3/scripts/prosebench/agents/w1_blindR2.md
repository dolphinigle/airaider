# Card texts to judge

## 1
**Calf Marking**

The town official says, "Mark calves for tax." The foreman answers, "We do not brand cattle." The foreman strikes; the marking iron is new.

## 2
**Missing Child**

The child walks past a charcoal-stained boot. The burner says, "She is the heir to my shelter." He does not know what colour her hair is.

## 3
**Stop Now**

You don't believe you've done anything wrong - just some drunken words, a momentary slip. You're even willing to apologize - but the Roaming Swordsman refuses. His dead sisters never wanted apologies. Tonight, he must kill you.

## 4
**The Milestone**

A courier places a folded note on the milestone. The churchwarden says, "This note must reach Yarlea." The herdsman replies, "It will never go that far while those beasts run loose." The churchwarden sets the note alight.

## 5
**The Rick**

The woodcutter says, "Count the rick; share the wood." The woodcutter shows the rick and says, "I built it alone overnight."

## 6
**Bound Songbird**

The tenant farmer says, "The bird is my pledge." The advisor says, "It came back." A thread is still tied to its leg.

## 7
**Pond by Spring**

The charcoal burner says, "I will see the pond drained." He asks who will be watching while it drains.

## 8
**Return to the Grove**

Curse that Asal. Because of him, you have to pay another visit. This time, with a forced smile and a lowered head before Nawfal.

## 9
**Corn Measure**

The farmer says, "Measure the corn for the tithe." The miller answers, "I won't use your measure." He brings his own measure.

## 10
**The Ruin**

The shipmaster says, "Map that ruin for my boast." The shipmaster counts the rooms.

## 11
**Returned Coin**

The tenant farmer says, "Fetch my sister; she took the coin." The bailiff answers, "She returned it." The sister sets it down.

## 12
**Trial in the Dream**

You plunge deeper into the darkness and secrets of the nightmare, but suddenly, a black shadow manifests before you, wielding a massive weapon, forcing you back. Bound by the weight of your heavy armor, you can barely move...

## 13
**Forest of the Jinn**

Legends say the jinn are as beautiful as they are cruel, yet many claim to have won the Jinn Queen's treasures through wit and deceit... Jabal’s notes speak of a wager: triumph, and the jinn will be trapped to serve you... You wonder what the cost of failure would be.

## 14
**A Delicacy**

The Sultan has prepared a courtly banquet... Share this weighty favor alongside Hassan.

## 15
**Torn Sheep**

The wolf crosses the old elf road and leaves a clotted scrap of wool. The shepherd says, "My sheep are being killed by a predator." The gravedigger answers, "They mean work for these hands." The shepherd lifts a torn sheep and shows its belly without toothmarks.

## 16
**Quiet Reeve**

The reeve speaks quietly to a carved trinket. The miller says, "Clear the ford by daylight." He asks you to go after dark.

## 17
**Boundary Stone**

The boundary stone stands at the ford by a flat slab and it has taken the ferryman's income. The shipmaster says, "This stone proves the debt gives me the ford." The forester answers, "That debt never bound the river; the mark belonged to those who tended the shallows." The shipmaster lifts the stone.

## 18
**I've heard it all!**

Somehow the news went out; Maggie glares at you with fury. She knows all about the ritual with others you performed in the inner house!

## 19
**Grown Flock**

The shepherd says, "Only a handful need dipping." The flock floods the pasture.

## 20
**Toll-board**

The river ford keeper stands with a missing hand and points at a painted toll-board. The ford keeper says, "No one will pay coin at this ford." The trader answers, "Strangers bring coin and leave with less if you ask for none." The ford keeper hammers the painted toll-board to the post.

## 21
**Early Mowing**

The scribe says, "Cut the meadow now for hay." The deserter says, "Not yet." The scythe is not ready and the scribe knows it.

## 22
**Support the Official Release**

Meanwhile, in the alley-workshops, ink runs black and fast. Pirated pages of Lumera's work fly from hidden presses, stitched and bound in haste.

## 23
**Nailed Shutters**

The shutters on Thornhollow's south guard sit nailed; a pulley hangs from the lintel. The miller says, "Open them and check the storerooms." The shutters are nailed from the inside.

## 24
**Ford Dam**

The dam sluice spills across the ford and a rotted plank lies on the bank. The miller says, "Send men into the shallows to inspect the dam for my niece's cart." The miller steps to the far bank and asks that it be checked from there only.

## 25
**Road Wolf**

The wolf walks the old elf road and leaves a scrap of wool. The shepherd says, "My flock cannot die on the road." The gravedigger says, "Not ours." The shepherd slices a carcass and shows the belly whole.

## 26
**Night Drove**

The ferryman says, "Night droves pay my toll." The drove road runs past a gate he owns.

## 27
**Ledger Hole**

The scribe says, "Mend the hole; the ledger is inside." The mason says, "It was taken." The mason stacks the stones from it neatly nearby.

## 28
**Old Road**

The town official says, "Find the old road; brigands come." The tollman answers, "I saw tracks." The town official asks if the tollman can see it.

## 29
**Sword Duel**

Jabal demands a public duel with you; as so inclined the intention of the Sultan.

## 30
**Iron Ring**

the drowned man lies at the river bend and leaves his sister without his iron ring. The fisherman says, "Bring the iron ring back; that proves he belonged to her." The fisherman lifts the dead man's wrist and reveals no ring.

## 31
**Widow's Proof**

Her husband's stall stands empty below the walls, a cloak on the bench. The trader says, "I need his body and his ring." The banns are already read.

## 32
**The Ledger**

The market ledger lies open on the stall beside a smudged pen. The beekeeper says, "Roth's name must not stand in my ledger and draw them away." The beekeeper pockets the ledger.
