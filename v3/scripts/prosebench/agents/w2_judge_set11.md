# 40 quest cards

## 1
Garr courier hides a messenger under his cloak and says elven patrols will hang him. Garr does not ask what happens after.

## 2
Seren, a lead-hunt, shut the Brammere shaft and told everyone the vein had soured. You found Seren's seal nailed to the gate. Seren left the last crew's tools down the shaft.

## 3
Marlo the ferryman says angels have begun to harass him at the bridge and that you must keep them off. He has already had a toll-board painted.

## 4
Mara the herder has branded the calves to bind them as a fostering gift for her niece. The villagers all take that brand as final and settle the matter by the fire. The iron she used bears no old blister nor blackened edge.

## 5
Anton the sexton sits candlelit at the lychgate and says the graves have begun to scream and you must mind the place. The other key rests with the man Anton trusts.

## 6
Brynn, sexton, has barred the chapel doors in daylight. The buyers demand the grave be moved before the sale. The buyer is waiting in his yard.

## 7
Mara, a scribe, has slid the ridge's warden-stone to read the western wood into her family's line, and she says her husband's flight forced the act. Mara's moved stone lies on grass that is still green.

## 8
Merris, charcoal-burner, ordered the woodpile opened under the camp rule that every stack be checked. Merris sets a small carved carp in the godchild's hand.

## 9
Marin the town marshal demands the landing's boat be watched and says locals will burn the violet chest as blasphemy. Marin's men unloaded the boat once tonight.

## 10
Hale the hawker hauls a sap-blackened staff and has folded his stall at the clearing. Hale says the day's tolls were taken and you may trust that. Hale stands with his back to the road.

## 11
Marrek the roadwarden has shoved a rival against the old gravestone. You are told the rival tried to take the toll for the day. Marrek stands with his back to the road.

## 12
Maren, a scribe, scraped a name from the register. The council ordered it to spare you. Maren still carries the register.

## 13
Merrin the oarsman has drawn a bound courier ashore at the ford and set her down saying the company took the debt. Merrin never asks after what becomes of the people he leaves.

## 14
Corin the miller has shut the seed-barn. He shut it to keep the village seed from plunderers driven by drought and ambition. Corin finds the nails driven from the outside.

## 15
Marta the provisioner brings a list and says the elder sent raiders to plunder the granary. She pins the blame on drought and the elder's sudden ambition. Marta stood beyond the paling and watched the door open.

## 16
Thane the scribe locked the deserter's bundle and swore the papers would not leave the ridgeline. The key is with a man he will not name.

## 17
Bram the tenant farmer came through the gate with a confession and told you he had capped the village well to stop the poachers. The rope Bram said held the cap has been cut off short.

## 18
Bren, oarsman, dug up a village grave and set the coffin before the door to ruin the sale. The buyer waits in his yard.

## 19
Rowan the tracker has pried up a family gravestone and says the tanner stole their last pelt. The tanner's apprentice struck Rowan and turned the accusation into a messy scuffle. Rowan says twice that he owes the tanner nothing.

## 20
Maren the surveyor resets the boundary stone and says the families agreed the swap for the widow. The grass under the stone is still green.

## 21
Marik the tollman shuts his ledger and says the road brought no traders. He says the jars and sacks at his feet are mistakes and that nothing crossed this clearing. He stands where he cannot see the road.

## 22
Bryn the tenant farmer told you he picked the elven cistern lock for his sheep. A man he refuses to name holds the key.

## 23
Garon the ferryman has chained the bridge and says the passing pilgrims bring defilement. He has already had a toll-board painted.

## 24
Rena the weaver says thieves rifled her dowry on the road. She blames Sergeant Marta for arranging the ambush to sell the goods. Rena keeps the chest it came in in her room.

## 25
Rowan the gravedigger dug the pit for the child's baptism the neighbours tolerate. The neighbours told you the baptism could not wait. Rowan's heap of spoil is smaller than the pit.

## 26
Farah the milkmaid has branded the calves. Everyone says she marked them as the company’s due and you are meant to accept that. The marking iron is new.

## 27
Merrin the reeve says a stranger followed a caravan home to spread violet rites. Merrin already knows which lane.

## 28
Edda the innkeeper says the sergeant seized her dowry to pay a gambling debt. She shows you a battered chart of the marks she carved on the chest's lid. Edda's chest sits in her room.

## 29
Marn the shipmaster says he struck Lingcroft's name from the captain's roll to keep them out of the levy. He has the roll folded in his coat where you can see the names.

## 30
Rurik the tollman has shoved the day's takings under a grave slab. Everyone there says he did it to spite a rival who took his dues. He stands where he cannot see the road.

## 31
Garr the shipmaster boasts he has counted his cellar for rats and tells you not to bother. The traps in his cellar are unset.

## 32
Marek the sexton locks the churchyard under candlelight and says the vicar hired him. His man holds the other key.

## 33
Mara the beekeeper asks you to watch the hut roof nightly for her husband's memory. Only one side of the roof bears fresh thatch.

## 34
Garren the beekeeper carries a sealed plea into the elven clearing to fetch an antidote. Garren never asks what happens after they read it.

## 35
Daric the sexton pried a vault open and dragged a corpse out to sell. You have traced the pulley marks on the chapel wall where he worked. Daric's buyer waits in his yard.

## 36
Rilla the sexton has lashed fresh reed to the chapel's southern slope and says the parish is abandoned. The villagers accept that and wash their hands of the place. Rilla left only the south slope bearing fresh thatch.

## 37
Haldan the ferreter carries a lamp toward the barrow to show you he means no trick. The village exiled him for stealing a pommel and he says he walks to return it as penance. He stops at the field gate and waits.

## 38
Marek the scribe forced the ledger chest open to spare a deserter's shame. Marek keeps the key with a man he will not name.

## 39
Garrick the burner hauled winter wood for you and says the godchild owns it. Garrick says which end to start.

## 40
Marek the tenant farmer comes through the gate and tells you he capped the village well to stop poachers. The rope has been cut off short.
