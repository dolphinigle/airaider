# 48 cards

## 1
Merrin the beekeeper stops a rider in his clearing and cannot say what the rider carries, only that it must not arrive. She lashed a spindle to the coffin and sent for you.

## 2
Marrek the charcoal-burner has torn open the woodpile to find a carved carp. The hound wears another man's collar.

## 3
Marrek the charcoal-burner drags a hound from the wood and bolts it to his cart, telling anyone it chased his fires. The hound wears another man's collar.

## 4
Maren the ferryman lashed a lord's letter to the ridgeline pulley and sent it upriver. Maren has been saying he only ferried cargo and the seal on that letter is fresh while the paper is old. Maren leaves the pulley rope knotted around a warden-stone for whoever takes it.

## 5
Mara the beekeeper says the rider bears the cottage charm owed to you. Mara cannot say what the rider carries.

## 6
Mara the shipmaster presses her husband's debt into the company's hands. Mara has already taken his sling from the shed. Will you find him at the river ford?

## 7
Nala the scribe has come to declare the warden-stone boundary settled and blames a deserter. The grass under the warden-stone is still green.

## 8
Mira the washer says the charcoal-burners bought her dowry as payment and then refused to hand it over. Mira says the burners promised to return the chest and then sold whatever they could to close a debt. Nell's rope has been cut off short.

## 9
Lina the beekeeper hid a woman in an overturned ladle and says the patrol will burn kin they find so there is nothing to ask. He already knows which lane the follower uses.

## 10
Bram the charcoal-burner grips a lead and forbids the hound the hunt for you, claiming it answers to him. The neighbor finds the spoil heap beside it smaller than the hole?

## 11
Hale charcoal-burner split the woodpile and told you the burners' law forbids naming the side to start. The hound belongs to someone else.

## 12
Alric the beekeeper tends the lamp at the crossing. The deep wood thing showed itself before any word came. He pays for a month at once and gives no name?

## 13
Marrek the shipmaster boasts he can keep a name off a list. Will a bear pry the chest free?

## 14
Coren the gravedigger hauled a coffin from the family vault and tipped it into the lane saying the buyer fled so the company claim to the coin was void. The buyer waits in his yard now.

## 15
Merrin vintner holds up an old list and says he set every trap in his cellar. He says the drought drove the field rats into his cellar and he brought the list to you himself. Marta notes the wax is newly pressed while the paper itself creaks with age.

## 16
Jory the tenant farmer says he burned the key to stop poachers tracing it and you heard the story at the gate. The chest it came in is still in her room.

## 17
Marrek shipmaster brags his name is a namesake and asks you to keep it off. He stands where he cannot see the road?

## 18
Bren the reeve tells you a stranger followed him home when he opened the gate. He already knows which lane the follower uses.

## 19
Marin the beekeeper finds the lamp guttering before any word of it comes and drives a carved staff into the earth to hold it. They pay for a month up front and give no name. They feel the staff tremble under a fresh weight.

## 20
Garron the ferryman demands you guard the bridge against a hated angel. His own man holds the other key.

## 21
Edda the ditcher has smothered a new pit with turf and sod. She insists the pit is a baptismal hole for a neighbor's child. He has already had a toll-board painted.

## 22
Marta the ferryman hauls a sealed envelope toward the headwater for her lord. She holds the envelope with iron tongs and says the manor ordered you to carry its reply. Marta notes the wax is newly pressed while the paper itself creaks with age.

## 23
Marin the apiarist carried a message along other work and received no answer. The violet ribbon still hangs on the gate.

## 24
Raff the churchwarden brings a plea and says a prowling beast has been carrying off livestock from the road by the churchyard. He has led you along the walls and pointed out how the battlements have a view of the old elf road. She asked you what would happen to the woman.

## 25
Marek the toller has spent the morning quarrelling with a rival over a gravestone. He says the scuffle proved the rival's right to levy on the old elf road. He stands where he cannot see the road?

## 26
Osric the shepherd drove his flock across the churchyard onto the old elf road. He asks that you watch the roof at night. There is new thatch on one side only.

## 27
Edda wellwright capped the old forest spring after the last keeper died. Will you take the lamp from Fen?

## 28
Mayra the tanner had her dowry hauled away by a kinsman who called it restitution. She rolled a millstone up to his gate to shame him. The chest it came in still sits in her room and men will come to prise it.

## 29
Bryn innkeeper brings you a damp list and asks you to count his cellar for rats. Bryn tells the elder a story about plunder and leaves the traps unset. Maren leaves the pulley rope knotted around a warden-stone for whoever takes it.

## 30
Fen, gravedigger, carries a lamp into the old barrow and strikes the pommel. Your own searching turned him up at the field gate and he stops there. Will you take the lamp from Fen?

## 31
Marda the herdswoman branded the calves and says they were owed to the company. She took a plain iron from her pack and heated it in coals. Marda holds the iron that burned the calves and it is newly forged?

## 32
Orrin the sexton posts a watch at the churchyard and tells you thieves forced the crypt. His own man holds the other key.

## 33
Bryn ferryman watches the landing and says the owners swore curses on thieves. The boat has already been unloaded tonight.

## 34
Marin the lampbearer carried a lamp to the barrow to fetch a banished man's bones. Your searching turned him up at the western field gate alone. Marin stops at the field gate and waits?

## 35
Rilan the tollkeeper has spent a day counting tolls beneath a toppled gravestone. He stands where he cannot see the road after nailing the stone over the toll chest following a scuffle with a rival. The elder comes with a brass key to open the cellar.

## 36
Edrin the sexton bolted the candlelit churchyard gate after taking a private proposal. They feel the staff tremble under a fresh weight.

## 37
Rell the sergeant has petitioned to reopen the mine and calls the stoppage cowardice. Rell points at a battered chart and tells you the last crew left nothing. The key is in the belt pouch of a man Jory will not name.

## 38
Maren the sexton has dragged a coffin into the market lane while the buyer waits in his yard. She lashed a spindle to the coffin and sent for you.

## 39
Maris the bailiff ties a violet ribbon and the merchant already knows the lane. A clerk finds the marking iron new and carries it toward the ford.

## 40
Maren the ferryman nails a painted toll-board and claims the bridge needs guarding. They leave the painted board leaning against the gate.

## 41
Harlan sergeant demands the mine be reopened and presses a stained chart into the steward's hand. The last crew's tools are still down there. The key is with a man he will not name and bolt shows pry marks.

## 42
Nell the wellwright caps the village shaft and she says she did it to keep children safe. Marin stops at the field gate and waits?

## 43
Merrin, a tenant farmer, has confessed through the gate that he forced the granary lock. The key is with a man he will not name and bolt shows pry marks.

## 44
Maren the scribe moves the warden-stone inward and writes an oath into her ledger. You find the grass under the stone still green.

## 45
Bram gravedigger dug a pit at the ford for a drowned neighbor and packed its sides with river silt to hide the depth. Bram finds the spoil heap beside it smaller than the hole. Will you lay sod and earth to fill the pit before the rains?

## 46
Marta the herder pressed her niece's silver trinket into a clerk's hand. She branded the calves with a foreign oak mark. Jory finds the anvil beached at low water.

## 47
Marta woodcrafter has ridden to the fort and demanded you find her husband. A shipmaster put her before the company and said she watched a wildcat seize him at the river ford. The grass under the warden-stone is still green.

## 48
Jory ferryman lashed a boat to the landing and kept watch from the winter hut while a logger reports someone unloaded it once tonight. Jory finds the anvil beached at low water.
