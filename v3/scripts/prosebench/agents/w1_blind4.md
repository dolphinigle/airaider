# Card texts to judge

## 1
**Rumour**

The charcoal-burner says, "Stop the rumour that my godchild is damned." He repeats it in full, twice, to be sure you have it right.

## 2
**Debt Read**

The receipt lies on the bench. The miller says, "Read this debt aloud; the town must know who owes." The miller cannot read the receipt himself.

## 3
**Warped Board**

The charburner's hut settles over a warped floorboard beside a cracked millstone. The foreman says, "Find it." He tells you which board to look under.

## 4
**Hollow Oak Hives**

The hives hang from a hollow oak by the ford, combs glisten and bees stream from the knot like a grey river. The apiarist says, "Move them intact." He has bought the hives already.

## 5
**Widow's Paper**

The widow walks down to the ferry bank and places a threadbare shawl on a flat stone with a pale lantern set beside it. The ferryman says, "Steward's seal." He holds the paper for her to mark.

## 6
**Stop Now**

You don't believe you've done anything wrong - just some drunken words, a momentary slip. You're even willing to apologize - but the Roaming Swordsman refuses. His dead sisters never wanted apologies. Tonight, he must kill you.

## 7
**Anvil Hounds**

The dogs sit around an old anvil at Thornhollow's ruined gate and the wanderer stands a pace back. The logger says, "Put them down; they lure men." The dogs come to the wanderer when he calls them.

## 8
**Heroic Act of Dragonslaying**

A heroic and awe-inspiring battle penned for the valorous, apart from Adila and Jabal, only your deeply bonded friend is willing to risk everything and embark on this near-suicidal endeavor beside you.

## 9
**Stall Watch**

The tenant farmer says, "Keep the stall till I return; the widow needs the day's coin." The stall's goods arrive from beyond the trees.

## 10
**Raider's Hideout**

Following Fatuna's directions, you come to a noble winery... a winery that looks very peaceful. Is this the attacker's lair? Perhaps. You look at Fatuna... This is clearly a property dispute between nobles, but in the Sultan's Game, you can do whatever you want.

## 11
**Bookstore Encounter**

If you've been a slave long enough, you know everything is arranged, and the Sultan's eunuchs understand this secret deeply.

## 12
**Ridge Smoke**

The beekeeper says, "It's only my kettle — honey boiling for the market." He smelled of it when he arrived.

## 13
**Drove Past Gate**

The herd moves along the drove road past a gate he owns. The charcoal-burner says, "Do them at night; I can open my gate for you." The gate is chained.

## 14
**Ridge Dam**

The dam on the ridge sags and a rotted beam lies across the spillway. Marin says, "Put men on the crest; stop that spill before Fernlea goes." Hugh answers, "Not the crest." He asks that it be checked from the far bank only.

## 15
**The Hedge**

The hedge shows ragged stubs. The shipmaster says, "My name's on the road." The hedge shows a newer set of stubs above an older set.

## 16
**Saddled Horse**

Marin says, "No rider will use this road." A horse stands saddled in the yard.

## 17
**The Frenzied Highlord**

The mightiest of Star-Souleds has returned to earth, consumed by excitement, joy, and insatiable hunger, devouring mortal dreams without restraint.

## 18
**Missed Market**

A missed market day waits. The smith says, "Keep the furnace burning; do not let work run out." He pays for a week of work with nothing to make.

## 19
**Sealed Bundle**

The apprentice holds a sealed bundle. The beekeeper says, "Burn it before it taints the honey." The frayed string belongs to the apprentice.

## 20
**Thorn and Tower**

From afar, you can see this green, broken tower... But is that a mirage or real landmark?

## 21
**The Box**

The charcoal burner says, "Find the deed; it's proof I own the camp." He describes the box it is in before you ask.

## 22
**Loose Board**

The charcoal burner says, "It is not under our boards." He tells you which board to look under.

## 23
**Testing the Edge of the Blade**

With indomitable dragon-forged spirit, you stand unwavering before the Sultan. This clash transcends good and evil, ruler and subject, all known and unknown fates – it is just two warriors testing each other's blades, even if you might cast your life in vain upon the lapis lazuli.

## 24
**Old Road**

The old elf road runs past the toll gate with a leaning gravestone beside it. The forester says, "If they hold the line, they'll charge our woodcutters." He asks whether the man on the toll gate can see the road.

## 25
**Idle Bellows**

The smith says, "Keep my hands from idleness; I will pay for a week." The charcoal-burner answers, "You buy fire, you feed smoke." He pays for a week of work with nothing to make.

## 26
**Smoked Hives**

The scribe says, "Open the hives. The ledger is inside." Tomas says, "You'll want smoke." He has bought the hives already.

## 27
**Stranger's Shroud**

The body lies on the wagon, a cracked wooden trinket at its throat and a grey shroud folded at its feet. The steward says, "He leaves nothing for kin." He supplies the shroud.

## 28
**Bee-stained Box**

The strongbox rests beneath a bee-stained stall and a carved staff leans against it. The beekeeper says, "Move it while the market runs; otherwise thieves will find it." The request names the market day below the walls as the time to take it.

## 29
**Dragged Fleece**

The wolf drags fleece. The shepherd says, "It ate them." The sheep were killed but not eaten.

## 30
**Broken Slab**

The thief smashes a gravestone. The gravedigger says, "Do not bring him through the village." The thief stands at the village gate with the broken slab.

## 31
**Room for Redemption**

Maggie is very angry about the secret things you and Qais did behind her back, and your hidden attitude!

## 32
**Beekeeper's Shroud**

The beekeeper says, "He earned a decent burial." The gravedigger answers, "Burials cost coin." The beekeeper supplies the shroud.
