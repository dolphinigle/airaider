# Card texts to judge

## 1
**Salted Stranger**

The stranger lies in the gatehouse wrapped in a salt-stained sail. Shipmaster says, "Give him ground." The shroud waits on the bench.

## 2
**Old Cellar**

The cellar sits beneath a merchant's house and a rusted iron hasp hangs on its door. Marta says, "For my name." The lock is new and the door is old.

## 3
**Ridge Smoke**

Smoke from the ridge curls over a black plume above the holly. The sergeant says, "Not mine." He smelled of it when he arrived.

## 4
**Dusk Tally**

The shepherd counts the sheep at dusk and pins the tally to the cracked warden-stone. The shepherd says, "Give the dusk count to me alone." The tally stays on the stone for anyone to read.

## 5
**Hunters' Remnants**

This Freeman Hunting Band operate from a riverside outpost. As you approach, they're unloading weapon crates from moored vessels - steel will decide this day.

## 6
**Sealed Loft**

The loft bolts shut, and a ladder rests against the garden wall. Maris says, "That ledger must come down." Derrin says, "It is rotten." The ladder has been moved away from it.

## 7
**Public Reading**

The ledger lies on the stump and names Tomas's debt with a rusted pommel pressed to the page. The smith says, "This proves him guilty." He cannot read it himself.

## 8
**Hearth Board**

The room hides a splintered board by the hearth. The forester says, "Find what lies beneath it; the ford's water could be ruined." Marta answers, "Burn it." He tells you which board to look under.

## 9
**Returned Hounds**

The dogs circle a gravestone. Gravekeeper Marta says, "They came back from that road and they will not leave people alone." They come to him when he calls them.

## 10
**Churchyard Watch**

The churchyard by the old stone chapel has its low iron gate chained and a length of rope looped through one link, the rope dragging across the packed earth. Vicar says, "My niece will not rest while that grave is left open." The other key sits with his man.

## 11
**Anvil Hounds**

The dogs sit around an old anvil at Thornhollow's ruined gate and the wanderer stands a pace back. The logger says, "Put them down; they lure men." The dogs come to the wanderer when he calls them.

## 12
**Long-Term Research**

After two projects of collaboration, Mahir considers you her best partner. She hopes to form a closer, long-term relationship with you, just like before – you invest, she conducts research, and happiness is that simple.

## 13
**Horse Betting**

Send your rider and steed to compete, place bets. If victorious, you may receive a reward. Failure means losing everything.

## 14
**Check for vulnerabilities**

The issue of cultists taking advantage of conversion to commit tax evasion is a deep-rooted and complicated one. Something must be done, but you cannot do it alone. You summon advisors from different walks of life to chart a path forward.

## 15
**Peatbourne Well**

The well mouth is blocked. A trader says, "My caravan will not drink water that smells of rot." He will not draw from it himself while you work.

## 16
**Bookstore Business**

[The Angler] isn’t the largest bookstore in town, but it’s certainly the most refined and oldest one. If you're willing, you can surely find some good books there.

## 17
**Marked Mare**

The mare lifts its hoof. The merchant says, "Keep the mark hidden; it will spoil the sale." The smith answers, "Show it; buyers pay." A brand sits beneath the new shoe.

## 18
**Disposing of a Corpse**

Jalila accidentally killed a noble while providing service. She hopes that you will use the Bloodshed Card to take responsibility for this accident, or she is but doomed.

## 19
**Violet Bundle**

The bundle lies on the council table with a violet string. The town official says, "Burn it; it came from raiders beyond the trees." The string on it is his own.

## 20
**Wall Measure**

The wall runs along the river bend and holds a spindle jammed into its mortar where a stone has been prised free. The oarsman says, "There is coin." He paces the distance along the wall for you before you agree.

## 21
**Beehive Pit**

The pit at the beekeepers' clearing stands open where rain will run in. Marta the beekeeper says, "If the hole fills the hives will drown." The spoil heap is smaller than the hole.

## 22
**Stranger's Shroud**

The body lies on the wagon, a cracked wooden trinket at its throat and a grey shroud folded at its feet. The steward says, "He leaves nothing for kin." He supplies the shroud.

## 23
**Branded Shoe**

The mare limps on the old elf road, a fresh shoe flashing against the churchyard wall. The shepherd says, "She's ours; nobody marks our stock." A brand sits under the new shoe.

## 24
**Saddled Horse**

Livestock stray onto the old elf road by the churchyard. The priest says, "Keep riders off the road; they steal the herds." Rook replies, "They are already here." A horse stands saddled in the churchyard.

## 25
**Sealed Cellar**

The cellar door shows a fresh hasp hammered over weathered planks. The scribe says, "Seal it." The lock is new and the door is old.

## 26
**Against Nawfal**

You found the hall where Nawfal is hiding, with his followers gathered there. Perhaps this is an opportunity to capture them all, but it will be a tough battle.

## 27
**Lamp Delivery**

The beekeeper bears a dented iron lamp across the mossy glade toward the low barrow. Tomas says, "Set it at his stone; that's how it is meant to be." He stops at the field gate and waits.

## 28
**Barrel Tally**

The stack of barrels sits under the charcoal-burners' lean-to and one end bears a chipped iron spigot. The foreman says, "They were full when I locked them." Two of the barrels are lighter than the rest and he knows which.

## 29
**Hollow Oak Hives**

The hives hang from a hollow oak by the ford, combs glisten and bees stream from the knot like a grey river. The apiarist says, "Move them intact." He has bought the hives already.

## 30
**Debt Read**

The receipt lies on the bench. The miller says, "Read this debt aloud; the town must know who owes." The miller cannot read the receipt himself.

## 31
**Branded Hoof**

A mare at the river ford bears a new iron; the rasp flakes still cling to the pastern. Hobb the ferryman says, "It must cross." A brand under the new shoe marks the hoof.

## 32
**The Most Popular Man**

After showing Nabhani a carnal card, he mysteriously told you to dress up well and meet him at the House of Delights that day.
