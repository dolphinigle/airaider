# Card texts to judge

## 1
**Warden Dogs**

The dogs work the Warden-stones and tear at a bleached fox pelt stuck to a thorn. The hunter says, "They fetch me better coin than pups." They come to him when he calls them.

## 2
**Unopened Bundle**

The tenant farmer is standing on the lane holding an unopened bundle by its twine. "Burn it and bury nothing," says the tenant farmer. The string on the bundle is his own.

## 3
**Split Gravestone**

The gravestone lies split on the old elf road. "He did it for sport," says the miller. He is in the next village and can name who saw him.

## 4
**Straw Crates**

The merchant's crates have been left at the charcoal-burners' camp, lids bowed and stacked beside a rotted pallet. Maela says, "Fair value, please." The crates are packed in straw from another county.

## 5
**Cellar Count**

The cellar keeper is lifting a grubby spindle and pointing at the warped floorboards. An oarsman says, "I do not pay for slack excuses." The traps in it are unset.

## 6
**Toll Ledger**

The tollkeeper at the ridge gate props open his ledger on a splintered plank and counts coins with a thumb stained by ink. Ansel the scribe says, "I must know if the book matches what passed through." He cannot see the road.

## 7
**Peatbourne Well**

The well mouth is blocked. A trader says, "My caravan will not drink water that smells of rot." He will not draw from it himself while you work.

## 8
**Palace Duel**

Yesterday, you presented a Bloodshed Card in court. Today is the day you and your opponent must settle this fight to the death.

## 9
**Barn Door**

The barn door's iron hasp is split and the lower hinge has rotted through. Bailiff Orin says, "Keep it shut; if what is inside comes out there will be no ledger worth keeping." The barn door sits ajar.

## 10
**Fight with the Prisoner**

This is the Prisoner's only chance. Why was he sentenced to death? Honestly, you and he aren't that different.

## 11
**Widower's Care**

The widower is guiding his niece toward the outbuilding, a brass charm clenched in his fist. "They are my burden, not yours," says a widower. The outbuilding holds their beds.

## 12
**Your name**

You have awakened this ancient being from its long slumber, shaped it in form. Now it demands something deeper... something of the soul.

## 13
**Tollhouse Door**

The tollhouse doors bar daylight. "The pulley keeps my gold where I want it," says the miller. He stands where he cannot see the road.

## 14
**Alden's Charge**

Forester Alden speaks for the small cluster of the ill and has a bruised pommel knife left on the table beside his ledger. Forester Alden says, "They are under my roof; I will pay for their care." The outbuilding holds their beds.

## 15
**The Vault**

Nawfal's library is located in a cool, dry cellar directly beneath the residence. He allows you only a brief stay within its depths.

## 16
**Split with**

A Bloodshed Card placed you and Nabhani at opposite ends of life and death. When he drew his sword, his face showed reluctance.

## 17
**Branded Hoof**

A mare at the river ford bears a new iron; the rasp flakes still cling to the pastern. Hobb the ferryman says, "It must cross." A brand under the new shoe marks the hoof.

## 18
**Debt Read**

The debt notice lies on the market table and a candle gutters beside it. "I call what is owed into the square," the bailiff says. He cannot read it himself.

## 19
**One Against Hundred**

The moment the Sultan appears, you regret your decision - he's accompanied by a well-equipped Royal Guard, clearly not just for his protection...

## 20
**Sealed Bundle**

The bundle sits sealed on the bunk. "Burn it before anyone reads it," the sergeant says. The string is his own.

## 21
**The Missing**

It's very late, Maggie hasn't shown up at dinner... this isn't typical. Should you go look for her?

## 22
**The Hound**

The hound is racing down the old elf road toward loose livestock by the churchyard. Reeve Hal says, "Let it not bring down the flocks." The hound belongs elsewhere.

## 23
**Hut Cellar**

The hut's cellar door stands open and a single torch smolders on its sconce. The beekeeper says "The visitors must not find anything I hid." The traps in the cellar are unset.

## 24
**Blackened Comb**

The beekeeper below the walls lifts a lid and shows the blackened comb. "Dead hives make better prices for survivors," he says. The hives are already bought.

## 25
**Raid the Slums.**

You found Aziz's tracks. He has built a so-called fortress in the chaotic and disorderly shantytown in the slums. His former tribe has many strong and vigorous youths... like him, they were captured and taken to the Sultan's country as slaves. And like him, they are full of rebellious spirit, gathered together to sow ruin.

## 26
**Marked Hoof**

A rider fits a fresh shoe to his mare beneath the warden-stone, and a hammer rests on a flat rock. A scribe says, "Bear the fort's mark." A brand sits underfoot.

## 27
**Running Wheel**

The mill wheel in the beekeepers' clearing runs on, splashing a dark smear across the sluice gate. Marketmaster says "The market cannot wait." The miller is not asked.

## 28
**Violet Well**

The town well is staining a tin bucket violet as the bucket hangs from its rope. A town official says, "I will fine houses that keep using it." He will not draw from it himself while you work.

## 29
**Measured Walk**

The shipmaster is counting off the stones along the river wall with a hemp rope in his fist. The shipmaster says, "This cost me more than coin." He paces the wall.

## 30
**Gravestone Boars**

The boar pack is charging down the old elf road, smashing a leaning gravestone and throwing clods of earth into the hollow where names once were. A gamekeeper says, "They shame me before neighbours." The brands lie cut out.

## 31
**Outbuilding Beds**

The town official slams a violet pennant onto the table and shoves a damp ledger across it. "I won't pay," he says. Their beds sit in the outbuilding.

## 32
**Toll Count**

The tollkeeper is ticking beads on a string over a cracked wooden tray. An old tollkeeper says, "My dues are everything." He stands where he cannot see the road.
