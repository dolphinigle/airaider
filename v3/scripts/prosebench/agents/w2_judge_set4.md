# 48 quest cards

## 1
Your concubine Inal has eloped with a slave! If this disgraceful matter spreads, it would truly tarnish your reputation!

## 2
Maren, warden, capped the old well and found the rope cut off short. She pins the warden's receipt to the stone and drags it down the ridge though her hands shake.

## 3
Dolan the tollkeeper has been carving tally marks into a borrowed gravestone. He stands where he cannot see the road. Each time strangers come to count his tolls the old bear smashes the stone.

## 4
Marin the ferryman set a painted toll-board across the bridge and has already had a toll-board painted. If you fail him he will try to hold the crossing and become hated.

## 5
Edda the forester sank a pit for a neighbor's shrine and shoveled earth aside. The spoil heap beside it is smaller than the hole. Will you haul sod from the ford and force it back before the rains?

## 6
Rian the gravedigger drags a family coffin into the buyer's yard to stop the sale. She leaves the coffin across his gate and the buyer waits in his yard.

## 7
Marin the charcoal burner cuts the tether and frees a hound that belongs to someone else. If you do nothing Marin will trail the hound to the smuggler, hands shaking.

## 8
Haw the ferryman nails an anvil to the boat and sits watching the landing. The boat has been unloaded tonight.

## 9
Halla, laundress, claimed she had traded her dowry to the charcoal-burners. She keeps the dowry chest in her room. A charcoal-burner answers any demand for others' goods by beating you.

## 10
Merrin the ferryman watches men strip his cargo from the launch tonight. Merrin will hammer an anvil at the landing and call it repairs.

## 11
Callan gravedigger unpinned a marked plot and the buyer waits in his yard. Callan cannot row and will ferry the coffin himself across the fen.

## 12
Jonas, charcoal-burner, has taken a hound and held it from the hunt. You find the hound wears another man's brand.

## 13
Alden, a warden, takes a well the dead left and drives a wooden cap into its throat. He has the rope cut off short and you find his folded receipt pinned beneath the cap's rim.

## 14
Rell, tollkeeper, wedges a cracked gravestone across the old elf road. He stands where he cannot see the road and hides the toll tokens in his sleeve. Hunters come down from the woods and smash the marker to take his coin.

## 15
The smell of the wound, the sounds within your body, the color of the vomit... all tell you that the footsteps of death are near.

## 16
Following Fatuna's directions, you come to a noble winery... a winery that looks very peaceful. Is this the attacker's lair? Perhaps. You look at Fatuna... This is clearly a property dispute between nobles, but in the Sultan's Game, you can do whatever you want.

## 17
You decide to spare Murtaz. He is nothing more than a man driven mad by the Sultan's cruelties. You will try to dissuade him from speaking so openly of his schemes – it is not wise, and you will have something to satisfy the Vizier.

## 18
Jory the burner trailed a limping hound to the camps and the hound belongs elsewhere. Jory will fetch it himself.

## 19
Mara, a vintner, says she carried her dowry away with the charcoal-burners. The chest it came in is still in her room. When someone comes for this the burners demand the chest from you and beat those who cannot produce it.

## 20
Ilse gravedigger has lifted a child's bones into a spindle of linen and the buyer waits in his yard. She will try to pass the parcel to you despite her hands shaking.

## 21
Bram the ferryman affixed a painted toll-board across the forest bridge. He will sit on the planks and demand coin from every passer.

## 22
The owner of the dark brothel hosted a feast, inviting you and your servant Lumera. You had already decided to make him pay for Lumera's revenge.

## 23
Rook the gravedigger dug a pit to bury what the neighbors dragged from the chapel. Rook lashed planks across the mouth and swore no one would find it. Will you fill the pit before the rains lift those planks, when the spoil heap beside it is smaller than the hole?

## 24
Rowan the shipmaster brings a folded list and keeps it under his coat. He says he will take it into the old woods and burn the page himself though his hands shake.

## 25
Marta the herdswoman marks calves with a spiral to claim them. Neighbours point out the marking iron is new while Marta insists the brands are old. Hunters from the wood arrive with knives and haul branded calves away.

## 26
Yori, the sexton, chased livestock off the road by the churchyard. Yori nailed boards along the eaves and asked you to watch the roof at night. One side of the roof bears new thatch only and someone will test the eaves tonight.

## 27
Habib has made an incredibly delicious Marvelous Mush from kitchen scraps - er, I mean, from leftover ingredients! It's now more popular than the main courses! Um... would you like to send someone to taste it?

## 28
Marta herdswoman has branded the calves with a private ringmark. She has pressed a pawned trinket into a buyer's hand to seal the bargain. The marking iron is new and a steward will come to seize it.

## 29
In a hidden corner of the city, a silent death duel took place. All you know of it is what the survivor later told you.

## 30
Aldren the woodwright digs a pit to baptize the drowned neighbor. The spoil heap beside it is smaller than the hole. Will you fill the pit before the rains?

## 31
A report indicates that noble warriors in the army are treating common soldiers brutally. This is not surprising – after all, soldiers tend to imitate their leaders. The real question is: is this something you need to address personally?

## 32
Bram shepherd drove his flock into the churchyard. He then herded them under the south eave with new thatch only. Anyone who watches the thatch at night finds the eave split and thin-clawed things pouring onto them.

## 33
You hope to convince Sadani to join your regicide plot. It is better to conspire of such things in person. You decide to take the risk and sneak into the palace to meet Sadani.

## 34
Talen the shipmaster tore a name from a ledger to keep it off a list. He carries the ledger on his person.

## 35
Soon, Buthayna discovers the four girls are missing. She confronts you at your room's doorway with several guards, demanding to know what happened.

## 36
Jabal stands irreconcilably against cultists. Out of past friendship, he offers you a fair duel, but it's to the death.

## 37
Edda the beekeeper keeps a lamp burning at the crossing. She pays ahead for the lamp and gives no name. Armed men take a keeper's staff whenever anyone answers that light.

## 38
Marrin wellwright lashed lid to shaft and finds hauling rope cut short. He intends to cap the well himself despite no hauling rope.

## 39
Rowan the herdsman branded the calves with a fresh iron he bought. The marking iron is new. The kin take knives and strip brands from hides when strangers bring claims.

## 40
Bryn smith sets an anvil at the landing and has seen the boat unloaded once. He will keep watch if you do not come though his left hand burns.

## 41
Tobin the lamplighter set a lamp at the crossing. A stranger pays for a month at once and gives no name. The honey-stained staff on the post will call folk tonight.

## 42
Bram the tollkeeper pried an old elven gravestone free. He wedged it across the old road and carved every passer's name. He cannot see the road and a bruised bear comes for the stone.

## 43
Rowan the beekeeper fixes a lamp on an ash staff. He pays for a month at once and gives no name. A keeper who comes for that lamp finds elms snapping its staff.

## 44
This giant comes from deep within the mountains. The masons found him in an abandoned cave... He has suffered through abuse and torment, refuses to or cannot speak the human tongue, and is filled with rage towards everything.

## 45
Lysa, a brewer, has accused the charcoal-burners of stealing her dowry. The chest it came in is still in her room and she has ridden to their camps. Her brother has set a millstone at the fort's gate for you.

## 46
Marin the shipmaster scratched a namesake onto the travellers' list and carries the list with him. He will ride the old elf road to make you erase it.

## 47
Alen, forester, climbs the churchyard wall and presses the ridge with his hand. He reports there is new thatch on one side only. It comes at night to tear what you set.

## 48
Marek the ferryman has set himself at the ford and begun to demand coin for passage. A messenger carried his painted toll-board to the gate and left it nailed to the post.
