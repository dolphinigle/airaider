# Card texts to judge

## 1
**Widow's Proof**

Her husband's stall stands empty below the walls, a cloak on the bench. The trader says, "I need his body and his ring." The banns are already read.

## 2
**Account Ledger**

The ledger lies under a split tar barrel. The miller says, "That ledger proves who pays and who does not." He asks for it unopened, and says so twice.

## 3
**Fair Strongbox**

The strongbox sits on a trestle beside an iron weight. The trader says, "Keep it locked until the fair ends." The trader asks that it be moved during the fair.

## 4
**Spare him**

You decide to spare Murtaz. He is nothing more than a man driven mad by the Sultan's cruelties. You will try to dissuade him from speaking so openly of his schemes – it is not wise, and you will have something to satisfy the Vizier.

## 5
**Gold, Crystal, and Mud**

This ancient residence once belonged to court physicians of the fallen kingdom. Now it stands restored, long since in new hands.

## 6
**Night Drove**

The ferryman says, "Night droves pay my toll." The drove road runs past a gate he owns.

## 7
**Intimidation Example**

Someone has been opposing you in palace, calling you a big pervert. Now show him just how perverted you can be.

## 8
**Project Investment II**

Mahir is again inspired. She touts a new elixir that can grant the body limitless vigor and unleash the deep-seated powers of the soul. It would be a groundbreaking achievement, if it can actually be made. But... the chances sound slim...

## 9
**Tavern Dispute**

You spot the swordsman in the tavern... He spends generously, embracing several prostitutes while buying drinks for everyone - all with the nobleman's coin. This man truly stands apart.

## 10
**Tavern Brawl**

Though the venue is filthy and vulgar, many nobles come to watch the show. If you are madness or foolish enough, you can even participate.

## 11
**The Smith**

The smith lifts a hammer and points at a half-finished horseshoe; the apprentice has had a hand taken by the ford. The shipmaster says, "Keep the smith busy to settle my debt." The bailiff answers, "We will hold the hammer." The shipmaster pays for a week of work with nothing to make.

## 12
**Duel**

To cover Jalila, you claimed the noble's murder at the House of Delights. Naturally, his family comes for revenge.

## 13
**Boundary Stone**

The boundary stone stands on the bank and takes the widow's field. The shipmaster says, "I must have my river back." The widow says, "He lifted the stone." The grass under the boundary stone is still green.

## 14
**Mud-playing Tribe**

Deep in the mountains lives a superstitious tribe who live naked, their bodies smeared with mud, practicing slash-and-burn agriculture for survival. Many claim that they possess some eerie form of inherited black magic, which might be worth investigating.

## 15
**Tarnished Collar**

The cat slips beneath overturned turf beside a tarnished collar; Marta has had her hand taken by the earth. Marta says, "Find my cat and its collar." The foreman answers, "We will follow the collar's trail." The yard has already been dug over.

## 16
**Burial Run**

The corpse lies beneath a travel cloak. The scribe says, "The church must take the corpse." The sexton says, "Not for free." The scribe has already paid the sexton.

## 17
**Learning Sociability**

The youth should make the most of their physical advantages and steal everyone's eyes in the bathhouse.

## 18
**Woodpile**

The woodpile buries the miller's son and takes the boy's jacket. The miller says, "I will pay for the boy's body." The miller says which end to start.

## 19
**Strongbox**

The strongbox sits on the millstone. The miller demands, "Keep the strongbox until the fair." He asks that it be moved during the fair.

## 20
**Frozen Ledger**

The pond takes the ferryman's hand and a mud-caked sling lies on the ice. The shipmaster says, "My ledger names who owes me." The forester says, "That hole keeps its own wonders." The shipmaster asks the thing that floats up.

## 21
**Sealed Bundle**

The rider slams a sealed bundle at the barred doors. The miller says, "Those goods must not cross." The miller names only the bundle's destination and not the contents.

## 22
**Causeway Watch**

The causeway bares an iron post. The beekeeper says, "The carts must not cross at low tide." The beekeeper knows exactly when that is.

## 23
**Staked Ford**

The river ford stands staked and a ferryman lies maimed beside a stake. The ferryman says, "Those stakes ruin the crossing; my boat cannot pass." The reeve says, "They are for the lord's rites." The stakes are already cut and stacked.

## 24
**The Strangers**

The strangers crowd the warden-stones and carry a list. Maris says, "Move the strangers. The company needs the food." The forester answers, "She feeds them." Maris has fed the strangers every night for a week.

## 25
**Tainted Well**

The village well draws up a black bucket; peasant Elen lost her son and his strength. The merchant says, "My caravans need honest water." The merchant will not drink the water in front of you.

## 26
**Widow's Cat**

The widow says, "Find my cat; it has my husband's ring." Her yard has already been dug over.

## 27
**Barrow Lamp**

The lamplighter carries a lamp to the barrow. The warden says, "Place the lamp at the barrow; the dead need their mark." The lamplighter stops at the field gate and waits.

## 28
**Boarded Room**

The town official says, "A room hides a threat." The miller answers, "Not that room." He tells you which board to look under.

## 29
**Bloody Oasis**

You stumbled upon a nomad settlement – a precious oasis, like a pearl in the heart of the desert.

## 30
**Churchyard Key**

The churchyard gate stands ajar and a rusted key lies on the path. The ferryman says, "Keep watch; that key must not leave the churchyard." The ferryman's man has the other key.
