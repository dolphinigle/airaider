# Card texts to judge

## 1
**Chaos King**

The Corrupter sits enthroned, compressing Their hideous form into sacred posture. Those believers you once deceived into serving Them now truly wield authority, attending at Their side...

## 2
**Family Portrait**

You want everyone to know that challenging you, a dignified Sultan Card player, will not end well. May all who scheme against you or employ petty tricks carefully weigh the consequences and understand this simple truth.

## 3
**Performing at the feast**

Whether nobles or commoners, performing at the feast, singing or dancing, receiving praise and rewards is a dignified and honorable thing.

## 4
**Coin Bag**

The captive sits bound on a river stone and his left hand lies in the mud beside a small coin-bag. Marta says, "My coin buys my son back." The priest answers, "Not enough." The coin-bag on his knees contains less than the family already paid.

## 5
**Cellar at Road**

The cellar door hangs under an iron lock by the churchyard. The shepherd says, "Those beasts keep finding shelter there; shut it before the road is ruined." The gravedigger says, "It was leased." The lock gleams and the door rots.

## 6
**Anvil Rumour**

The logger says, "Stop the rumour about the anvil among the huts." He repeats it to you in full, twice, to be sure you have it right.

## 7
**Ferryman's Loft**

The ferryman says, "Clear the loft; strangers keep coming up." The ladder has been moved away from it.

## 8
**Warden Stones**

The miller says, "Drag him to the warden stones for all to see."

## 9
**Fallow Strip**

The fields beyond the warden-stones hold a pale strip of dead earth and a rusted pulley caught on a fencepost. The reeve says, "Bless it and make the seed take." Nothing has grown in the near strip for two seasons.

## 10
**Ridge Smoke**

The beekeeper says, "It's only my kettle — honey boiling for the market." He smelled of it when he arrived.

## 11
**Namesake**

The shipmaster says, "He bears the Ashhollow name; vouch for him." They arrived on the same day

## 12
**Anonymous assailant**

Night falls. Wickedness stirs beneath the shroud of darkness... Shadows close in around you. Beware.

## 13
**Dragged Fleece**

The wolf drags fleece. The shepherd says, "It ate them." The sheep were killed but not eaten.

## 14
**Unholy Birth**

Badriyyah's summoned Cultic God has emerged... but power... They crave more power... Evil creatures unleashed upon the city desperately hunger for blood, for flesh...

## 15
**Missing Pigs**

The scribe says, "Those pigs are my wages." The farmhand answers, "The pigs went to traders." The farmhand has cleaned the sty out ready.

## 16
**The Umbrous Temple**

An evil spirit guards the legendary Shadow Compass... Jabal's ancestral notes records that adventurers can bribe it with a blood sacrifice.

## 17
**Tavern Brawl**

Though the venue is filthy and vulgar, many nobles come to watch the show. If you are madness or foolish enough, you can even participate.

## 18
**Rain Prayer Sacrifice**

The abnormal drought is an omen. God has turned Their back on this land. So, you make a suggestion to the people: pray to the devil instead. Desperation dulls hesitation. They agree to the vile ritual without much thought. They will forsake all else, just so the skies will weep again.

## 19
**Toll Road**

The drover says, "The old road brings grain to market." The drover asks whether the man on the toll gate can see it.

## 20
**Covered Sleeve**

The beekeeper speaks, "Have someone look at the wound on my arm." The mason answers, "You'll pay, then." He keeps his sleeve down while he talks.

## 21
**River Strongbox**

The strongbox lies on Nethergill's riverbank with the baker's ledger clamped to it; it has already taken the baker's wages. The merchant says, "Don't bring it through the fair." The cooper answers, "It travels with a guard." He asks that it be moved during the fair.

## 22
**Boundary Gap**

The boundary wall by the warden-stones holds a square gap and a small pile of stones. Coren says, "Those stones are mine; I need them to pay debts." The steward says, "Not while they're watched." The stones from it are stacked neatly nearby.

## 23
**Laid Twice**

The charcoal-burner says, "Put the hedge so no one crosses our ground." It has been laid twice this year.

## 24
**Mare at Millstone**

The stranger's mare stands in the charcoal-burners' shed with a millstone lying at her hooves. The charcoal-burner speaks, "I need the beast held until its owner pays his debt." The saddlebags are gone from her.

## 25
**Lengthy Inquiry**

Nawfal found the High Constable. It seems he wants to figure what the hell is going on with the pardon you've been given for the crime of murder.

## 26
**Ridge Smoke**

The beekeeper says, "The ridge smoke kills my hives." The woodcutter answers, "He lied." He smelled of smoke when he arrived.

## 27
**Duel**

Jawad awaits you at the arena while the entire Court laughs at both of you.

## 28
**Ridge Dam**

The dam on the ridge sags and a rotted beam lies across the spillway. Marin says, "Put men on the crest; stop that spill before Fernlea goes." Hugh answers, "Not the crest." He asks that it be checked from the far bank only.

## 29
**A Midnight Call**

Deep in the night, your door shakes beneath urgent pounding. "Physician—Lord Samir—he... Lord [player.name], you must help him!"

## 30
**Oarsman's Pack**

The oarsman says, "Put them down; they bite." The smith answers, "They won't come." They come to him when he calls them.

## 31
**Dusk Count**

The beekeeper says, "Give me the dusk count; it's for a debt." He asks the count be given to him alone.

## 32
**Barrel Count**

A barrel bears a sun-brand. The merchant says, "My niece expects that trinket; the tally must be right." Two barrels are lighter than the rest and he knows which.

## 33
**Living Fence**

The forester speaks, "Fix the hedge before spring; the small wood needs its boundary." It has been laid twice this year.
