# Card texts to judge

## 1
**Lion King's Hunting Ground**

On the agreed day, Adila waits at the city gates long before the sun has fully risen. The warrior stands tall, her face set with determination – there is no smile, only the hunger for battle.

## 2
**Namesake**

The shipmaster says, "He bears the Ashhollow name; vouch for him." They arrived on the same day

## 3
**Hidden Scar**

Harlan says, "My godchild's wound must be seen." The forester answers, "Not here." Harlan keeps his sleeve down while he talks.

## 4
**Nailed Shutters**

The shutters on Thornhollow's south guard sit nailed; a pulley hangs from the lintel. The miller says, "Open them and check the storerooms." The shutters are nailed from the inside.

## 5
**The Widow**

The widow says, "Make him sign the names before the priest reads them." He has a paper for her to mark.

## 6
**The Missing**

It's very late, Maggie hasn't shown up at dinner... this isn't typical. Should you go look for her?

## 7
**Barrel Count**

A barrel bears a sun-brand. The merchant says, "My niece expects that trinket; the tally must be right." Two barrels are lighter than the rest and he knows which.

## 8
**Cellar at Road**

The cellar door hangs under an iron lock by the churchyard. The shepherd says, "Those beasts keep finding shelter there; shut it before the road is ruined." The gravedigger says, "It was leased." The lock gleams and the door rots.

## 9
**Ring in Rushes**

The town official says, "Find the ring in the rushes; the violet seal must not be left." Jory answers, "Looks plain to me." He describes the inside of the band.

## 10
**Old Road**

The charcoal-burner says, "Find the old road; my carts keep losing it." The tollman says, "I see the old road." The man on the toll gate can see it.

## 11
**I, Freeloader**

Soon, Buthayna discovers the four girls are missing. She confronts you at your room's doorway with several guards, demanding to know what happened.

## 12
**Market Wagon**

The town official says, "Escort the market wagon to market; the grain must not be taken." The wagoner answers, "We go with men we choose." The market wagon rides high for a full load.

## 13
**Thirst for Blood**

An unseen presence in your dreams craves more blood... endlessly, insatiably, more blood...

## 14
**Quiet Reeve**

The reeve speaks quietly to a carved trinket. The miller says, "Clear the ford by daylight." He asks you to go after dark.

## 15
**Beekeeper's Shroud**

The beekeeper says, "He earned a decent burial." The gravedigger answers, "Burials cost coin." The beekeeper supplies the shroud.

## 16
**Laid Twice**

The charcoal-burner says, "Put the hedge so no one crosses our ground." It has been laid twice this year.

## 17
**Fortune Scattered**

Abdul covets the wealth Fatuna inherited. He sends you to take what was never yours to claim – by whatever filthy means necessary.

## 18
**Duel with the Lion**

This lion has devoured many of the Sultan's ministers... Half of the audience hopes your warrior can kill the lion, while the other half hopes you get eaten by the lion, sooner or later.

## 19
**Even you...**

This morning is like any other – allies gather at your home, awaiting your orders or bringing valuable news and advice. Suddenly, as if on cue, they slowly begin to surround you... before the sharp glints of blade unsheathed.

## 20
**Sacred Blood Theft**

Mahir's experiments and creations require Aether, but the Purist Order believes all Aether in the world is sacred... which of course means they have plenty.

## 21
**Namesake Rumour**

The shipmaster says, "The rumour about the namesake ship must stop." He repeats it to you in full, twice.

## 22
**Dusk Count**

The smith says, "Count them at dusk and tell no one." The count goes to him.

## 23
**Ledger Reading**

The baker says, "Read the debt aloud; it is mine." The reeve answers, "Bring escorts." The baker holds the debt; he cannot read it himself.

## 24
**Burned Glove**

A thief slips from the charcoal-burners' camp and drops a burned glove. The charcoal-burner says, "He stole the cart's iron ring." The charcoal-burner asks that the thief not be brought through the village.

## 25
**Ambush at the Dragon's Lair**

Adila and Jabal have located the precise position of the dragon's lair and plan to learn from the experiences of predecessors by sneaking in early to set traps. Then you realize, perhaps there's an expert in this field around you?

## 26
**Idle Bellows**

The smith says, "Keep my hands from idleness; I will pay for a week." The charcoal-burner answers, "You buy fire, you feed smoke." He pays for a week of work with nothing to make.

## 27
**Dead Man's Ring**

The beekeeper says, "This corpse belongs to no one here." He brings the dead man's ring with him.

## 28
**Bound Songbird**

The tenant farmer says, "The bird is my pledge." The advisor says, "It came back." A thread is still tied to its leg.

## 29
**Against Nawfal**

You found the hall where Nawfal is hiding, with his followers gathered there. Perhaps this is an opportunity to capture them all, but it will be a tough battle.

## 30
**Smoked Hives**

The scribe says, "Open the hives. The ledger is inside." Tomas says, "You'll want smoke." He has bought the hives already.

## 31
**Nightmare's Edge**

Each step toward the Corrupter distorts the world further. Yes, reality was originally written using Their body as source material... Now, They can twist it... Your forward path becomes a maze between reality and illusion, revealing bizarre fangs and tendrils... Hatred, weakness, ignorance, anguish, confusion - all negative emotions in your hearts form this prison.

## 32
**Gravestone Chip**

The woodcutter points at a broken gravestone. The forester says, "That deed proves the lane is ours." He describes the box it is in before you ask.

## 33
**River Strongbox**

The strongbox lies on Nethergill's riverbank with the baker's ledger clamped to it; it has already taken the baker's wages. The merchant says, "Don't bring it through the fair." The cooper answers, "It travels with a guard." He asks that it be moved during the fair.
