# 36 quest cards

## 1
Maris sergeant sealed the mine after the crew tore itself over claim. Maris drew a chart and urged you to reopen it yet tools remain down there. The main shaft needs prying open.

## 2
A shifty merchant seems to be peddling some suspicious stones... The luster, the purity—surely, these are diamonds!

## 3
Quill the beekeeper has run from his hut and blown a horn through the glade. He cannot say what the rider carries, only that it must not arrive. He leaves a rope across the ford.

## 4
Purist followers hunt cultists throughout the city again - their sacred privilege. Whenever they identify violators of the Purist's faith, they may arrest and punish them according to their laws. Badriyyah and her cultists have scurried like rats through countless close escapes. This time, they've tired of hiding and prepare a ritual to teach the priests a lesson...

## 5
Lysa the tenant farmer broke the larder lock to hide a poached stag. The key is with a man she will not name.

## 6
Mara the herdswoman brands the calves and calls them foster-stock. She braids a glass bead from her niece into each halter as a seal. She leaves a new marking iron on the gate.

## 7
You think perhaps it’s best to get rid of the evidence in your hands as soon as possible.

## 8
Bram the shipmaster boasts he smoothed a condemned name off the magistrate's roll to save his namesake. Yet he carries the magistrate's roll folded into his inner coat where nobody may read it. You can lift the folded roll from his coat pocket.

## 9
Joren beekeeper has burned the hut roof to drive watchers into the road. Joren has set the watches and told you the rider must not arrive but he cannot say what the rider carries. A rider still rides for the stone ford.

## 10
A Wolf King has emerged in the prairies, rumored to possess human-like wisdom. Whenever someone tries to hunt the Wolf King, it becomes aware and completely vanishes. If the hunt fails, it’s likely that the hunters will be surrounded by the wolf pack.

## 11
This Amulet of Wind allows you to bind a powerful air spirit that will unleash tempests at your command, earning its freedom in exchange.

## 12
Rothold the beekeeper has fixed a dread-smoke lamp above the crossing. He pays up front and gives no name. Rothold finds the lamp has summoned something that showed itself before any word came.

## 13
Marta, charburner, has her dowry seized by the miller's nephew. She hauls the council and tells you the nephew has taken it. Her sister closes and locks the dowry chest inside her room.

## 14
Brynn the ferryman lashed an anvil to the landing post. Brynn paces the quay with a lantern to watch a boat yet it has been unloaded once tonight. A soaked rope tag marks the path for you.

## 15
Sera the brewer and alewife pawned her dowry to the charcoal burner foreman. She stages a public riot for its return but the dowry chest remains in her room. The foreman's son pours the coins into his palm and you watch.

## 16
Jory the gravedigger has pried Old Mullen's coffin from the chapel plot. He has lashed it to a rotten spindle yet the buyer stands in his yard. Someone must carry the coffin over the hedgerow.

## 17
Bram the tollkeeper pried an old elven gravestone free. He wedged it across the old road and carved every passer's name. He cannot see the road and a bruised bear comes for the stone.

## 18
To cover Jalila, you claimed the noble's murder at the House of Delights. Naturally, his family comes for revenge.

## 19
Lysa, a brewer, has accused the charcoal-burners of stealing her dowry. The chest it came in is still in her room and she has ridden to their camps. Her brother has set a millstone at the fort's gate for you.

## 20
Thane the sergeant shuts the mine after the shaft collapses. Thane nails a chart blaming the last crew and appeals to you. The last crew left their tools still down there.

## 21
The House of Delights has received a new batch of slave girls, and tonight they're holding an auction for their first night. You've naturally obtained Buthayna's invitation in advance, and the disguised Riel arrives at your side as agreed, eyes flashing dangerously: "My people are already positioned nearby. If all else fails, I'll rescue my sisters by force if necessary."

## 22
Marek the ferryman has set himself at the ford and begun to demand coin for passage. A messenger carried his painted toll-board to the gate and left it nailed to the post.

## 23
Marrek the huntsman carries a lamp to the barrow. He claimed banishment freed him but he stops at the field gate and waits. Will you take the lamp from his hands?

## 24
Here, there are no innocents, no mercy or blame. You can complete the Sultan's killing game and everyone will cheer.

## 25
Tomas the sexton bolts the chapel each night after the candlelit service and tells you the doors are safe. He hires his man to walk the stones each dusk but that man keeps the second key. The second key sits in that man's pocket by the gate.

## 26
Joss the beekeeper carried a sealed note through the old clearings. He sheltered a refugee in the apiary and spooned broth with a ladle, yet he does not ask what happens after. The village leaves an answer on the boundary post.

## 27
Marin, charcoal-burner, has chained a hound to his cart. The hound belongs to someone else. Marin has left the hound muzzled where you can take it.

## 28
Tomas the tollkeeper has driven a rough gravestone into the old elf road. He props himself behind it where he cannot see the road. A rival has come to tear the stone aside while you count.

## 29
Tobin sexton has bolted the churchyard gate and set a candlelit lantern on the stile. He hired you to watch and his own man holds the other key.

## 30
Gaven the wellman caps the village well as a duty inherited from the dead. He cuts the draw rope off short after finishing the knot. You find the rope knotted and cut beneath the cap.

## 31
Mahir's experiments and creations require Aether, but the Purist Order believes all Aether in the world is sacred... which of course means they have plenty.

## 32
Your evil deeds have bred hatred and repulsion in the dark, and some conspiracies against you are quietly brewing...

## 33
The abnormal drought is an omen. God has turned Their back on this land. So, you make a suggestion to Fardak: pray to the devil instead. Desperation dulls hesitation. He agrees to the ritual without much thought. If the skies will weep again, if his people will be spared, he will forsake all else.

## 34
Legend tells that blessed, beautiful women can tame even the fiercest beasts. Only the largest and most ferocious creatures are worthy of Lady Jalila's training. Do you count yourself among them?

## 35
Tobin the lamplighter set a lamp at the crossing. A stranger pays for a month at once and gives no name. The honey-stained staff on the post will call folk tonight.

## 36
Jonas, charcoal-burner, has taken a hound and held it from the hunt. You find the hound wears another man's brand.
