# Card texts to judge

## 1
**Traveller**

The traveller says, "See me home to Peatmere; an old ladle marks my cottage." He asks which way you will take him.

## 2
**Confrontation at Court**

You persuade the Sultan. He orders the guards to summon in the troublesome noble who disturbed your home. Allegedly, he is at home holding a large banquet; probably celebrating how he outwitted you.

## 3
**Shipmaster's Sister**

The shipmaster says, "She took my pay and must come home." The sister sent back the money that was sent to her.

## 4
**Search for Tyrian Relic**

The name Tyria is unfamiliar to you, but perhaps not to Bharat, the merchant under your roof, who is always selling exotic goods. If you ask him, you might learn something useful.

## 5
**Stranger's Boots**

The logger says, "Those are his boots; get him back to Hawbarrow." The boots do not fit the man he names.

## 6
**Weighed Sack**

The beekeeper says, "We need that sack weighed." The miller answers, "I'll have it." The scales are his.

## 7
**Cycle of Death**

[xiaochou.name], in its final mercy, lets you choose—who do you want to kill you? And who... most desires your death?

## 8
**The Boy**

The logger says, "Keep the boy from the old shaft." The boy's father is not mentioned once.

## 9
**Spoken Debt**

The beekeeper says, "That debt pays for our hives." The cooper says, "He swore to me at dawn." The debtor holds the paper; he cannot read it.

## 10
**Torn Flock**

The shepherd says, "Find the wolf that killed my sheep." The flock lies with throats torn; the sheep were killed but not eaten.

## 11
**Long-Term Research**

After two projects of collaboration, Mahir considers you her best partner. She hopes to form a closer, long-term relationship with you, just like before – you invest, she conducts research, and happiness is that simple.

## 12
**Burl Thief**

A charcoal-burner sets a burl by the pit. The merchant says, "Bring him to the market magistrate." The merchant forbids the thief to pass through Elmgill.

## 13
**Unsigned Will**

The will rests on a crate; the testator lies broken and his right hand is gone. The shipmaster says, "This debt needs a witness." The bailiff says, "He is not here." The man who is to sign is not at the crate.

## 14
**Your Game**

This is your game; you control the guest list, indulge in pleasures, savor every moment – provided the Sultan isn't present.

## 15
**The Frenzied Highlord**

The mightiest of Star-Souleds has returned to earth, consumed by excitement, joy, and insatiable hunger, devouring mortal dreams without restraint.

## 16
**The Sultan's Summons**

Princess Raedina was to face the Sultan. The city buzzed—everyone knew this might be the last time she was spoken of in public.

## 17
**Laid Hedge**

The scribe says, "The hedge must hide the route from the ridgeline." The deserter says, "They cut it, not we." The hedge bears fresh lays twice this year.

## 18
**Tanner's Mare**

The horse stands at the ford and paws a torn rein. The tanner says, "The horse is my niece's and I need it back." The saddle is already gone, sold separately.

## 19
**Hot Air Balloon Platform**

On the ground, it's merely a simple basket and a shriveled sack. It shows that divinity exists in the flames, and in the skies so high...

## 20
**Tomas Watch**

Maris says, "Keep Tomas from the hunters." The oarsman says, "We will take the road then." She pays more than the work is worth.

## 21
**Black Pond**

The pond holds a sheet of black ice and a split branch rests on it. The charcoal-burner says, "The water must run for the kilns." He asks what floats up.

## 22
**UlLTIMATE Dog-Fighting**

Higher stakes gambling, open only to the most generous, imposing patrons! Each hound's owner has made extensive preparations to secure victory.

## 23
**Flock at Dusk**

The flock grazes beneath the warden-stone; one brass bell lies in the grass. The shepherd says, "I need the tally to settle shares." He asks the count be given to him alone.

## 24
**Overturned Cart**

The harvest cart lies overturned at the ford; it takes the carter's wife's life. The shipmaster pleads, "My debt rides in those crates." The miller snaps, "Let debts swim their own way." The shipmaster has hired three separate parties to guard it.

## 25
**Tied Bird**

The tenant farmer says, "My daughter's bird took a favour; bring it back alive." The advisor says, "Leave its wing untroubled." There is a thread still tied to its leg.

## 26
**Market Cart**

The beekeeper says, "I need the cart whole by market day." The smith says, "Not worn." The axle is cut, not worn through.

## 27
**Please the Sultan**

As expected, the Sultan still suspects you of hiding something in the coin affair... Offer him a little more to sate his greed and cruel pleasures.

## 28
**Weighed Sack**

The beekeeper says, "We must weigh this sack for the dues." The miller answers, "I will take the blame." The scales are his.

## 29
**Rotten Staff**

The pond keeps a rotten staff afloat. The marketman says, "We need it empty for the spring carts." He asks who will be watching while it drains.

## 30
**Fast Crop**

The miller says, "The crop got in fast." Two different factors have receipts for it.

## 31
**Treasure Location**

The map found on Jawad's corpse marks a location, a normal townhouse as mundane as any other in appearance, situated in the Upper City.

## 32
**Garran**

The beekeeper says, "Make Garran explain the fire; the bees are dying." Garran was in the next village that night, and he can name who saw him.

## 33
**Dead Man's Ring**

The beekeeper says, "This corpse belongs to no one here." He brings the dead man's ring with him.
