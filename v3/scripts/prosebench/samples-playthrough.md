Zamolxis, Almon Mosswalker and Alea Dawnsinger arrived at the hollow beneath the old road. A druid ring guarded the pit. Two sentries blocked the plank with a thorn rope.

## P2 · report · saga beat 1 "Hold Highford for the night" (q24, before + after) — original of anchor A1
Auline drew up to Highford before dusk with Sibbe's wrapped loaf still warm in her pack. A lean band camped in the feeder hollow and kept a line of wagons and foot traffic from crossing the ford.
Auline walked the hollow, spoke low and firm to the camp, and drove the roughs off by bluff and a single show of steel. She stayed at the ford through the dark and kept every cart moving until morning. She found a ring of salted pebbles and a line of bare bootprints leading into the pines toward Rimebarrow pass and brought the pebbles back to the fort.
