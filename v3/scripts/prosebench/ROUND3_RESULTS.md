# Prose bench — round 3 results (2026-07-18, r3 on seeds 90501 + 91201, 16 samples, 6 judges + stamp)

r3 = r2 minus transactions-rule, + combinatorial shape atoms (openers 4 × closers 5 × speech 3,
co-prime cycle 60), prop-cap-2, wound-own-sentence, shown-once, anti-receipt speech, finale
person-reacts.

Calibration: holdout 10 ×6; repeats V0-q34 → 3/3 (r1: 3), V3-q75 → 8/8 (r1: 7). Cohorts stable.

| config | median | mean | floor | variety |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| V0 control | 4.5 | 4.5 | 3 | 3 |
| V3 rules-only (r1) | **7** | **6.5** | 5 | 3 |

## What r3 PROVED
- The floor fixes WORK: zero garbled-injury/garbled-staging complaints in 6 judge reports
  (r2 had four 3s from that class; r3's lows are different classes entirely).
- Combinatorial rotation buys variety 3 → 4-5, and the residual "same scene ×4" stamping is
  CHAIN CONTENT (same ground staged across beats — the arc-skeleton problem), not prose.
- Failures and finales write well now (grave rescue 8: "a single leaf caught in his hair";
  the failed-finale exchange "Then keep your proof. The custom was never whole for me.").

## What r3 COST
Per-sample dropped ~1.5 vs V3. Judge-evidenced causes: (a) rule-budget overload — ~8 style
directives vs V3's 4; joint compliance decays (IFScale) and pays in coherence elsewhere;
(b) the speech axis is scene-blind — "no speech" dealt onto a NEGOTIATION produced
"argued a name and a promise" (the pivot summarized from orbit); (c) new leak classes noticed:
approach-contract echo in finales ("the steady escort the contract named"), uncaused loot pastes
(mossgrown chair — the deliveredSummary unlooked-for-find class), outcome-restates-setup on
parley-partials.

## The decision experiment: r4
r4 = V3's lean 4-rule stack + ONLY the proven zero-cost floor fixes (wound-own-sentence,
prop-cap-2, shown-once, whereabouts-own-words, fate-as-event + person-reacts, anti-receipt
speech wording) — ALL rotation axes dropped. Head-to-head vs V3, same seed 90501, same judge
batch (kills batch effects). Ship whichever wins; rotation/variety returns later as INPUT
shaping (archetype-weighted speech axis) under the story-interest phase.
