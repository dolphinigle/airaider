## Why it regressed (5 judges convergent)
1. GARBLED ACTION GRAMMAR at body contact — "a shoulder gash ripped into by the wolf's shoulder
   teeth", wound bound before its cause, a wrist released that was never taken. BOTH partial-
   outcome samples garbled (partials = heaviest per-word load: job + cost + injury). The r2
   stack demands more physical staging than the word budget carries — impossible-brief signature.
2. RECEIPT-DIALOGUE — quota satisfied by lines restating narration to nobody ("Found it").
3. Ledger closes SURVIVE in finales (custody-memo ending despite the fate-as-event line).
4. Outcome RESTATED 2-3× (over-satisfying "result unmistakable").
5. Stamp verdict (2 sequence judges): variety 3 and 4 — **3 rotating molds = 3 stamps**:
   palm-closer 4/8 (the ACT directive's "a hand doing one small thing" made everything end in
   palms), wrap-the-prize closer, "'X,' he said, and he [verb]ed" frame, and the
   one-terse-quote-per-report cadence is itself machine-visible.

## Round-3 design (cut-and-restructure, no net rule adds)
- DROP the on-screen-transactions rule (r2 addition; correlated with staging overload).
- COMBINATORIAL rotation (style atomization): independent co-prime axes — openers ×4,
  closers ×5 (adds OTHER-side and road closers), speech ×3 (none / one line / two-voice
  exchange), cycle 60; adjacent reports never share a shape; the always-one-quote cadence breaks.
- ACT closer de-nouned ("someone doing one last concrete thing" — no "hand").
- PROP CAP: after names at most TWO objects; the rest unnamed.
- WOUND = one plain sentence of its own (who, what struck, where it bit).
- Result shown ONCE, never restated.
- Speech bar: a line DOES something narration cannot (answer/refusal/demand).
- Finale: central person gets one line or visible reaction of their own.
