# GUIDELINE v0 — DRAFT, written from the designer's four samples ONLY
Deliberately written BEFORE the research agents reported, so their findings can either corroborate
or falsify it rather than be woven in retroactively. Every clause below is traceable to a specific
sample; the trace is given. **The designer's four Sultan samples are the ground truth — where any
other source disagrees, the samples win.**

## THE CARD

**C1. Frame: DESIRE, not restitution.** The card puts something within the player's reach and prices
it. It is not a report that somebody else is suffering a loss.
*Trace: 4/4 — an opportunity (1, 3), a wonder to be caught (2), a project to undertake (4). None is a
misfortune with a cost attached. All 18 of my P26 cards are the opposite.*

**C2. One to three sentences, 15–60 words.** Bimodal in practice: either a single sentence of ~15–22
words, or two to three sentences totalling ~54–59.
*Trace: 15, 54, 59, 22 words; 1, 3, 2, 1 sentences.*

**C3. Sentences run LONG and joined — 15 to 30 words each.** Subordination is allowed and expected.
*Trace: averages 15.0, 18.0, 29.5, 22.0 w/sentence; longest sentences 22, 22, 37. My "none past
fifteen words" rule inverts this and is the likeliest cause of the chopped, flat feel.*

**C4. Open on a person acting, a sourced rumour, or a standing offer** — never on an object sitting
in a place. *Trace: "Someone swore…", "A female craftsman … came to your door", "People are still
willing to come…". My P26 opens on scenery 18/18.*

**C5. The withheld thing lives INSIDE a noun phrase, not in a bolted-on last sentence.**
*Trace: "a female craftsman who **calls herself** an inventor"; "**Someone swore** that they had
seen". Doubt is carried by the epithet. My P26 appends a whole extra sentence to do this job.*

**C6. The last sentence is a VECTOR at the player, of one of four kinds:**
a failed precedent / dare (*"Many hunters tried… all returned empty-handed"*), a named prize as
promise (*"an instrument capable of unveiling secrets beyond the starry sky"*), a wry threat
(*"or the Sultan will get bored before you even finish it"*), or an aphorism about the player's own
condition (*"Nobles need not labor to make a living…"*). Never a summary, never a fact.

**C7. Cost, when present, is a loss ALREADY TAKEN, stated concretely** — never "he cannot work while
this lasts". *Trace: absent in all four Sultan samples; present in KoDP as "They've taken two
shipments of goods in the last season" and as the destroyed grain, urns and masks. Pending
cross-check.*

**C8. A card may be pure REQUIREMENTS spoken in voice**, with the stake carried as a joke.
*Trace: sample 4 entire.*

**C9. Name the prize as a thing that EXISTS IN THE WORLD, never as a payout somebody hands over.**
*Trace: the instrument, the black horse. This is the form that should dissolve our old
possession-contradiction class — DESIGNER RULING NEEDED before use.*

## THE RESULT

**R1. Repeat the card's text verbatim, then write on past it.** *Trace: 4/4. Already ruled and
recorded as `docs/PROMPT_RULES.md` §11.*

**R2. Compress the approach in one clause** — "Following the hunter's guidance, you lay in ambush
for several days and finally saw it."

**R3. The card's hook returns as SPEECH at the pivot.** *Trace: the intro's failed precedent becomes
the guide's whisper.*

**R4. The pre-roll ends on a LEAN, never a resolution**: an aphorism trailing off, a tricolon of
craving, or the sent soldier rising by name to commit. *Trace: 3/3 where a roll follows.*

**R5. Outcome text: 19–53 words, 1–5 sentences.** *Trace: 21, 53, 51, 19.*

**R6. The outcome's last line delivers a FEELING or points FORWARD, never a fact.** Tonal, not
formulaic: a win undercut by dread (1), wonder (2), a promise of more (3), owned hyperbole (4).

**R7. The feeling belongs to the SENT SOLDIER, not the reader.** *Sultan's writes the player's
feelings directly; Failbetter forbids it. For us the boss never leaves the fort, so there is no
"you" in the field — the interiority we are entitled to write is the soldier's. Adopt Failbetter's
rule, keep Sultan's device.*

**R8. The person the card kept anonymous is NAMED in the result.** *Trace: "a female craftsman" →
Mahir throughout.*

**R9. No numbers in prose; the grant line carries them.** *Trace: 4/4, and matches our design.*
