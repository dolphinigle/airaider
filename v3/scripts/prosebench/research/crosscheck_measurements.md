## What converges
1. **Card ≈ 20–40 words, 1–3 sentences.** Sultan's designer samples, Sultan's full corpus, and
   Fallen London all land here; Failbetter's published ≤30 rule sits in the middle of it. Only the
   two Western studios (Battle Brothers, Fort of Chains) run long, and they are the least
   well-regarded prose of the set.
2. **Result ≈ 30–55 words.** Sultan's designer samples (19–53), FL shipped (31–33), KoDP (31) all
   agree closely. Failbetter's ≤100 is a CEILING, not a target — their own shipped median is 33.
3. **Success runs slightly longer than failure.** Failbetter states it as a rule ("make success text
   a bit longer or more interesting than failure text"); measured independently in FL (33 vs 31) and
   much more strongly in Battle Brothers (105 vs 75). Three sources, one direction.

## What does NOT converge — and how to settle it
**Sentence length: 8.5 → 29.5 words per sentence across the four corpora.** Fallen London is the
short end (8.5–10.5), Sultan's the long end (15–29.5), KoDP and Battle Brothers in between (15–16.7).
There is no craft consensus here — it is house style.
**Settlement**: the designer's ruling is readability first, and the designer's chosen samples are
Sultan's. Take the OVERLAP: **12–20 words per sentence**, which is inside KoDP and Battle Brothers,
at the low end of Sultan's, and above Fallen London. My P26 rule ("none past about fifteen words")
is the bottom of that band, not the middle — loosen it to a centre rather than a ceiling.

## Consequence for our current numbers
Our P26 cards run **50–75 words in four sentences**. Against the converged band (20–40 words,
1–3 sentences) they are roughly **double the length and double the sentence count**. Our resolution
caps (common 35/75, rare 60/125) sit above the converged result band of 30–55 but within
Failbetter's ceiling — so the CARD is the thing that is badly out, not the result.
