# Kinds of WORK in Sultan's Game (2026-08-28)

~900 of 1,382 rites read in full (name + pitch + every card slot), spread across all id blocks,
cross-checked with keyword sweeps over all 1,382. ~450 rites are not field jobs at all (104 book
rites, ~60 shop/location fixtures, ~90 conversation beats) and are excluded.

## Would not transfer

Sultan-card quota (~117, an overlay not a category) · carnality as work (~40) · court audience and
appeasing the sovereign (~30 — a merc company answers per contract, not to a permanent lord) ·
cabinet governance (13 — you ARE the state) · faith-order chores (~30, membership upkeep) ·
household loop (~80 — no manor, no spouse) · 104 book rites (a stat shop in quest clothing).

## What transfers, and it is NOT the name list

1. **The variation lives in the SLOT SCHEMA, not the title.** Each rite is 2–8 TYPED slots, one
   NAMED ROLE per slot, each keyed to a different stat. An expedition is
   `guide(Survival) + muscle(Physique) + puzzle-solver(Wisdom) + negotiator(Charisma) + funds`;
   a heist is `lookout + guard-eliminator + lock-pick + evidence-cleaner`. Two cards in the SAME
   category feel unrelated because the assignment puzzle differs. **airaider's slots carry a stat
   and a tag bias but no role name** — this is the biggest borrowable idea in the corpus.
2. **Self-vs-delegate is an independent dial.** ~83 rites let you send someone; ~70 demand you
   attend in person. Same job, different scarcity.
3. **Optional slots make a job a bid, not a gate.** ~156 offer a consumable slot; gold slots
   routinely offer 3-or-6, 10-or-20 for a better outcome.
4. **The fork is on DISPOSAL, not setup** — kill or recruit, three buyers for one delivery. The
   verb is fixed; what you do with the result is the choice.
5. **Chains escalate a category rather than inventing a verb** — Field → Preliminary → In-Depth
   Investigation each adds a slot and a stat floor. Arc from escalation, not novelty.
6. **Reactive jobs are the taxonomy inverted** — the board deals a job AT you with defend-shaped
   slots (The Besieged Keep, Their Revenge).
