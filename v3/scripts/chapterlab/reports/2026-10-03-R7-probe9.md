## 8. North-star verdict and the next bigger change

**R7 does not beat R5.** Follow held in both orders is 7–14, M5 and M2 move only on the drifted seat, and M1 is n.s. Not a checkpoint. **Plateau rule: two rounds in a row (R6, R7) have failed to beat the checkpoint.**
- **Save the state.** R7 is uncommitted on top of b884c87, and this round forbids commits, so committing it and tagging `storyteller-r7` as a "not a checkpoint" row is for you/the designer.
- **Then make a bigger change.**

**What plateaued: readability of single screens.**
- Since R5 every screen is followable on its own: M1 strict went 85 → 89 → 93.
- Opus rereads 82–84% of texts every round, whatever changes.
- R6 and R7 each added engine-printed lines: the buttons, card 1's Open question, At stake, Learned/Took/Outcome. Words on screen went up (card 1 97 → 128, reports 140 → 170).
- Every added line is one more place for the screen to disagree with the scene. **Whole-saga follow is now lost between screens**: 17 of R5's 31 follow reads name such a line.

**Next bigger change (presentation only, no mechanics): one author for the saga's spine.**
- **One pass, whole story.** The plan call already sees the whole story. In that single pass it writes every line the engine will print for the reader:
  - each job's road line (job plus hope);
  - each Learned sentence;
  - each Took phrase, naming a real thing;
  - the finale's At stake sentence;
  - one Outcome sentence per ending way.
- **Lines point at the answer.** They are written against the answer, so every printed line points toward it. No line is assembled by the engine from fragments. The engine only checks that each is a complete sentence and swaps in met names safely, then prints.
- **Prose stages, never restates.** Every prose call is dealt the lines that will print on its own screen as fixed content ("on screen: …; your scene makes these true; never restate them").
- **Why this is the right size.** It is law (c), the road outline written from the story's own plan, which was the biggest single followability win (95–0–7), extended from the road to every printed line. It closes the class behind N29, N30, N31 and N33: lines nobody wrote as sentences, and prose written blind to its own ledger.
- **Cost.** It restructures existing plan fields (win, why, learn, gain, lose) into reader sentences rather than adding rules, so the prompt budget holds.
- **Risk.** Law (b) says each labelled field becomes a stock sentence. Measure restatement of the on-screen lines with a mech echo row.
- **Fixes that ride along, not counted as the change:**
  - N31: block the compound replacement at `v4lab.ts:1063`;
  - N32: the landmark is never a dealt place;
  - N30: reject a gain that equals its dealt atom;
  - recalibrate the Sonnet seat (§2.2).
- **Judge it the same way:** R8 against R5, J3 follow held in both orders.
