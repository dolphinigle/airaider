## 8. What is still failing, by class

- **A. The `why` line (C1's own fix) now drives rereads, spoilers, paperwork and broken caps.**
  - The cards paste plan reasoning: "forces him to show his hand", "yields a hidden receipt that narrows…", "gives the company a physical trace".
  - **Rereads:** 41 of 61 (L_full) and 25 of 59 (L_lean) gpt-5 card-reread quotes come from the `why` field.
  - **Spoilers:** `why` is the top source, 16 of 26 and 12 of 23 spoilers. Card 1 aims the job at the culprit and `why` says what it will prove. The showdown `why` lacks "never the answer" (verifier round 3 flagged this). Titles name the answer ("Under-Axle Ambush", "The Bone-bell", "The Loosened Knot").
  - **Paperwork:** "how it gets them closer" pulls toward proof. 34 of 81 (full) and 44 of 81 (lean) episodes name proof, ledger, papers, writ or claim in job or why. Lean's sneak jobs are 19 of 21 paperwork.
  - **Paste:** card paste rate is 60% on card 1 and 67–73% on all cards (base 57.6%, probe1 46%).
  - **Caps:** four parts in 70 words. 47 of 65 card caps of 70 words broke (median +7), and 22–23 of 24 finale caps broke (median +11).
- **B. Engine tags printed as story claims.**
  - The names filter gives a person their static seat `part` whenever `latest` mentions their label. The card then prints "Natalgreesh the lizardfolk merchant stands in the way" on a job she is not in, or after she has sided with the company. This is L_lean's top who_in_way miss.
  - L_full has the same class: "caught between the two sides".
  - Win-kind strings get pasted into reports: "Someone took a side.", "Someone was found."
  - Verifier round 3 flagged both, and both were open at run time.
- **C. The answer is printed, not earned. This is what keeps M3 below base.**
  - C4 works on its own terms: M8 is 100% and every finale printed `truth`.
  - But J2 calls the reveal "tacked on", "pasted" or "one flat line" in most of the 48 notes.
  - Opus J1's worst moment is a pointless middle win in 8 of 24 lean sagas ("the ledger we stole was never used", "the climax hands over an apprentice we already caught").
  - **Design tension for the designer:** the plan rule "the showdown works even if earlier jobs failed" (from §2.4.3, "every plan field valid on every path") makes every middle win optional, so nothing a job wins is used later. Rewording a prompt line will not fix this; it needs a ruling.
- **D. Continuity (M11 2.79 per saga, against 0.5 at G2).**
  - Retries are not marked to the card writer (verifier round 3).
  - Taken things and caught people reappear in enemy hands; people change sides with no reason given (F4_3, F7_1, F7_2).
  - Places drift: the stone and the oak move from town to town.
- **E. Repetition (watch only).** The card-1 skeleton repeats: "Nobody knows" in 20 of 20; "carry spears… and will bar…"; "if nobody acts". All J4 seats call this the main repeat. It is content and dealt-mandate territory, not a gate.
