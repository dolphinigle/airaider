**Don't build R6 as it stands, and don't make it the new checkpoint.** Five of R6's fixes worked, but its reveal change broke the one gate R5 had met: the answer to the saga's question no longer comes through. On the whole-saga follow pair, R6 no longer leads R3.

- **Keep: R3 15, R6 7.** Over all reads it is 16–32, the same as R5.
- The position effect is gone: the saga shown first was picked 24/48 times for keep and 25/48 for follow.

By the North Star rule, a round that wins sub-metrics but loses the whole-saga pair has not moved the goal. R6 did not beat the best-follow checkpoint (`storyteller-best-follow-r5`), at least as measured against R3. No direct R6-vs-R5 pair was run. If R7 also fails to beat R5, the plateau rule applies.
